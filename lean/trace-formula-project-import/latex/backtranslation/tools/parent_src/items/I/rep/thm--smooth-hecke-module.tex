Let $G$ be a locally profinite group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $\mu$ a left Haar measure on $G$, and $\pi$ a
smooth representation of $G$ on $V$. Then $(f,v)\mapsto\pi(f)v$ makes $V$ a non-degenerate $C_c^\infty(G)$-module.
Moreover, for every compact open subgroup $K'\subseteq G$, $\pi(e_{K'})$ is an idempotent linear map of $V$ with image
$V^{K'}$, and $\pi(e_{K'})v=v$ for $v\in V^{K'}$.
