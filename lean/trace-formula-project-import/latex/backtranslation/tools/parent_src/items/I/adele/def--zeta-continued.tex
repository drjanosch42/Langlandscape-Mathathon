Let $\nu$ be a Haar measure on $\A^\times$, $\Phi\colon\A\to\C$ and $\chi$ a Hecke character. Write
$\omega=\omega_\chi$, $\sigma=\sigma(\chi)$, $\theta=\theta_{\Phi,\omega}$, and let $\hat\Phi$ be the Fourier transform of
$\Phi$ with respect to $\dd x$. Put $f_0=\delta(\omega)\Phi(0)$, $g_0=\delta(\omega)\hat\Phi(0)$ and
\[
  \theta^\natural(r)=\begin{cases}\theta(r)-f_0,&r>1,\\ \theta(r)-g_0/r,&0<r<1,\\ 0&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
\]
For $w\in\C$ let
\[
  \Lambda_{\Phi,\omega}(w)=\int_0^\infty\theta^\natural(r)\,r^{w-1}\,\dd r-\frac{f_0}{w}-\frac{g_0}{1-w},
\]
where the integral is the Bochner integral, and $f_0/w$ and $g_0/(1-w)$ are read as $0$ at $w=0$ and $w=1$
respectively. The \emph{completed zeta integral} is
\[
  Z^*(s,\Phi,\chi;\nu)=\kappa(\nu)\,\Lambda_{\Phi,\omega}\bigl(s+\sigma\bigr)\qquad(s\in\C),
\]
with $\kappa(\nu)$ as in \cref{I.adele.lem:idele-haar-decomposition}.
