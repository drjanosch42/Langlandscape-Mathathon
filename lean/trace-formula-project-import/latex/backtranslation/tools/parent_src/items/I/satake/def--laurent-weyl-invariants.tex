Let $\C[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}]$ be the $\C$-algebra of Laurent polynomials in two commuting variables $X,Y$, that is, the
group algebra of $\Z\times\Z$: it has the $\C$-basis of monomials $X^aY^b$ ($a,b\in\Z$), with
$X^aY^b\cdot X^{a'}Y^{b'}=X^{a+a'}Y^{b+b'}$. Let $s$ be the $\C$-algebra automorphism of $\C[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}]$ with
$s(X^aY^b)=X^bY^a$; it satisfies $s\circ s=\mathrm{id}$. The \emph{Weyl group invariants} are the $\C$-subalgebra
\[
  \C[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}]^{S_2}=\{P\in\C[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}]:s(P)=P\}.
\]
