Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$, $B=B(F)$, let $\chi_1,\chi_2$ be quasi-characters of $F^\times$,
and let $\psi\colon B\to\C^\times$ be the character $\psi(n(x)\diag(t_1,t_2))=\chi_1(t_1)\chi_2(t_2)\abs{t_1/t_2}_F^{1/2}$.
Let $K'\subseteq G$ be a compact open subgroup. Then the set $B\backslash G/K'$ of double cosets is finite, the space
$I(\chi_1,\chi_2)^{K'}$ of $K'$-fixed vectors is finite-dimensional, and
\[
  \dim_\C I(\chi_1,\chi_2)^{K'}=\#\bigl\{BgK'\in B\backslash G/K':\ \psi(b)=1\text{ for all }b\in B\cap gK'g^{-1}\bigr\}.
\]
(The condition does not depend on the representative $g$ of the double coset.)
