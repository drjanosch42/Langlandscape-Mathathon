Let $\dd g$ be the standard Haar measure on $G(\A)$ (\cref{I.haar.def:haar-adelic}) and $\dd\dot g$ the measure on
$G(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1$ of \cref{I.quot.def:automorphic-quotient-measure}. Then
\[
  \vol\bigl(G(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1\bigr)=\int_{G(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1}\dd\dot g=\frac{\pi^2}{3}.
\]
