The \emph{standard Haar measure} $\dtimes x$ on $\A^\times$ is the product of $\dtimes x_\infty=\dd x/\abs{x}$ on
$\R^\times$ and the restricted-product measure $\bigotimes'_p\dtimes x_p$ on $\prod'_p(\Qp^\times,\Zp^\times)$
(\cref{X.haar.def:restricted-product-measure}), where $\dtimes x_p$ is the normalized multiplicative Haar measure of $\Qp^\times$, transported
to $\A^\times$ along the identification $\A^\times=\R^\times\times\prod'_p(\Qp^\times,\Zp^\times)$. It is a Haar measure
on $\A^\times$, and $\dtimes x\bigl([1,e)\times\Zhat^\times\bigr)=1$.
