Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field with uniformizer $\unif$, $G=\GL_2(F)$, and let $\chi_1,\chi_2$ be
quasi-characters of $F^\times$ with $\abs{\chi_1(\unif)\chi_2(\unif)^{-1}}<1$. Then $f\mapsto M(w)f$ is $\C$-linear on
$I(\chi_1,\chi_2)$, and
\[
  \bigl(M(w)\,\pi_\chi(h)f\bigr)(g)=(M(w)f)(gh)\qquad(g,h\in G,\ f\in I(\chi_1,\chi_2)),
\]
where $\pi_\chi$ is the right translation representation on $I(\chi_1,\chi_2)$.
