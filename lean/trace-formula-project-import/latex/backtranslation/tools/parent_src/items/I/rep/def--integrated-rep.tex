Let $G$ be a locally compact Hausdorff group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $\mu$ a left Haar measure on $G$, and
$\pi$ a strongly continuous unitary representation of $G$ on $\mathcal H$. For a continuous function $f\colon G\to\C$
with compact support and $v\in\mathcal H$ put
\[
  \pi(f)v=\int_Gf(g)\,\pi(g)v\,\dd\mu(g)\qquad(\text{Bochner integral}).
\]
The integrand is continuous with compact support, hence integrable, and $\pi(f)$ is a bounded linear operator on
$\mathcal H$ with $\lVert\pi(f)\rVert\le\int_G\abs f\,\dd\mu$.
