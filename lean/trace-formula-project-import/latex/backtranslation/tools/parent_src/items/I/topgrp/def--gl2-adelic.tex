The \emph{adelic group} is $G(\A)=\GL_2(\A)$ and the \emph{finite adelic group} is $G(\Af)=\GL_2(\Af)$, each with the
units topology (the topology making $g\mapsto(g,g^{-1})$ a homeomorphism onto its image in $\Mat_2\times\Mat_2$, with the
product topology on $\Mat_2(\A)=\A^{2\times2}$, resp.\ $\Mat_2(\Af)$). For $g\in G(\A)$ and a prime $p$ we write
$g_\infty\in\GL_2(\R)$, $g_f\in G(\Af)$ and $g_p\in\GL_2(\Qp)$ for the images of $g$ under the homomorphisms induced by
the coordinate ring homomorphisms $\A\to\R$, $\A\to\Af$, $\A\to\Qp$ (\cref{I.conv.def:gl2}). We write $G(\Q)\subseteq G(\A)$
for the image of $\GL_2(\Q)$ under the homomorphism induced by the diagonal embedding $\Q\to\A$.
