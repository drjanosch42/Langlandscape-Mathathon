Let $\chi$ be a Hecke character. Its \emph{finite part} is the continuous homomorphism
$\omega_\chi\colon\Zhat^\times\to\C^\times$, $\omega_\chi(u)=\chi((1,u))$, and its \emph{exponent} is the complex number
$\sigma(\chi)$ with $\chi((r,1))=r^{\sigma(\chi)}$ for all $r\in\R_{>0}$. Here $(r,u)\in\A^\times$ is the idele with
component $r$ at $\infty$ and $u_p$ at $p$, as in \cref{I.adele.thm:idele-decomposition}.
