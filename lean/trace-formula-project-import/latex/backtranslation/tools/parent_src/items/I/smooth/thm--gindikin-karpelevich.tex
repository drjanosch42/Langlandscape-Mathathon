Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field with uniformizer $\unif$ and residue cardinality $q$, let $\chi_1,\chi_2$ be
unramified quasi-characters of $F^\times$, and put $\gamma=\chi_1(\unif)\chi_2(\unif)^{-1}$. Suppose $\abs{\gamma}<1$.
Then
\[
  M(w)\,f^\circ_{(\chi_1,\chi_2)}=\frac{1-q^{-1}\gamma}{1-\gamma}\,f^\circ_{(\chi_2,\chi_1)}
  =\frac{L(0,\chi_1\chi_2^{-1})}{L(1,\chi_1\chi_2^{-1})}\,f^\circ_{(\chi_2,\chi_1)},
\]
where $\chi_1\chi_2^{-1}$ is the unramified quasi-character $x\mapsto\chi_1(x)\chi_2(x)^{-1}$.
