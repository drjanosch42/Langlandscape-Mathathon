Let $G$ be a locally profinite group and $\pi$ an admissible representation of $G$ on a complex vector space $V$ that
is irreducible: $V\neq0$, and the only subspaces $W\subseteq V$ with $\pi(g)W\subseteq W$ for all $g\in G$ are $0$ and
$V$. Then every $\C$-linear map $T\colon V\to V$ with $T\pi(g)=\pi(g)T$ for all $g\in G$ is a scalar multiple of the
identity.
