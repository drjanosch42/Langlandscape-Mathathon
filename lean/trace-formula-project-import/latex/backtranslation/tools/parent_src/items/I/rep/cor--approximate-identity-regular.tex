Let $G$ be a locally compact Hausdorff group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and $\mu$ a left Haar measure on $G$, let
$X$, the action and $\nu$ satisfy the hypotheses of \cref{I.rep.thm:right-regular-strongly-continuous}, and let
$(f_n)$ be a Dirac sequence on $G$ with respect to $\mu$. Then $R(f_n)\varphi\to\varphi$ in $\Ltwo(X,\nu)$ for every
$\varphi\in\Ltwo(X,\nu)$.
