Let $p$ be a prime. The topology of $\Qp$ defined by the metric $(x,y)\mapsto\abs{x-y}_p$ is the topology defined by the $p$-adic valuation: for every $x_0\in\Qp$ the sets $\{x:\abs{x-x_0}_p<r\}$, $r>0$, and the sets $\{x:v(x-x_0)<\gamma\}$, $\gamma$ in the value group of the valuation, form bases of neighbourhoods of $x_0$ defining the same topology.
