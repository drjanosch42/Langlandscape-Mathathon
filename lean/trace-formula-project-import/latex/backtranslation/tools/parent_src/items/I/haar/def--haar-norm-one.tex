Let $\mu$ be a left Haar measure on $G(\A)$. The measure $\mu^1$ on $G(\A)^1$ is
\[
  \mu^1(E)=\mu\bigl(\{a(r)g:1\le r<e,\ g\in E\}\bigr)\qquad(E\subseteq G(\A)^1\text{ Borel}),
\]
with $a(r)$ as in \cref{I.topgrp.def:central-split-component}. It is a left Haar measure on $G(\A)^1$, and under the
isomorphism $\AGplus\times G(\A)^1\cong G(\A)$ of \cref{I.topgrp.thm:norm-one-splitting} the measure $\mu$ corresponds
to $\dd r/r\otimes\mu^1$, where $\dd r/r$ is transported to $\AGplus$ by $r\mapsto a(r)$. We write $\dd g^1$ for
$(\dd g)^1$.
