For an integer $N\ge1$ the \emph{congruence subgroups} of $\Kf$ are
\begin{align*}
  K_0(N)&=\{k\in\Kf:k_{21}\in N\Zhat\},\\
  K_1(N)&=\{k\in K_0(N):k_{22}-1\in N\Zhat\},\\
  \Kf(N)&=\{k\in\Kf:k-1\in\Mat_2(N\Zhat)\},
\end{align*}
where the entries of $k\in\Kf$ lie in $\Zhat$. They are subgroups of $\Kf$, and $\Kf(N)\subseteq K_1(N)\subseteq K_0(N)$.
