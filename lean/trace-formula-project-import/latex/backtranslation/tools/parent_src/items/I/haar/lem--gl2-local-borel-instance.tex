Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra. Then the $\sigma$-algebra of $\GL_2(F)$, induced by the inclusion into $\Mat_2(F)$ with the product $\sigma$-algebra, is the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of $\GL_2(F)$. (This is \cref{X.topgrp.thm:gl2-borel-measurable} for $F$, since $F$ is second countable and Hausdorff.)
