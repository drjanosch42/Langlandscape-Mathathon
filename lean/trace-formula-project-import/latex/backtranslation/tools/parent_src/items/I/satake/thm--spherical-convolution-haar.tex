Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$ with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $K=K_F$, and let $\mu$ be a
left Haar measure on $G$. Every $f\in\mathcal H(G,K)$ lies in $C_c^\infty(G)$, and for all
$f_1,f_2\in\mathcal H(G,K)$
\[
  f_1*_\mu f_2=\mu(K)\,(f_1*f_2),\qquad\text{that is,}\qquad
  \int_Gf_1(y)\,f_2(y^{-1}x)\,\dd\mu(y)=\mu(K)\,(f_1*f_2)(x)\quad(x\in G),
\]
where $f_1*f_2$ is the product of $\mathcal H(G,K)$ (a finite sum over $G/K$).
