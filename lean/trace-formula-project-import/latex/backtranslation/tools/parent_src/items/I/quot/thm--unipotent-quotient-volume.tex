The map $n\colon\A\to N(\A)$ is an isomorphism of topological groups, and $N(\Q)=\{n(q):q\in\Q\}$ is a discrete
subgroup of $N(\A)$. The quotient $N(\Q)\backslash N(\A)$ is compact. Let $\lambda$ be a Haar measure on $\A$ and
$\dd n$ the image of $\lambda$ under $n$ (\cref{I.haar.def:borel-coordinate-measures}). Then $\dd n$ is a Haar measure on
$N(\A)$, and the quotient measure of $\dd n$ gives $N(\Q)\backslash N(\A)$ the total mass $\lambda([0,1)\times\Zhat)$.
For $\lambda=\dd x$ this volume is $1$.
