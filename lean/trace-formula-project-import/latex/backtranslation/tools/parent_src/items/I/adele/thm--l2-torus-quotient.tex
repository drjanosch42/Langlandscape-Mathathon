Let $C$ and $\Theta$ be as in \cref{I.adele.thm:l2-norm-one-quotient}, and give $C\times C$ the product of the Haar
probability measures. The functions $(c_1,c_2)\mapsto\mu_1(\Theta c_1)\,\mu_2(\Theta c_2)$, where $\mu=(\mu_1,\mu_2)$
runs over the Dirichlet pairs, form a Hilbert basis of $\Ltwo(C\times C)$.
