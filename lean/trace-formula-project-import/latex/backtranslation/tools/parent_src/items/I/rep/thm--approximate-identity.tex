Let $G$ be a locally compact Hausdorff group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $\mu$ a left Haar measure on $G$, $\pi$ a
strongly continuous unitary representation of $G$ on $\mathcal H$, and $(f_n)$ a Dirac sequence on $G$ with respect to
$\mu$. Then $\pi(f_n)v\to v$ as $n\to\infty$, for every $v\in\mathcal H$ (with $f_n$ regarded as complex-valued).
