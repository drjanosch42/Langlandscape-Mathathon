Let $G(\R)^1=\{g\in\GL_2(\R):\abs{\det g}=1\}$. For $g=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)\in G(\R)^1$ put
$z(g)=(a\varepsilon i+b)/(c\varepsilon i+d)$ with $\varepsilon=\det g\in\{\pm1\}$; then $\operatorname{Im}z(g)>0$. Let $\mathcal D'$ be as in
\cref{I.quot.lem:modular-area}, let $P=\{(c,d)\in\R^2:c>0,\text{ or }c=0\text{ and }d>0\}$, and
\[
  D_\infty=\{g\in G(\R)^1:z(g)\in\mathcal D',\ (g_{21},g_{22})\in P\},\qquad
  D=\{x\in G(\A):x_\infty\in D_\infty,\ x_f\in\Kf\}.
\]
Then $D$ is a Borel subset of $G(\A)^1$, and every $x\in G(\A)^1$ has at least one $\gamma\in G(\Q)$ with $\gamma^{-1}x\in D$. Let $\mu$ be a
left Haar measure on $G(\A)$. The set of $x\in G(\A)^1$ with more than one such $\gamma$ is $\mu^1$-null (\cref{I.haar.def:haar-norm-one}),
and the measure $\mu_X$ on $X=G(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1$ of \cref{I.quot.def:automorphic-quotient-measure} is
\[
  \mu_X(E)=\mu^1\bigl(D\cap p^{-1}(E)\bigr)\qquad(E\subseteq X\text{ Borel}),
\]
where $p\colon G(\A)^1\to X$ is the quotient map.
