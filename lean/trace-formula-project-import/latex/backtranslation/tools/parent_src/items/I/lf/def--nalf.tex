A \emph{non-archimedean local field} is a field $F$ equipped with a topology and with a non-archimedean
valuation $v\colon F\to\Gamma\cup\{0\}$, taken up to equivalence ($\Gamma$ a totally ordered abelian group written
multiplicatively), such that
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the topology of $F$ is the topology defined by $v$: for every $x_0\in F$ the sets
  $\{x\in F:v(x-x_0)<\gamma\}$, $\gamma\in\Gamma$, form a base of neighbourhoods of $x_0$;
\item $v$ is non-trivial: there is $x\in F$ with $v(x)\neq0$ and $v(x)\neq1$;
\item $F$ is locally compact.
\end{enumerate}
Its \emph{ring of integers} is $\Ocal_F=\{x\in F:v(x)\le1\}$, with maximal ideal
$\mathfrak{p}_F=\{x\in F:v(x)<1\}$ and \emph{residue field} $k_F=\Ocal_F/\mathfrak{p}_F$.
