Let $R$ be a commutative topological ring such that $R$ and $R^\times$ (units topology) are locally compact, Hausdorff
and second countable, and let $\abs{\cdot}\colon R^\times\to\R_{>0}$ be a continuous homomorphism. Let $\lambda$ be an
additive Haar measure on $R$ such that, for every $a\in R^\times$, the image of $\lambda$ under $x\mapsto ax$ is
$\abs{a}^{-1}\lambda$, and let $\nu$ be a Haar measure on $R^\times$. Then the image $\mu_B$ of the measure
\[
  \abs{t_1/t_2}^{-1}\,\dd\lambda(x)\,\dd\nu(t_1)\,\dd\nu(t_2)\qquad\text{on }R\times R^\times\times R^\times
\]
under $(x,t_1,t_2)\mapsto n(x)\diag(t_1,t_2)$ is a left Haar measure on $B(R)$ (with the topology induced from
$\GL_2(R)$).
