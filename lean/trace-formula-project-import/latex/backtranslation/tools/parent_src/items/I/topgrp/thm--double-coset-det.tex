Let $K'\subseteq\Kf$ be an open subgroup and $G(\R)^+$ as in \cref{I.topgrp.thm:strong-approximation}. Then
$\det(K')$ is an open subgroup of $\Zhat^\times$, and $g\mapsto u(\det g)\det(K')$ (with $u$ as in
\cref{I.adele.thm:idele-decomposition}) induces a bijection
\[
  G(\Q)\backslash G(\A)/G(\R)^+K'\longrightarrow\Zhat^\times/\det(K').
\]
