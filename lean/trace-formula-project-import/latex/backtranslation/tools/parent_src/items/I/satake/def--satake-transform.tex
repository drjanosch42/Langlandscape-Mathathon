Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field with uniformizer $\unif$ and residue cardinality $q$, $G=\GL_2(F)$ and
$K=K_F$, and let $q^{1/2}>0$ be the positive square root. Every left coset $gK$ ($g\in G$) contains an upper triangular
matrix $\beta$; its diagonal entries are $\beta_{11}=u\unif^i$ and $\beta_{22}=u'\unif^j$ with $u,u'\in\Ocal_F^\times$ and
$i,j\in\Z$, and the pair $(i,j)$ depends only on $gK$. Put
\[
  \mu(gK)=q^{-(i-j)/2}\,X^iY^j=\deltaB(\beta)^{1/2}\,X^iY^j\in\C[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}].
\]
The \emph{Satake transform} of $f\in\mathcal H(G,K)$ is the Laurent polynomial
\[
  \Sat f=\sum_{gK\in G/K}f(g)\,\mu(gK),
\]
a finite sum, since $f$ vanishes outside finitely many left cosets of $K$.
