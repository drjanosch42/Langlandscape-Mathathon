Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$, let $\chi_1,\chi_2$ be quasi-characters of $F^\times$, and let
$(\pi_\chi)_N$ be the action of $T(F)$ on the Jacquet module $I(\chi_1,\chi_2)_N$ of the right translation
representation $\pi_\chi$ on $I(\chi_1,\chi_2)$. Then $\dim_\C I(\chi_1,\chi_2)_N=2$, and there are an injective
$\C$-linear map $\iota\colon\C\to I(\chi_1,\chi_2)_N$ and a surjective $\C$-linear map $p\colon I(\chi_1,\chi_2)_N\to\C$
with $\operatorname{im}\iota=\ker p$ such that for all $t_1,t_2\in F^\times$, $z\in\C$ and $\xi\in I(\chi_1,\chi_2)_N$,
with $t=\diag(t_1,t_2)$,
\[
  (\pi_\chi)_N(t)\,\iota(z)=\iota\bigl(\chi_2(t_1)\,\chi_1(t_2)\,\abs{t_1/t_2}_F^{1/2}\,z\bigr),\qquad
  p\bigl((\pi_\chi)_N(t)\,\xi\bigr)=\chi_1(t_1)\,\chi_2(t_2)\,\abs{t_1/t_2}_F^{1/2}\,p(\xi).
\]
That is, $0\to(w\chi)\deltaB^{1/2}\xrightarrow{\ \iota\ }I(\chi_1,\chi_2)_N\xrightarrow{\ p\ }\chi\deltaB^{1/2}\to0$ is an
exact sequence of representations of $T(F)$.
