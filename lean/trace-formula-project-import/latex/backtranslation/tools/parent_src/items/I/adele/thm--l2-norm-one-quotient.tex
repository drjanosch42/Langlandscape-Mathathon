Let $C=\A^1/\Q^\times$ with its Haar probability measure, and let $\Theta\colon C\to\Zhat^\times$ be the inverse of the
isomorphism of \cref{I.adele.thm:norm-one-quotient}. The functions $\chi\circ\Theta$, where $\chi$ runs over the
continuous homomorphisms $\Zhat^\times\to\C^\times$, form a Hilbert basis (a complete orthonormal family) of $\Ltwo(C)$.
