Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$ and $K=K_F$, and let $\C[T,R^{\pm1}]$ be the $\C$-algebra of
Laurent polynomials in $R$ with coefficients in the polynomial ring $\C[T]$ (it has the $\C$-basis $T^nR^m$,
$n\in\N$, $m\in\Z$). The $\C$-algebra homomorphism
\[
  \Theta\colon\C[T,R^{\pm1}]\to\mathcal H(G,K),\qquad T\mapsto T_\unif,\quad R\mapsto R_\unif,
\]
is well defined and an isomorphism. In particular $\mathcal H(G,K)$ is generated as a $\C$-algebra by $T_\unif$,
$R_\unif$ and $R_\unif^{-1}$, and $T_\unif$ and $R_\unif$ are algebraically independent over $\C$.
