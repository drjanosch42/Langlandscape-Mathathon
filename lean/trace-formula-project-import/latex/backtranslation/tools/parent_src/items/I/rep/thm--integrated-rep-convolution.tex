Let $G$ be a locally compact Hausdorff group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $\mu$ a left Haar measure on $G$, $\pi$ a
strongly continuous unitary representation of $G$ on $\mathcal H$, and $f,g\colon G\to\C$ continuous with compact support.
Then $f*_\mu g$ (\cref{X.haar.def:group-convolution}) is continuous with compact support and
$\pi(f*_\mu g)=\pi(f)\pi(g)$ (\cref{I.rep.def:integrated-rep}). If moreover $G$ is unimodular, then $\pi(f)^*=\pi(f^*)$, where
$f^*(x)=\overline{f(x^{-1})}$.
