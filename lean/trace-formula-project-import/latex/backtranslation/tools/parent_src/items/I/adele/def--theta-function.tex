Let $\Phi\colon\A\to\C$ and let $\omega\colon\Zhat^\times\to\C^\times$ be a continuous homomorphism. Let $\dd u$ be the
Haar probability measure of $\Zhat^\times$. The \emph{theta function} of $(\Phi,\omega)$ is
$\theta_{\Phi,\omega}\colon\R\to\C$,
\[
  \theta_{\Phi,\omega}(r)=\int_{\Zhat^\times}\omega(u)\sum_{q\in\Q}\Phi\bigl(q\cdot(r,u)\bigr)\,\dd u,
\]
where $(r,u)\in\A$ is the adele with component $r$ at $\infty$ and $u_p$ at $p$. We also put $\delta(\omega)=1$ if
$\omega$ is trivial and $\delta(\omega)=0$ otherwise.
