For every prime $p$ let $\mu_p$ be a left Haar measure on $\GL_2(\Qp)$, with $\mu_p(K_p)=1$ for all but finitely many
$p$, and let $\mu_\infty$ be a left Haar measure on $\GL_2(\R)$. The measure $\dd g_f=\bigotimes'_p\mu_p$ on $G(\Af)$ is
the restricted-product measure (\cref{X.haar.def:restricted-product-measure}) on
$\prod'_p(\GL_2(\Qp),K_p)$, transported to $G(\Af)$ along the isomorphism of
\cref{I.topgrp.thm:gl2-adelic-restricted-product}(a). The measure $\dd g=\mu_\infty\otimes\dd g_f$ on $G(\A)$ is the
product measure, transported along $G(\A)\cong\GL_2(\R)\times G(\Af)$. They are left Haar measures, and
$\dd g_f(\Kf)=\prod_p\mu_p(K_p)$. The \emph{standard} choice is $\mu_p=\dd g_p$ for all $p$ and
$\mu_\infty=\dd g_\infty$.
