Let $R$ be a commutative topological ring, $\lambda$ a measure on $R$ and $\nu$ a measure on $R^\times$ (Borel
$\sigma$-algebras). The measure $\dd n$ on $N(R)$ is the image of $\lambda$ under $x\mapsto n(x)$, and the measure
$\dd t$ on $T(R)$ is the image of $\nu\otimes\nu$ under $(t_1,t_2)\mapsto\diag(t_1,t_2)$.
