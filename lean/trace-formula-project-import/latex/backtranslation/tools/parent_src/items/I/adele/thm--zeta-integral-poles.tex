Let $\nu$ be a Haar measure on $\A^\times$, $\Phi\in\Schw(\A)$, and let $\chi$ be a Hecke character with trivial finite
part $\omega_\chi$, i.e.\ $\chi=\abs{\cdot}_\A^{\sigma}$ with $\sigma=\sigma(\chi)$. Let $\hat\Phi$ be the Fourier
transform of $\Phi$ with respect to $\dd x$. Then there is an entire function $E$ such that
\[
  Z^*(s,\Phi,\chi;\nu)=E(s)-\frac{\kappa(\nu)\,\Phi(0)}{s+\sigma}+\frac{\kappa(\nu)\,\hat\Phi(0)}{s+\sigma-1}
  \qquad(s\notin\{-\sigma,1-\sigma\}).
\]
In particular $(s+\sigma)\,Z^*(s,\Phi,\chi;\nu)\to-\kappa(\nu)\Phi(0)$ as $s\to-\sigma$ and
$(s+\sigma-1)\,Z^*(s,\Phi,\chi;\nu)\to\kappa(\nu)\hat\Phi(0)$ as $s\to1-\sigma$ ($s$ tending to the point through
other values), and the only possible poles of $Z^*(\cdot,\Phi,\chi;\nu)$ are simple, at $-\sigma$ and $1-\sigma$.
