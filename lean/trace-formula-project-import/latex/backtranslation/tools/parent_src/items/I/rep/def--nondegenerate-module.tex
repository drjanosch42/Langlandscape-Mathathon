Let $G$ be a locally profinite group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and $\mu$ a left Haar measure on $G$. A
\emph{non-degenerate $C_c^\infty(G)$-module} (with respect to $\mu$) is a complex vector space $V$ together with a
$\C$-bilinear map $C_c^\infty(G)\times V\to V$, $(f,v)\mapsto f\cdot v$, such that
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $(f*_\mu g)\cdot v=f\cdot(g\cdot v)$ for all $f,g\in C_c^\infty(G)$ and $v\in V$;
\item every $v\in V$ is a finite sum $v=\sum_{i=1}^rf_i\cdot v_i$ with $f_i\in C_c^\infty(G)$ and $v_i\in V$.
\end{enumerate}
