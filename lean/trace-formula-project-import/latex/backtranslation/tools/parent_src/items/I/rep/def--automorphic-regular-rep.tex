Let $\mu$ be a left Haar measure on $G(\A)$. The \emph{automorphic regular representation} $R$ of $G(\A)^1$ on
$\Ltwo(G(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1)$ (\cref{I.quot.def:l2-automorphic}) is the right regular representation
(\cref{I.rep.def:right-regular-rep}) for the right action of \cref{I.quot.def:automorphic-right-action} and the measure of
\cref{I.quot.def:automorphic-quotient-measure}: $(R(g)\varphi)(\dot x)=\varphi(\dot x\cdot g)$.
