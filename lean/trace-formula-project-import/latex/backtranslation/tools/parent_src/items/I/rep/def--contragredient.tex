Let $G$ be a topological group and $\pi$ a smooth representation of $G$ on $V$. Let $V^*$ be the space of
$\C$-linear forms on $V$, with the representation $\pi^*(g)\lambda=\lambda\circ\pi(g^{-1})$. The
\emph{contragredient} of $\pi$ is the representation $\tilde\pi(g)=\pi^*(g)|_{\tilde V}$ on the subspace
$\tilde V=\{\lambda\in V^*:\{g\in G:\pi^*(g)\lambda=\lambda\}\text{ is open in }G\}$ of smooth vectors of $V^*$.
