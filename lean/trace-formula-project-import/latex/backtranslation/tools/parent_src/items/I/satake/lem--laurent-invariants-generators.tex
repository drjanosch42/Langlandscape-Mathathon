Let $\C[T,R^{\pm1}]$ be the $\C$-algebra of Laurent polynomials in $R$ with coefficients in the polynomial ring
$\C[T]$. The $\C$-algebra homomorphism
\[
  \varphi\colon\C[T,R^{\pm1}]\to\C[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}],\qquad T\mapsto X+Y,\quad R\mapsto XY,
\]
is well defined and injective, and its image is $\C[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}]^{S_2}$. In particular
$\C[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}]^{S_2}$ is generated as a $\C$-algebra by $X+Y$, $XY$ and $(XY)^{-1}$, and $X+Y$ and $XY$ are
algebraically independent over $\C$.
