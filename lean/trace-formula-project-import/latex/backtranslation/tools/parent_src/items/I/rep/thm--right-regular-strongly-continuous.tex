In the situation of \cref{I.rep.def:right-regular-rep}, assume moreover that $X$ is an $R_1$ space (topologically
distinguishable points have disjoint neighbourhoods; e.g.\ $X$ Hausdorff), that $G$ carries a $\sigma$-algebra
containing the open sets, and that $\nu$ is locally finite (every point has a neighbourhood of finite measure) and
inner regular on sets of finite measure ($\nu(E)=\sup\{\nu(C):C\subseteq E\text{ compact}\}$ for every Borel $E$ with
$\nu(E)<\infty$). Then the right regular representation $R$ on $\Ltwo(X,\nu)$ is strongly continuous.
