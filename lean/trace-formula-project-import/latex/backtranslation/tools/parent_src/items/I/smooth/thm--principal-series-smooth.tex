Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$, $K=K_F$, and let $\chi_1,\chi_2$ be quasi-characters of
$F^\times$. For $h\in G$ and $f\in I(\chi_1,\chi_2)$ the right translate $(\pi_\chi(h)f)(g)=f(gh)$ lies in
$I(\chi_1,\chi_2)$, and $\pi_\chi$ is a representation of $G$ on $I(\chi_1,\chi_2)$. This representation is smooth, and
the restriction map $f\mapsto f|_K$ is injective on $I(\chi_1,\chi_2)$.
