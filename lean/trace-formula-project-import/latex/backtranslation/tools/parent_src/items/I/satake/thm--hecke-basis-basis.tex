Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$ and $K=K_F$. The functions $c_{a,b}$ with $a,b\in\Z$ and
$a\ge b$ form a basis of the complex vector space $\mathcal H(G,K)$. More precisely, for every $f\in\mathcal H(G,K)$
there are only finitely many pairs $a\ge b$ with $f(t_{a,b})\neq0$, and
\[
  f=\sum_{a\ge b}f(t_{a,b})\,c_{a,b}.
\]
