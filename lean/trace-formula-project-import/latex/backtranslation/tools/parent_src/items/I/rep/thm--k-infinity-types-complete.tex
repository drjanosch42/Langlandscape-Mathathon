Let $G$ be a topological group, $\iota\colon\Kinf\to G$ a continuous group homomorphism and $\pi$ a strongly continuous
unitary representation of $G$ on $\mathcal H$. For $n\in\Z$ and $v\in\mathcal H$ put
\[
  P_nv=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}e^{-in\theta}\,\pi(\iota(k_\theta))v\,\dd\theta
\]
($k_\theta$ as in \cref{I.conv.def:rotation}). If $P_nv=0$ for every $n\in\Z$, then $v=0$.
