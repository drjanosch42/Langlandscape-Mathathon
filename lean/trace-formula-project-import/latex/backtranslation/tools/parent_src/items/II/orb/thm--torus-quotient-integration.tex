Let $a,b\in\Q^\times$ with $a\neq b$, $\gamma=\diag(a,b)$ and $T^1=G_\gamma(\A)^1=T(\A)\cap G(\A)^1$ (\cref{II.orb.thm:hyperbolic-centralizer}; the group does not depend on $(a,b)$), a closed unimodular subgroup of $G(\A)^1$, and $T^1\backslash G(\A)^1$ the space of
right cosets. Let $\mu$ be a left Haar measure on $G(\A)$, $\nu_T$ a left Haar measure on $T^1$, $\nu$ a Weil measure for $(\mu^1,\nu_T)$ on
$T^1\backslash G(\A)^1$ (\texttt{X.quot.def:weil-measure}), $\lambda$ a Haar measure on $\A$ and $\dd k$ the Haar probability measure on $K$
(\texttt{I.topgrp.def:max-compact-adelic}). Then there is a constant $c>0$, depending on $\mu$, $\nu_T$ and $\lambda$ only, such that for every Borel
function $\varphi\colon T^1\backslash G(\A)^1\to[0,\infty]$
\[
  \int_{T^1\backslash G(\A)^1}\varphi\,\dd\nu=c\int_K\int_\A\varphi\bigl(T^1n(u)k\bigr)\,\dd\lambda(u)\,\dd k,
\]
and for every $\nu$-integrable $\varphi\colon T^1\backslash G(\A)^1\to\C$ the function $(u,k)\mapsto\varphi(T^1n(u)k)$ is $\lambda\otimes\dd k$-integrable and the same
formula holds. Here $n(u)=\begin{psmallmatrix}1&u\\0&1\end{psmallmatrix}$ (\texttt{I.conv.def:unipotent}); $n(u)k\in G(\A)^1$.
