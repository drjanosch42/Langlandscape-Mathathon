Let $\gamma\in\GL_2(\Q)$ be elliptic (\cref{II.orb.def:elliptic}), and let $\mu_\infty$, $\dd g_p$, $\mu$, $\nu_{\gamma,\infty}$, $\nu_{\gamma,p}$, $\nu_\infty$, $\nu_p$, $\nu_\gamma$, $\nu^1$, $F_\infty$, $F_p$ and $F$ be
as in \cref{II.orb.thm:orbital-factorization}, with $G_\gamma(F)=F[\gamma]^\times$ (\cref{II.orb.thm:centralizer-explicit}) in place of the diagonal torus. Then
$x\mapsto F_v(x^{-1}\gamma x)$ is $\nu_v$-integrable for every place $v$, $O_{\gamma,p}(F_p;\nu_p)=1$ for almost all $p$, and
\[
  O_\gamma\bigl(F|_{G(\A)^1};\nu^1\bigr)=O_{\gamma,\infty}(F_\infty;\nu_\infty)\prod_pO_{\gamma,p}(F_p;\nu_p),
\]
a finite product.
