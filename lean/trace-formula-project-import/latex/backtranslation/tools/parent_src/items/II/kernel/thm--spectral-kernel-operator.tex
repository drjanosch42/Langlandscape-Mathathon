Assume H3 (\cref{I.spec.hyp:langlands-l2}) and H2a (\cref{I.eis.hyp:eis-continuation}) for every Dirichlet pair $\mu$, every $n\in\Z$ and every $N\ge1$. Let
$X$, $R$, $S$, $\nu$ and $J$ be as in \cref{I.spec.hyp:langlands-l2} (for the standard measure $\dd g$ of \cref{I.haar.def:haar-adelic}). For an orbit $o$
(\cref{II.kernel.def:cuspidal-datum}) let $S_o$ be the set of $F\in S$ such that for $\nu$-almost every $t$, $F(t)_\eta=0$ for every Dirichlet pair
$\eta\notin o$, and let $P_o$ be the orthogonal projection of $\Ltwo(X)$ onto the closure of the linear span of
\[
  J(S_o)\cup\bigl\{[\eta_\nu]:\nu\colon\Zhat^\times\to\C^\times\text{ continuous},\ (\nu,\nu)\in o\bigr\}
\]
($\eta_\nu$ as in \cref{I.spec.def:residual-spectrum}). Let $f\in\Hecke\cap\HeckeTwo$ (\cref{I.hecke.def:test-function,I.hecke.def:convolution-span}, for $\dd g$),
$(N,F)$ and the bases $B_\mu$ as in \cref{I.spec.def:eisenstein-kernel}, and $K_{o,f}$ the spectral kernel of \cref{II.kernel.def:spectral-kernel}. Then for every
$\psi\in\Ltwo(X)$ and every $x\in G(\A)^1$ the function $\dot y\mapsto K_{o,f}(x,y)\,\psi(\dot y)$ is integrable on $X$, and
\[
  \bigl(R(f)P_o\psi\bigr)(\dot x)=\int_XK_{o,f}(x,y)\,\psi(\dot y)\,\dd\dot y\qquad\text{for almost every }\dot x\in X
\]
(\cref{I.rep.def:automorphic-test-function-action}).
