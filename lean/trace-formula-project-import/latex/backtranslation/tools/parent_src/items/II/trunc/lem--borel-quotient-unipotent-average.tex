Let $\mu$ be a left Haar measure on $G(\A)$, $\dd y$ the quotient measure of $\mu^1$ on $B(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1$ (\texttt{X.quot.def:quotient-measure}), and
$D=[0,1)\times\Zhat\subseteq\A$ with the measure $\dd u$ of \texttt{I.adele.def:adele-haar}. Let $F\colon G(\A)^1\to[0,\infty]$ be Borel and left $B(\Q)$-invariant. Then
\[
  \int_{B(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1}\int_DF(n(u)y)\,\dd u\,\dd y=\int_{B(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1}F(y)\,\dd y ,
\]
where the outer integrands are evaluated at a fixed lift $y\in G(\A)^1$ of each point of $B(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1$.
