Let $f\colon G(\A)^1\to\C$, let $\nu\colon\Zhat^\times\to\C^\times$ be a continuous homomorphism with residual character $\eta_\nu$ (\texttt{I.spec.def:residual-character}), and put for $x,y\in G(\A)^1$
\[
  K_{\mathrm{res},\nu,f}(x,y)=\operatorname{vol}(X)^{-1}\,\hat f(\nu)\,\eta_\nu(x)\,\overline{\eta_\nu(y)},
\]
where $\hat f(\nu)$ is the Fourier coefficient of $f$ at $\nu$ (\texttt{I.spec.def:residual-fourier}) and $\operatorname{vol}(X)$ is the volume of $X=G(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1$ for the standard measure. This is the $\nu$-term of the residual kernel (\texttt{I.spec.def:residual-kernel}); the residual part of $K_{o,f}$ in \cref{II.kernel.def:spectral-kernel} is the sum of these terms over the $\nu$ with $(\nu,\nu)\in o$.
