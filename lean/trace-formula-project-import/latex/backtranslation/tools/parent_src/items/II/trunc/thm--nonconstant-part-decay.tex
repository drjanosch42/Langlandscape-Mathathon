Let $\varphi\colon G(\A)^1\to\C$ be smooth (\texttt{I.autf.def:smooth-norm-one}), left $G(\Q)$-invariant and of uniform moderate growth
(\texttt{I.autf.def:moderate-growth}), with constant term $\varphi_B$ (\texttt{I.cusp.def:constant-term}). Then for every $m\in\N$ there is $C\in\R$ with
\[
  \abs{\varphi(nak)-\varphi_B(nak)}\le C\,(a_1/a_2)^{-m}
\]
for all $n\in N(\A)$, $k\in K$ and $a\in T(\A)$ with $a_p=1$ for every prime $p$ and $a_\infty=\diag(a_1,a_2)$, where $a_1,a_2>0$, $a_1a_2=1$ and $a_1/a_2>1$.
