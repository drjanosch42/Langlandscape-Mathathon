Let $T\in\aB$ with $\alpha(T)\ge0$, and let $\varphi\colon G(\A)^1\to\C$ be Borel, left $G(\Q)$-invariant and bounded on every compact subset
of $G(\A)^1$. If $x\in G(\A)^1$ satisfies $\alpha(\HB(x))>\alpha(T)$, then the constant term (\texttt{I.cusp.def:constant-term}) of the truncation
$\Lambda^T\varphi$ (\cref{II.trunc.def:truncation}) vanishes at $x$:
\[
  (\Lambda^T\varphi)_B(x)=0 .
\]
