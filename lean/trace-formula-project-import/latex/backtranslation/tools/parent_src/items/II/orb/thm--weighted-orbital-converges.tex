Let $a,b\in\Q^\times$ with $a\neq b$, $\gamma=\diag(a,b)$, $T\in\aB$ and $f\colon G(\A)^1\to\C$ continuous with compact support. Let $\mu$ be a left Haar
measure on $G(\A)$, $\nu_\gamma$ a left Haar measure on $G_\gamma(\A)^1$ (\cref{II.orb.def:centralizer-norm-one}) and $\nu$ a Weil measure for
$(\mu^1,\nu_\gamma)$ on $G_\gamma(\A)^1\backslash G(\A)^1$ (\texttt{X.quot.def:weil-measure}). Then $x\mapsto f(x^{-1}\gamma x)\,v(x,T)$
(\cref{II.par.def:weight}) is $\nu$-integrable on $G_\gamma(\A)^1\backslash G(\A)^1$; that is, the weighted orbital integral $J_T(\gamma,f;\nu)$
(\cref{II.orb.def:weighted-orbital-integral}) converges absolutely.
