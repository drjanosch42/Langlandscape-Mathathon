Let $\gamma\in\GL_2(\Q)$ be elliptic and let $\nu$ be a left Haar measure on $G_\gamma(\A)^1$. Let $\nu_\gamma$ be the quotient
measure of $\nu$ on $G_\gamma(\Q)\backslash G_\gamma(\A)^1$ (\texttt{X.quot.def:quotient-measure}, with the discrete subgroup
$G_\gamma(\Q)$ of \cref{II.orb.thm:centralizer-rat-points}). Then
\[
  0<\nu_\gamma\bigl(G_\gamma(\Q)\backslash G_\gamma(\A)^1\bigr)<\infty.
\]
