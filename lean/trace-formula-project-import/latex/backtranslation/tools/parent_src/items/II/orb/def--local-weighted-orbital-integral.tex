Let $F$ be $\R$ or $\Qp$, $\gamma\in\GL_2(\Q)$, $\nu$ a measure on $G_\gamma(F)\backslash\GL_2(F)$, $f\colon\GL_2(F)\to\C$ and $w\colon\GL_2(F)\to\R$ (in practice
$w=v_p$ or $w=v_\infty$, \cref{II.orb.def:local-weight,II.orb.def:local-weight-real}). The \emph{local weighted orbital integral} is
\[
  J_{\gamma,F}(f,w;\nu)=\int_{G_\gamma(F)\backslash\GL_2(F)}f(x^{-1}\gamma x)\,w(x)\,\dd\nu(x),
\]
the integrand evaluated at a representative $x$ of the coset (a Bochner integral, $0$ if the integrand is not $\nu$-integrable). For
$\gamma=\diag(a,b)$, $a\neq b$, and $w=v_p$ or $v_\infty$ the integrand depends only on the coset. We write $J_{\gamma,p}(f;\nu)=J_{\gamma,\Qp}(f,v_p;\nu)$ and
$J_{\gamma,\infty}(f;\nu)=J_{\gamma,\R}(f,v_\infty;\nu)$.
