Let $\mu$ be a left Haar measure on $G(\A)$, $\dd\dot x$ the measure on $X=G(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1$ of \texttt{I.quot.def:automorphic-quotient-measure}, $p\colon G(\A)^1\to X$ the
quotient map, and $T\in\aB$ with $\alpha(T)\ge0$. Let $\varphi\colon G(\A)^1\to\C$ be Borel, left $G(\Q)$-invariant and bounded on every compact subset of $G(\A)^1$, and let
$\psi\colon G(\A)^1\to\C$ be continuous and left $G(\Q)$-invariant, with compact support in $X$: there is a compact set $C\subseteq X$ with $\psi(x)=0$ whenever $p(x)\notin C$.
Then the functions $\Lambda^T\varphi\cdot\bar\psi$ and $\varphi\cdot\overline{\Lambda^T\psi}$ (\cref{II.trunc.def:truncation}) are left $G(\Q)$-invariant, the functions they
induce on $X$ are $\dd\dot x$-integrable, and
\[
  \int_X\Lambda^T\varphi(x)\,\overline{\psi(x)}\,\dd\dot x=\int_X\varphi(x)\,\overline{\Lambda^T\psi(x)}\,\dd\dot x .
\]
