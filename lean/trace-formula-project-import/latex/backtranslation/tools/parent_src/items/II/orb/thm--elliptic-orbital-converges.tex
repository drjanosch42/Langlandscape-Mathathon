Let $\gamma\in\GL_2(\Q)$ be elliptic (\cref{II.orb.def:elliptic}) and $f\colon G(\A)^1\to\C$ continuous with compact support. Let $\mu$ be a left Haar
measure on $G(\A)$, $\nu_\gamma$ a left Haar measure on $G_\gamma(\A)^1$ (\cref{II.orb.def:centralizer-norm-one}) and $\nu$ a Weil measure for
$(\mu^1,\nu_\gamma)$ on $G_\gamma(\A)^1\backslash G(\A)^1$ (\texttt{X.quot.def:weil-measure}). Then $x\mapsto f(x^{-1}\gamma x)$ is $\nu$-integrable on
$G_\gamma(\A)^1\backslash G(\A)^1$; that is, the orbital integral $O_\gamma(f;\nu)$ (\cref{II.orb.def:orbital-integral}) converges absolutely.
