Let $f\colon G(\A)^1\to\C$ and let $\dd u$ be a Haar measure on $\A$, and let $K_B$ be the constant-term kernel of $f$ formed with $\dd u$
(\cref{II.kernel.def:constant-term-kernel}). For $u=(u_1,u_2)\in\Zhat^\times\times\Zhat^\times$ let $m(u)\in G(\A)^1$ be the element with component $1$ at
$\infty$ and finite component $\diag(u_1,u_2)\in\Kf$. For a Dirichlet pair $\mu$ put $\mu(u)=\mu_1(u_1)\,\mu_2(u_2)$. The \emph{constant-term kernels of the cuspidal
data} are the functions $K_{B,\chi,f}$, $\chi\in\mathfrak X$ (\cref{II.kernel.def:cuspidal-datum}), on $G(\A)^1\times G(\A)^1$: $K_{B,\mathrm{cusp},f}=0$, and for an
orbit $o$
\[
  K_{B,o,f}(x,y)=\sum_{\mu\in o}\int_{\Zhat^\times\times\Zhat^\times}K_B\bigl(x,m(u)\,y\bigr)\,\mu(u)\,\dd u ,
\]
where $\dd u$ in the integral is the product of the Haar probability measures on the compact group $\Zhat^\times$, the sum is over the elements of the set $o$, and
the integral is a Bochner integral (by convention $0$ if the integrand is not integrable).
