Let $R$ be a commutative $\Q$-algebra and $\iota_R\colon\GL_2(\Q)\to\GL_2(R)$ the group homomorphism induced by the
structure map $\Q\to R$ (\texttt{I.conv.def:gl2}). For $\gamma\in\GL_2(\Q)$ the \emph{centralizer} of $\gamma$ in $\GL_2(R)$ is the
subgroup
\[
  G_\gamma(R)=\{g\in\GL_2(R):g\,\iota_R(\gamma)=\iota_R(\gamma)\,g\}.
\]
For $R=\Q$, $G_\gamma(\Q)$ is the centralizer of $\gamma$ in $\GL_2(\Q)$. For $R=\A$, $\iota_\A(\gamma)$ is $\gamma$ regarded as an
element of $G(\A)$ (\texttt{I.topgrp.def:gl2-adelic}), and $G_\gamma(\A)\subseteq G(\A)$.
