Let $K$ be a field, $\gamma\in\GL_2(K)$ elliptic, and $E=K[\gamma]=\{a\cdot1+b\gamma:a,b\in K\}\subseteq\Mat_2(K)$. Then $E$ is a
subring of $\Mat_2(K)$ which is a field, $1$ and $\gamma$ are linearly independent over $K$ (so $\dim_KE=2$), and the
homomorphism of $K$-algebras $K[X]\to E$, $X\mapsto\gamma$, induces an isomorphism $K[X]/(P_\gamma)\cong E$. Every non-zero
element of $E$ is invertible in $\Mat_2(K)$, with inverse in $E$.
