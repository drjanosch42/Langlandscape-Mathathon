Let $T\in\aB$ with $\alpha(T)\ge0$, and let $\mathbf 1\colon G(\A)^1\to\C$ be the constant function $1$. Then its truncation
(\cref{II.trunc.def:truncation}) is the characteristic function of the compact part (\cref{II.trunc.def:compact-part}):
\[
  \Lambda^T\mathbf 1(x)=F^G(x,T)\qquad\text{for all }x\in G(\A)^1 .
\]
