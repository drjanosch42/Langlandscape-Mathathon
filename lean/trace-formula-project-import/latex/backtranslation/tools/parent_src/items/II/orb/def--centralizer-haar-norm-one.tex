Let $\gamma\in\GL_2(\Q)$ and let $\nu$ be a left Haar measure on $G_\gamma(\A)$ (\cref{II.orb.def:centralizer}). The measure $\nu^1$ on
$G_\gamma(\A)^1$ (\cref{II.orb.def:centralizer-norm-one}) is
\[
  \nu^1(E)=\nu\bigl(\{a(r)g:1\le r<e,\ g\in E\}\bigr)\qquad(E\subseteq G_\gamma(\A)^1\text{ Borel}),
\]
with $a(r)$ as in \texttt{I.topgrp.def:central-split-component}. It is a left Haar measure on $G_\gamma(\A)^1$, and under the isomorphism
$\AGplus\times G_\gamma(\A)^1\cong G_\gamma(\A)$ of \cref{II.orb.thm:centralizer-norm-one-splitting} the measure $\nu$ corresponds to $\dd r/r\otimes\nu^1$,
where $\dd r/r$ is transported to $\AGplus$ by $r\mapsto a(r)$.
