Let $N\ge1$ and let $F\colon\Zhat^\times\times\Zhat^\times\to\C$ be continuous and invariant under translation by $\ker(\mathrm{red}_N)\times\ker(\mathrm{red}_N)$. Then
\[
  F(1)=\sum_{\mu}\int_{\Zhat^\times\times\Zhat^\times}F(u)\,\mu_1(u_1)\mu_2(u_2)\,\dd u,
\]
the sum over the Dirichlet pairs $\mu=(\mu_1,\mu_2)$ with $\operatorname{cond}\mu_1\mid N$ and $\operatorname{cond}\mu_2\mid N$ (a finite set), and $\dd u$ the product of the Haar probability measures.
