In the situation of \cref{II.orb.thm:orbital-factorization} ($\gamma=\diag(a,b)$, $a\neq b$): $x\mapsto F_v(x^{-1}\gamma x)\,v_v(x)$ is $\nu_v$-integrable for every place $v$,
$J_{\gamma,p}(F_p;\nu_p)=0$ for almost all $p$, and the weighted orbital integral of \cref{II.orb.def:weighted-orbital-integral} at $T=0$ is
\[
  J_0\bigl(\gamma,F|_{G(\A)^1};\nu^1\bigr)=J_{\gamma,\infty}(F_\infty;\nu_\infty)\prod_pO_{\gamma,p}(F_p;\nu_p)
  +\sum_pJ_{\gamma,p}(F_p;\nu_p)\,O_{\gamma,\infty}(F_\infty;\nu_\infty)\prod_{q\neq p}O_{\gamma,q}(F_q;\nu_q),
\]
a finite sum of finite products (\cref{II.orb.def:local-orbital-integral,II.orb.def:local-weighted-orbital-integral}).
