Let $\gamma\in\GL_2(\Q)$. Then $\AGplus\subseteq G_\gamma(\A)$, and the map $\AGplus\times G_\gamma(\A)^1\to G_\gamma(\A)$,
$(a,g)\mapsto ag$, is an isomorphism of topological groups; it is the restriction of the isomorphism
$\AGplus\times G(\A)^1\cong G(\A)$ of \texttt{I.topgrp.thm:norm-one-splitting}.
