Let $\mu$ be a left Haar measure on $G(\A)$, $\dd\dot x$ the measure on $X=G(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1$ of \texttt{I.quot.def:automorphic-quotient-measure}, and $\dd y$ the quotient
measure of $\mu^1$ (\texttt{I.haar.def:haar-norm-one}) on $B(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1$ (\texttt{X.quot.def:quotient-measure}; $B(\Q)$ is a discrete subgroup of $G(\A)^1$). Let
$T\in\aB$ and let $F\colon G(\A)^1\to[0,\infty]$ be Borel and left $B(\Q)$-invariant. Then the sum
$\sum_{\delta\in B(\Q)\backslash G(\Q)}F(\delta x)\,\tau_B(\HB(\delta x)-T)\in[0,\infty]$ (whose terms depend only on the cosets $B(\Q)\delta$) depends only on $G(\Q)x$, is a
Borel function on $X$, and
\[
  \int_X\sum_{\delta\in B(\Q)\backslash G(\Q)}F(\delta x)\,\tau_B(\HB(\delta x)-T)\,\dd\dot x=\int_{B(\Q)\backslash G(\A)^1}F(y)\,\tau_B(\HB(y)-T)\,\dd y .
\]
