Let $I$ be a countable set. For each $i\in I$ let $G_i$ be a Hausdorff, second countable topological group with its
Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $K_i\le G_i$ a compact open subgroup, and $\mu_i$ a left Haar measure on $G_i$, such that
$\mu_i(K_i)=1$ for all but finitely many $i$. Let $G=\prod'_i(G_i,K_i)$ be the restricted product (cofinite filter), a
locally compact, Hausdorff, second countable topological group in which $\prod_iK_i$ is a compact open subgroup. The
\emph{restricted-product measure} $\bigotimes'_i\mu_i$ is the left Haar measure on $G$ with
\[
  \Bigl(\bigotimes\nolimits'_i\mu_i\Bigr)\Bigl(\prod_iK_i\Bigr)=\prod_i\mu_i(K_i),
\]
a finite product (all but finitely many factors equal $1$). The same definition applies verbatim to additive groups.
