Let $G$ be a locally compact, Hausdorff, second countable group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, and let $\Gamma\le G$ be a
discrete subgroup such that $\Gamma\backslash G$ is compact. Then for every left Haar measure $\mu$ on $G$ the quotient
measure of \cref{X.quot.def:quotient-measure} satisfies
\[
  0<\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}(\Gamma\backslash G)<\infty.
\]
