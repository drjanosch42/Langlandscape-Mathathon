Let $G$ be a locally compact, Hausdorff, second countable group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $\Gamma\le G$ a discrete
subgroup, $\mu$ a left Haar measure on $G$ and $c$ the counting measure on $\Gamma$. Then $\Gamma$ is closed and unimodular, $c$ is
a left Haar measure on $\Gamma$, and the quotient measure $\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}$ (\cref{X.quot.def:quotient-measure}) is a Weil
measure for $(\mu,c)$ (\cref{X.quot.def:weil-measure}).
