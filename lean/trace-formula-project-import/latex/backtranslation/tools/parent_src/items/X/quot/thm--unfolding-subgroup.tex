Let $G$ be a locally compact, Hausdorff, second countable group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $\mu$ a left Haar measure on
$G$, $\Gamma\le G$ a discrete subgroup and $\Gamma'\le\Gamma$ a subgroup, with the quotient measures $\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}$ and
$\mu_{\Gamma'\backslash G}$ (\cref{X.quot.def:quotient-measure}). Let $F\colon G\to[0,\infty]$ be Borel and left $\Gamma'$-invariant, regarded
also as a function on $\Gamma'\backslash G$. Then $\sum_{\delta\in\Gamma'\backslash\Gamma}F(\delta x)$ (the sum over the right cosets $\Gamma'\delta$; the
term $F(\delta x)$ depends only on $\Gamma'\delta$) depends only on $\Gamma x$, is a Borel function of $\Gamma x\in\Gamma\backslash G$, and
\[
  \int_{\Gamma'\backslash G}F\,\dd\mu_{\Gamma'\backslash G}=\int_{\Gamma\backslash G}\sum_{\delta\in\Gamma'\backslash\Gamma}F(\delta x)\,\dd\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}(\Gamma x).
\]
If $F\colon G\to\C$ is Borel, left $\Gamma'$-invariant and $\mu_{\Gamma'\backslash G}$-integrable, then the series
$\sum_{\delta\in\Gamma'\backslash\Gamma}F(\delta x)$ converges absolutely for $\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}$-almost every $\Gamma x$, the resulting function
is $\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}$-integrable, and the same formula holds.
