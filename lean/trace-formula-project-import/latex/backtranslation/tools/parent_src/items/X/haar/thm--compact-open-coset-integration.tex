Let $G$ be a locally compact, Hausdorff, second countable group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $K\le G$ a compact
open subgroup and $B\le G$ a closed subgroup with $G=BK$. Let $\mu_G$ be a left Haar measure on $G$, $\mu_B$ a left Haar
measure on $B$, and $\dd k$ the Haar probability measure of $K$. Then $0<\mu_B(B\cap K)<\infty$, and
\[
  \int_Gf\,\dd\mu_G=\frac{\mu_G(K)}{\mu_B(B\cap K)}\int_B\int_Kf(bk)\,\dd k\,\dd\mu_B(b)
\]
for every Borel function $f\colon G\to[0,\infty]$ and for every $\mu_G$-integrable $f\colon G\to\C$.
