Let $G$ be a locally compact, Hausdorff, second countable group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $H\le G$ a closed subgroup and
$\Gamma\le H$ a subgroup which is discrete in $G$. Assume that $G$ and $H$ are unimodular (\cref{X.haar.def:is-unimodular}). Let
$\mu_G$, $\mu_H$ be left Haar measures on $G$ and $H$, $\nu$ a Weil measure for $(\mu_G,\mu_H)$ on $H\backslash G$
(\cref{X.quot.def:weil-measure}), and $\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}$, $(\mu_H)_{\Gamma\backslash H}$ the quotient measures
(\cref{X.quot.def:quotient-measure}). Let $F\colon G\to[0,\infty]$ be Borel and left $\Gamma$-invariant. Then
$\int_{\Gamma\backslash H}F(hx)\,\dd(\mu_H)_{\Gamma\backslash H}(\Gamma h)$ depends only on $Hx$, is a Borel function of $Hx\in H\backslash G$, and
\[
  \int_{\Gamma\backslash G}F\,\dd\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}=\int_{H\backslash G}\int_{\Gamma\backslash H}F(hx)\,\dd(\mu_H)_{\Gamma\backslash H}(\Gamma h)\,\dd\nu(Hx).
\]
If $F\colon G\to\C$ is Borel, left $\Gamma$-invariant and $\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}$-integrable, then the inner integral converges absolutely for
$\nu$-almost every $Hx$, the resulting function is $\nu$-integrable, and the same formula holds.
