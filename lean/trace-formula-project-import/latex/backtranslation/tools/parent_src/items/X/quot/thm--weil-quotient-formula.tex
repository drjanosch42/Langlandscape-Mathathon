Let $G$ be a locally compact, Hausdorff, second countable group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $H\le G$ a closed subgroup,
$\mu_G$ and $\mu_H$ left Haar measures on $G$ and $H$, and $\Delta_G$, $\Delta_H$ their modular functions
($\mu(Eg)=\Delta(g)\mu(E)$). Let $G/H=\{xH:x\in G\}$ carry the quotient topology and its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, and for
$f\colon G\to\C$ put $f^\flat(xH)=\int_Hf(xh)\,\dd\mu_H(h)$ where this exists. Assume $\Delta_G(h)=\Delta_H(h)$ for all
$h\in H$. Then there is a unique Borel measure $\nu$ on $G/H$, finite on compact sets, such that
\[
  \int_Gf\,\dd\mu_G=\int_{G/H}f^\flat\,\dd\nu
\]
for every continuous $f\colon G\to\C$ with compact support. This $\nu$ is invariant under left translations,
$\nu(gE)=\nu(E)$ for $g\in G$, and the formula holds for every Borel $f\colon G\to[0,\infty]$ (with $f^\flat$ Borel with values in
$[0,\infty]$) and for every $\mu_G$-integrable $f$ (with $f^\flat$ defined and finite $\nu$-almost everywhere and $\nu$-integrable).
