Let $G$ be a second countable locally compact Hausdorff topological group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $\Gamma\le G$ a discrete subgroup and $\mu$ a Haar measure of $G$. Then the $\sigma$-algebra of $\Gamma\backslash G$ is the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of the quotient topology; $\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}(E)=\mu(D\cap\pi^{-1}(E))$ for every measurable $E$ and every measurable $D\subseteq G$ such that every $g\in G$ has a unique $\gamma\in\Gamma$ with $\gamma^{-1}g\in D$; and $\mu_{\Gamma\backslash G}$ is finite on compact sets.
