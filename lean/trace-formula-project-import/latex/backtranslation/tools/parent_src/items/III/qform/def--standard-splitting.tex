Let $a,b\in\Z$, let $K$ be a field of characteristic $0$ and $s_0\in K$ with $s_0^2=a$. The \emph{standard splitting} is the
splitting of $\Bq$ over $K$ with
\[
  i\mapsto\begin{pmatrix}s_0&0\\0&-s_0\end{pmatrix},\qquad j\mapsto\begin{pmatrix}0&b\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
  k\mapsto\begin{pmatrix}0&bs_0\\-s_0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
