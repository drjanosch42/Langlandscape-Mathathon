Let $a,b\in\Z$, $\Oord$ an order of $\Bq$ and $m,n\ge1$ coprime. Then $\mathbf 1_{\Ohat_m}*\mathbf 1_{\Ohat_n}=\mathbf 1_{\Ohat_{mn}}$ in
$\mathcal H(\Bhat^\times,\Ohat^\times;\Z)$, and for every field $K$ of characteristic $0$, splitting $s$ over $K$, $k\in\N$ and
$f\in M_k(\Oord;s)$: $T_mT_nf=T_{mn}f=T_nT_mf$.
