Let $F$ be a field and $a,b\in F$. The \emph{Hilbert symbol} is the integer
\[
  \hilb abF=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }z^2=ax^2+by^2\text{ has a solution }(x,y,z)\in F^3\setminus\{0\},\\-1&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}
\]
(Serre's definition; it is used for $a,b\in F^\times$). For a prime $p$ and $a,b\in\Qp$ put $\hilb abp=\hilb ab{\Qp}$; for $a,b\in\Q$ (in particular
$a,b\in\Z$), $\hilb abp$ is the symbol of their images in $\Qp$.
