Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $a,b\colon\N\to R$, and let $D\in\N$ be squarefree. Then
\[
  \sum_{d\mid D}\ \prod_{p\mid D/d}b(p)\prod_{p\mid d}a(p)=\prod_{p\mid D}\bigl(a(p)+b(p)\bigr)\quad\text{in }R,
\]
where $d$ runs over the positive divisors of $D$ and each product runs over the primes $p$ dividing the indicated number.
