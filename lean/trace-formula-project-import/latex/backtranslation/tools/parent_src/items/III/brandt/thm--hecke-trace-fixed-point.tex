Let $a,b\in\Z$ with $a<0$ and $b<0$, $\Oord$ an order of $\Bq$, $K$ a field of characteristic $0$, $s$ a splitting of $\Bq$ over $K$, $k\ge2$
and $n\ge1$; for $c\in X(\Oord)$ let $x_c$ be the chosen representative and $\Oord_{x_c}=\iota^{-1}(x_c\Ohat x_c^{-1})$. Then $T_n$ restricts to
a $K$-linear endomorphism of $M_k(\Oord;s)$, and
\[
  \tr\bigl(T_n\mid M_k(\Oord;s)\bigr)=\sum_{c\in X(\Oord)}\frac1{\abs{\Gamma_{x_c}}}\sum_{\substack{\gamma\in\Oord_{x_c}\\ \nrd\gamma=n}}P_k(\trd\gamma,n).
\]
