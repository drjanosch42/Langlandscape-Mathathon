Let $a,b\in\Z$, let $L\subseteq\Bq$ be a $\Z$-submodule (typically a lattice) and $p$ a prime. The \emph{completion of $L$ at $p$} is the
$\Zp$-submodule $L_p\subseteq\Bq_{\Qp}$ spanned by the image of $L$ under the base change $\Bq\to\Bq_{\Qp}$ along $\Q\to\Qp$
(\cref{III.qalg.def:quaternion-algebra}).
