Let $a,b\in\Z$ and $D\in\N$ with $\operatorname{RamEx}(a,b,D)$ (\cref{III.qalg.def:ramified-exactly}), and let $p$ be a prime with $p\mid D$.
Then
\[
  \Oord_{D,p}=\{x\in\Bq_{\Qp}:\nrd(x)\in\Zp\}
\]
is a $\Zp$-order of $\Bq_{\Qp}$ (a subring that is a compact open $\Zp$-submodule spanning $\Bq_{\Qp}$ over $\Qp$) and contains every element of
$\Bq_{\Qp}$ that is integral over $\Zp$. For every maximal order $\Oord$ of $\Bq$ (\cref{III.qord.def:maximal-order}), $\Oord_p=\Oord_{D,p}$.
