Let $H\colon\Z\to\Q$ be a function such that
\[
  H(p^2\Delta)=\bigl(p-\chi_\Delta(p)\bigr)\,H(\Delta)
\]
for every $\Delta\in\Z$ with $\Delta<0$ and $\Delta\equiv0$ or $1\pmod4$ and every prime $p$ (with $\chi$ as in \cref{III.ef.def:kronecker-prime}).
Let $\Delta_0<0$ be a fundamental discriminant (that is, $\Delta_0\equiv0$ or $1\pmod4$ and no prime divides the conductor of $\Delta_0$) and $m\ge1$
an integer. Then
\[
  H(\Delta_0m^2)=H(\Delta_0)\prod_{p\mid m}p^{v_p(m)-1}\bigl(p-\chi_{\Delta_0}(p)\bigr),
\]
the product running over the primes dividing $m$, where $v_p(m)$ is the exponent of $p$ in $m$.
