Let $t\in\Z$, $n\in\N$, $f\in\Ford(t,n)$ (\cref{III.ef.def:order-index-set}) and let $p$ be a prime. Then, with $\embmu$ as in \cref{III.ef.def:cohen-mu}
and $\chi$ as in \cref{III.ef.def:kronecker-prime},
\[
  \embmu_p(t,f,n)=\begin{cases}1+\chi_{t^2-4n}(p)&\text{if }p\nmid f,\\ p+1&\text{if }p\mid f.\end{cases}
\]
