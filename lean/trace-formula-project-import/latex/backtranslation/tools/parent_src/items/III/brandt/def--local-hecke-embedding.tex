Let $a,b\in\Z$, $D,M\in\N$ with $M\ge1$ and $\operatorname{RamEx}(a,b,D)$, $\Oord_0$ a maximal order of $\Bq$, $\phi$ a level structure of
level $M$ on $\Oord_0$ and $\Oord=\Oord_0(M;\phi)$. Let $p$ be a prime with $p\nmid D$ and $\iota_p\colon\Bq_{\Qp}\to\Mat_2(\Qp)$ as in
\cref{III.qalg.thm:maximal-order-local-split} for $\Oord_0$. For $\Phi\colon\GL_2(\Qp)\to\C$ define $\Theta_p(\Phi)\colon\Bhat^\times\to\C$ by
\[
  \Theta_p(\Phi)(x)=\Phi(\iota_p(x_p))\cdot\mathbf 1[\,x_q\in\Oord_q^\times\text{ for every prime }q\ne p\,],
\]
where $x_q\in\Bq_{\Q_q}^\times$ is the $q$-component of $x$ and $\Oord_q^\times=\{z:z,z^{-1}\in\Oord_q\}$.
