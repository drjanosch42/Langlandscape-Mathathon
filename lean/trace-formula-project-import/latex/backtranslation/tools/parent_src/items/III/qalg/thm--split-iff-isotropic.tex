Let $F$ be a field with $2\ne0$ in $F$, let $a,b\in F^\times$, and let $\Bq_F(a,b)$ be the quaternion algebra over $F$ with $i^2=a$,
$j^2=b$, $ij=-ji=k$ and reduced norm $\nrd(x)=x_0^2-a\,x_1^2-b\,x_2^2+ab\,x_3^2$ (\cref{III.qalg.def:quaternion-algebra,III.qalg.def:reduced-norm},
with $a,b\in F$). The following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $\hilb abF=1$;
\item there is $x\in\Bq_F(a,b)$ with $x\ne0$ and $\nrd(x)=0$;
\item $\Bq_F(a,b)\cong\Mat_2(F)$ as $F$-algebras.
\end{enumerate}
If they do not hold, every nonzero element of $\Bq_F(a,b)$ is a unit.
