Let $a,b\in\Z$ and let $U'\le U\le\Bhat^\times$ be subgroups with $[U:U']$ finite and $\Bq^\times\backslash\Bhat^\times/U$ finite. For every
double coset $c$ in $\Bq^\times\backslash\Bhat^\times/U$ or in $\Bq^\times\backslash\Bhat^\times/U'$ fix a representative $x_c\in\Bhat^\times$. Then
\[
  \sum_{c'\in\Bq^\times\backslash\Bhat^\times/U'}\frac1{\abs{\Gamma_{x_{c'}}(U')}}=[U:U']\sum_{c\in\Bq^\times\backslash\Bhat^\times/U}\frac1{\abs{\Gamma_{x_c}(U)}}
\]
in $\Q$, with the stabilizers of \cref{III.qcls.def:stabilizer} and the conventions of \cref{III.qcls.def:mass} for infinite sets.
