Let $t\in\Z$ and $n\in\N$ with $t^2<4n$. Then there is an integer $c\ge1$ such that $\Ford(t,n)$ is the set of positive divisors of $c$, the integer
$\Delta_0=(t^2-4n)/c^2$ is a fundamental discriminant with $\Delta_0<0$ (that is, $\Delta_0<0$, $\Delta_0\equiv0$ or $1\pmod4$, and no prime divides the
conductor of $\Delta_0$), and for every divisor $f$ of $c$ and every prime $p$,
\[
  p\mid\mathrm{cond}\bigl((t^2-4n)/f^2\bigr)\iff p\mid c/f,
\]
where $p\mid\mathrm{cond}\,\Delta$ is as in \cref{III.ef.def:conductor-prime} and $\Ford$ is as in \cref{III.ef.def:order-index-set}.
