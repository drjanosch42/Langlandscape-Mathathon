Let $s_H$ and $s_{11}$ be the standard splittings (\cref{III.qform.def:standard-splitting}) over $\C$ with $s_0=i$ of
$\Bq(-1,-1)$ and of $\Bq(-1,-11)$. For the Hurwitz order $\Oord_H$ (\cref{III.qcls.ex:hurwitz-order-mass}),
\[
  \dim_\C M_k(\Oord_H;s_H)=1,\,0,\,0,\,1,\,1,\,0\qquad\text{for }k=2,4,6,8,10,12,
\]
and for Pizer's order $\Oord_{11}$ of $\Bq(-1,-11)$ (\cref{III.qcls.ex:eichler-mass-instance-d11}), $\dim_\C M_2(\Oord_{11};s_{11})=2$.
