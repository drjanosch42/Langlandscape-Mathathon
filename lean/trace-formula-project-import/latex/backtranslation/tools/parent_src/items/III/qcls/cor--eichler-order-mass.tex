Let $a,b\in\Z$, $D\in\N$, let $\Oord$ be a maximal order of $\Bq$, $M\ge1$, and $\phi$ a level structure of level $M$ on $\Oord$.
Assume the hypothesis H-EM$(a,b,D,\Oord)$ (\cref{III.qcls.hyp:eichler-mass}) and $\operatorname{RamEx}(a,b,D)$. Then
\[
  \Mass(\Oord(M;\phi))=\frac1{24}\prod_{p\mid D}(p-1)\cdot\dedpsi(M).
\]
