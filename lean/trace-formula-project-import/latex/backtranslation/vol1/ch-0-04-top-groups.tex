By \cref{I.topgrp.thm:sl2-strong-approximation} the set $W$ contains the image of some $\gamma_1\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\Q)$. Take $\gamma=\gamma_0\gamma_1$, $a=\gamma_1^{-1}g_{1,\infty}$ (determinant $r>0$) and $k=k_1^{-1}$, where $k_1=g_{1,f}^{-1}\gamma_1\delta\in K'$. The identity $g=\gamma\,a\,\delta k$ is checked separately at the infinite and the finite component (\texttt{gl\_adele\_ext}, \texttt{group}). The theorem follows with $h=a$ at $\infty$ and $\delta k$ at the finite places.
\end{proof}

\begin{tffidelity}

\end{tffidelity}

\begin{proof}[Lean proof]
Differs from A in the openness argument (divergence note, \texttt{double-coset-det}). Notation: $\iota\colon\hat\Z^\times\to\A_f^\times$, $D=\iota^{-1}(\det K')$, $U$ the $\hat\Z^\times$-component of the decomposition $e$ (a homomorphism); $U(q\cdot(r,u))=u$ (\texttt{hUe}), so $U$ kills $\Q^\times$ and $\R_{>0}$. Openness: $u\mapsto\mathrm{diag}(u,1)$ is a continuous homomorphism $\hat\Z^\times\to K_f$, the preimage of the open $K'$ is an open subgroup, contained in $D$ since $\det\mathrm{diag}(u,1)=u$; a subgroup containing an open subgroup is open (\texttt{Subgroup.isOpen\_mono}). Surjectivity: a class $[v]$ is the image of $g=(1,\mathrm{diag}(v,1))$, as $U(\det g)=v$ (\texttt{hdetfin}). ``$\Leftarrow$'' of the equivalence: for $g'=\gamma g h k$ with $h\in G(\R)^+$ at $\infty$, $k\in K'$ at the finite places, $U(\det g')=U(\det g)\cdot w$ where $\iota(w)=\det k_f\in\det K'$ (\texttt{hdh}, \texttt{hdk}, \texttt{hd\gamma}: $\det\gamma$ is a rational idele and $\det h$ lies in $\R_{>0}$), so the classes agree. ``$\Rightarrow$'': the equality of classes gives $k_0\in K'$ with $\det k_0\in\iota(U(\det g)^{-1}U(\det g'))$. Write $\det g=q(r,u)$, $\det g'=q'(r',u'')$ (\texttt{exists\_ratIdele\_mul\_ideleInfFin}). Apply the claim of \cref{I.topgrp.thm:strong-approximation} (\texttt{strong\_approx\_claim}) to $g'$ with $\delta'=\mathrm{diag}(u'',1)$ and to $g$ with $\delta=\mathrm{diag}(u'',1)k_0^{-1}$ (its determinant is $\iota(u)$ by the choice of $k_0$): $g'=\gamma'a'\delta'k'$ and $g=\gamma a\delta k$. Eliminating the common factor $\delta'$, $g'=(\gamma'\gamma^{-1})\,g\,(a^{-1}a')\,\bigl((k_0^{-1}k)^{-1}k'\bigr)$, where $a^{-1}a'\in\GL_2(\R)^+$ sits at $\infty$ and $(k_0^{-1}k)^{-1}k'\in K'$ at the finite places; they commute with each other and the identity is checked at each component (\texttt{gl\_adele\_ext}).
\end{proof}
