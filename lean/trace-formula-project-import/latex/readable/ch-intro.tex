\chapter*{Introduction}\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}\label{ch:intro}

\noindent\emph{Purpose.} This volume collects the \emph{algebraic, arithmetic and topological} material of the
trace\_formula\_gl2 project that is needed for an approach to the Jacquet--Langlands correspondence between
$\GL_2$ over $\Q$ and a definite quaternion algebra $\Bq$ over $\Q$ that does not go through the trace formula.
Such approaches (Eichler's and Shimizu's comparison of Brandt matrices with Hecke operators, read through the
cohomology of the finite double coset spaces $\Bq^\times\backslash\Bhat^\times/\Ohat^\times$ and of the
congruence subgroups of $\SL_2(\Z)$; or the geometric approach through supersingular points and the reduction of
modular curves) need the arithmetic of quaternion orders, the class sets and their Hecke operators, the Brandt
matrices, the spherical Hecke algebra of $\GL_2(\Qp)$ and its Satake isomorphism, and the group-theoretic
structure of $\GL_2$ over $\Qp$, $\R$ and the adeles: decompositions, maximal compact and congruence subgroups,
double cosets and strong approximation. They do not need Haar measures, integration, $L^2$ spaces, automorphic
forms, Eisenstein series, kernels or spectral theory, and none of this is contained here.

\noindent\emph{Origin.} The volume is a fork of the parent project at commit \texttt{84818d0} (2026-11-14). It
consists, as the parent, of three documents: this readable edition (A), a back-translation edition (B) that mirrors
the Lean code, and the Lean~4 code on Mathlib (C), with the invariants A $\subseteq$ B $\cong$ C. Every statement is
the parent's statement, with the same identifier (\texttt{I.\dots}, \texttt{II.\dots}, \texttt{III.\dots},
\texttt{X.\dots}) and the same text, so that each item can be traced to the parent; the identifiers therefore still
carry the volume prefixes of the parent. The analytic declarations of the parent were removed; every resulting
change of a kept chapter (a dropped item, a proof that used a dropped analytic item and is replaced by a remark
with a citation, a statement from which a reference to a dropped item was deleted) is recorded in the file
\texttt{latex/EDITS.md} of the fork.

\noindent\emph{Contents.} Part~I treats the split group $G=\GL_2$ over $\Q$: conventions on the algebraic groups
(\cref{ch:ch-0-01-conventions}); non-archimedean local fields and their normalized absolute values
(\cref{ch:ch-0-02-local-field}); the adeles and ideles of $\Q$ as rings and topological groups
(\cref{ch:ch-0-03-adeles}); $\GL_2$ as a topological group over $\Qp$, $\R$ and $\A$, its maximal compact and
congruence subgroups and strong approximation (\cref{ch:ch-0-04-top-groups}); the Bruhat, Iwasawa and Cartan
decompositions and the index of a double coset (\cref{ch:ch-0-05-decompositions}); local compactness and restricted products (\cref{ch:ch-0-06-haar}); discrete subgroups and compact
quotients (\cref{ch:ch-0-07-quotient}); Siegel sets (\cref{ch:ch-0-08-reduction}); smooth and admissible representations (\cref{ch:ch-0-10-reps}); the spherical Hecke algebra and
the Satake isomorphism (\cref{ch:ch-1-01-spherical}); the algebraic theory of smooth representations of $\GL_2(\Qp)$
(\cref{ch:ch-1-02-smooth}); and parabolic subgroups, conjugacy classes, elliptic centralizers and the combinatorics of
cosets $B(\Q)\backslash G(\Q)$ (\cref{ch:ch-02-parabolic,ch:ch-03-kernel,ch:ch-04-orbital,ch:ch-06-truncation}).
Part~II is the parent's Volume~III without its analytic part: quaternion algebras over $\Q$, the Hilbert symbol and
ramification (\cref{ch:ch-iii-06-quatalg}); orders, Eichler orders, class sets, the mass and the compactness of
$\Bq^\times\backslash\Bq^\times(\A)^1$ (\cref{ch:ch-iii-07-orders}); quaternionic forms, Hecke operators and Brandt
matrices (\cref{ch:ch-iii-08-brandt}). \Cref{ch:ch-iii-01-explicit} (an appendix) is the finite arithmetic layer of
the Jacquet--Langlands identity: explicit formulas and local factors, compared as rational expressions, with no
analysis.

\noindent\emph{Hypotheses.} The only unproved input is Eichler's mass formula (\cref{ch:hypotheses}), used for the
mass of Eichler orders; it is a \texttt{Prop}-valued hypothesis, not an axiom, and it is proved for the instances
$D=2$ and $D=11$.
