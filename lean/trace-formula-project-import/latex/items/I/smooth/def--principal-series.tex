Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$, and let $\chi_1,\chi_2$ be quasi-characters of $F^\times$. The
\emph{(normalized) principal series} $I(\chi_1,\chi_2)$ is the set of functions $f\colon G\to\C$ such that
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item there is a compact open subgroup $K'\subseteq G$ with $f(gk)=f(g)$ for all $g\in G$ and $k\in K'$, and
\item $f\bigl(n(x)\diag(t_1,t_2)\,g\bigr)=\chi_1(t_1)\,\chi_2(t_2)\,\abs{t_1/t_2}_F^{1/2}\,f(g)$ for all $x\in F$,
  $t_1,t_2\in F^\times$ and $g\in G$,
\end{enumerate}
where $\abs{t_1/t_2}_F^{1/2}$ is the positive square root. It is a $\C$-linear subspace of the space of all functions
$G\to\C$.
