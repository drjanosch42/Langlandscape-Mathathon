Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field with uniformizer $\unif$, and for $x\in F^\times$ let
$\operatorname{ord}(x)\in\Z$ be the integer with $x\in\unif^{\operatorname{ord}(x)}\Ocal_F^\times$. Every unramified
quasi-character $\chi$ of $F^\times$ satisfies $\chi(x)=\chi(\unif)^{\operatorname{ord}(x)}$ for all $x\in F^\times$, and
the map $\chi\mapsto\chi(\unif)$ is a bijection from the set of unramified quasi-characters of $F^\times$ onto
$\C^\times$. Consequently $(\chi_1,\chi_2)\mapsto(\chi_1(\unif),\chi_2(\unif))$ is a bijection from the set of pairs of
unramified quasi-characters of $F^\times$ onto $(\C^\times)^2$.
