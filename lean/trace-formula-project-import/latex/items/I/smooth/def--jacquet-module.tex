Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$, and let $\pi$ be a representation of $G$ on a complex vector
space $V$. Put
\[
  V(N)=\operatorname{span}_\C\{\pi(n)v-v:n\in N(F),\ v\in V\}\subseteq V,\qquad V_N=V/V(N).
\]
The \emph{Jacquet module} of $\pi$ is $V_N$ with the action $\pi_N(t)(v+V(N))=\pi(t)v+V(N)$ of $T(F)$; this is well
defined, and $\pi_N$ is a representation of $T(F)$ on $V_N$.
