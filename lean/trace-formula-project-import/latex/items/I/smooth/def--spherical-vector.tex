Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$, $K=K_F$, and let $\chi_1,\chi_2$ be unramified
quasi-characters of $F^\times$. The \emph{spherical vector} $f^\circ_\chi\colon G\to\C$ is the function with
\[
  f^\circ_\chi\bigl(n(x)\diag(t_1,t_2)\,k\bigr)=\chi_1(t_1)\,\chi_2(t_2)\,\abs{t_1/t_2}_F^{1/2}
  \qquad(x\in F,\ t_1,t_2\in F^\times,\ k\in K).
\]
Every $g\in G$ has this form, and the value does not depend on the choice of $x$, $t_1$, $t_2$, $k$.
