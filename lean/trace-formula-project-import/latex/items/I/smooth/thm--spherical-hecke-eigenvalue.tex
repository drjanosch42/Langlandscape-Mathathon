Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field with uniformizer $\unif$ and residue cardinality $q$, $G=\GL_2(F)$, $K=K_F$,
let $\chi_1,\chi_2$ be unramified quasi-characters of $F^\times$, and $(\alpha,\beta)=(\chi_1(\unif),\chi_2(\unif))$.
For every $f\in\mathcal H(G,K)$, the action of $f$ on the $K$-fixed vectors of $\pi_\chi$ satisfies
\[
  \pi_\chi(f)\,f^\circ_\chi=\omega_{\alpha,\beta}(f)\,f^\circ_\chi=(\Sat f)(\alpha,\beta)\,f^\circ_\chi .
\]
In particular $\pi_\chi(T_\unif)f^\circ_\chi=q^{1/2}(\alpha+\beta)f^\circ_\chi$ and
$\pi_\chi(R_\unif)f^\circ_\chi=\alpha\beta f^\circ_\chi$.
