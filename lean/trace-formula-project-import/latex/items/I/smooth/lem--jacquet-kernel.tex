Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$, and let $\pi$ be a smooth representation of $G$ on a complex
vector space $V$. For $v\in V$ the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $v\in V(N)$;
\item there are compact open subgroups $N_1\subseteq N_0$ of $N(F)$ such that $\pi(n)v=v$ for all $n\in N_1$ and
  \[
    \sum_{nN_1\in N_0/N_1}\pi(n)v=0 .
  \]
\end{enumerate}
