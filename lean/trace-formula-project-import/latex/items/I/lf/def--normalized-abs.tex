Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field and $\mu$ an additive Haar measure on $F$. For $a\in F^\times$ the
\emph{normalized absolute value} $\abs{a}_F\in\R_{>0}$ is the modulus of the automorphism $x\mapsto ax$ of the
additive group of $F$: the unique positive real number with
\[
  \mu(aE)=\abs{a}_F\,\mu(E)\qquad\text{for every Borel set }E\subseteq F.
\]
It does not depend on the choice of $\mu$. We put $\abs{0}_F=0$.
