Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field and $K=K_F$. The transpose $\tau(g)=g^{\mathsf T}$ is a map
$\GL_2(F)\to\GL_2(F)$ with $\tau(gh)=\tau(h)\tau(g)$ and $\tau(\tau(g))=g$ for all $g,h\in\GL_2(F)$, with
$\tau(K)=K$, and with $\tau(t_{a,b})=t_{a,b}$ for all $a,b\in\Z$. Moreover $\tau(g)\in K\,g\,K$ for every
$g\in\GL_2(F)$.
