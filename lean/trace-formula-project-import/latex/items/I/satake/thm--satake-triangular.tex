Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field with residue cardinality $q$, $G=\GL_2(F)$ and $K=K_F$, and let $a\ge b$ be
integers. Then $\Sat(c_{a,a})=X^aY^a$, and for $a>b$
\[
  \Sat(c_{a,b})=q^{(a-b)/2}\Bigl(X^aY^b+X^bY^a+(1-q^{-1})\sum_{i=b+1}^{a-1}X^iY^{a+b-i}\Bigr).
\]
In particular $\Sat(c_{a,b})=q^{(a-b)/2}(X^aY^b+X^bY^a)+{}$(a linear combination of monomials $X^iY^{a+b-i}$
with $b<i<a$).
