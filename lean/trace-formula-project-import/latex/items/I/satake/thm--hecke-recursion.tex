Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field with residue cardinality $q$, $G=\GL_2(F)$ and $K=K_F$, and let $a\ge b$ be
integers. In $\mathcal H(G,K)$,
\[
  T_\unif*c_{a,b}=
  \begin{cases}
    c_{a+1,b}+q\,c_{a,b+1}&\text{if }a-b\ge2,\\
    c_{a+1,b}+(q+1)\,c_{a,a}&\text{if }a-b=1,\\
    c_{a+1,a}&\text{if }a=b.
  \end{cases}
\]
