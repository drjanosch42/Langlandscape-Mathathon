Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $G=\GL_2(F)$ and $K=K_F$. The Satake transform is an isomorphism of
$\C$-algebras from $\mathcal H(G,K)$ onto the Weyl group invariants: there is a $\C$-algebra isomorphism
\[
  e\colon\mathcal H(G,K)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\C[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}]^{S_2}\qquad\text{with}\qquad e(f)=\Sat f\ \text{ for all }f\in\mathcal H(G,K).
\]
