The ring of \emph{adeles} of $\Q$ is $\A=\R\times\Af$, with componentwise ring operations and the product topology. We
write $x=(x_\infty,x_f)$ and $x_p$ for the components. The rational numbers embed diagonally,
$q\mapsto(q,(q)_p)$; this is an injective ring homomorphism $\Q\to\A$, since $q\in\Zp$ for every prime $p$ not dividing
the denominator of $q$.
