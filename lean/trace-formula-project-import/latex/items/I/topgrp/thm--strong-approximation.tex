Let $G(\R)^+=\{h\in\GL_2(\R):\det h>0\}$, viewed in $G(\A)$ via $h\mapsto(h,1)$ (\cref{I.topgrp.thm:gl2-adelic-restricted-product}),
and let $K'\subseteq\Kf$ be an open subgroup, viewed in $G(\A)$ via $k\mapsto(1,k)$, with $\det(K')=\Zhat^\times$. Then
\[
  G(\A)=G(\Q)\,G(\R)^+K',
\]
that is, every $g\in G(\A)$ is $g=\gamma hk$ with $\gamma\in G(\Q)$, $h\in G(\R)^+$ and $k\in K'$.
