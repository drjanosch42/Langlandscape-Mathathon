Let $f\colon R\to R'$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings and $f_*\colon\GL_2(R)\to\GL_2(R')$ the induced
homomorphism. Then $f_*$ maps $B(R)$ into $B(R')$, $T(R)$ into $T(R')$, $N(R)$ into $N(R')$, $Z(R)$ into $Z(R')$,
$\Bbar(R)$ into $\Bbar(R')$ and $\Nbar(R)$ into $\Nbar(R')$. More precisely, for $x\in R$ and $t_1,t_2\in R^\times$,
\[
  f_*(n(x))=n(f(x)),\qquad f_*(\bar n(x))=\bar n(f(x)),\qquad f_*(\diag(t_1,t_2))=\diag(f(t_1),f(t_2)),
\]
where $f(t_i)\in R'^\times$ is the image of the unit $t_i$.
