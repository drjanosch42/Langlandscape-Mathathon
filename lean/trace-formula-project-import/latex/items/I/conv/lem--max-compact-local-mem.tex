Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $F$ a field and $\iota\colon R\to F$ an injective ring homomorphism, and let
$g\in\GL_2(F)$. The following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $g\in K(R)$;
\item all entries of $g$ lie in $\iota(R)$, and $\det g\in\iota(R^\times)$;
\item all entries of $g$ and of $g^{-1}$ lie in $\iota(R)$.
\end{enumerate}
