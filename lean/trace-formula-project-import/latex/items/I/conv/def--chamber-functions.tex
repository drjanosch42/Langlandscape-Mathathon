The functions $\tau_B,\hat\tau_B\colon\aB\to\R$ are the characteristic functions of the positive chamber and of the
positive cone:
\[
  \tau_B(h)=\begin{cases}1&\alpha(h)>0,\\0&\alpha(h)\le0,\end{cases}\qquad
  \hat\tau_B(h)=\begin{cases}1&\varpi(h)>0,\\0&\varpi(h)\le0,\end{cases}
\]
where $\varpi=\frac12\alpha$ is the fundamental weight.
