Let $D\in\N$ be squarefree with $\omega(D)$ odd, where $\omega(m)$ is the number of distinct primes dividing $m$. Let $m,k,n\in\N$, $c\in\Q$,
and let $F\colon\Z\times\N\times\Z\to\Q$ and $G\colon\N\times\N\to\Q$ be any functions. Assume that $n$ is not a square or $c=0$. For a
positive divisor $d$ of $D$ put
\[
  s_d(m)=(-1)^{\omega(D/d)}\,2^{\omega(D/d)-\omega(\gcd(D/d,m))}\in\Q,
\]
and define $F_d\colon\Z\times\N\times\Z\to\Q$ and $G_d\colon\N\times\N\to\Q$ by
\[
  F_d(t,f,\Delta)=F(t,f,\Delta)\prod_{p\mid d}\begin{cases}\esym_p(\Delta)&\text{if }p\mid m,\\ \mone_p(\Delta)&\text{if }p\nmid m,\end{cases}
  \qquad G_d(a,a')=2^{\omega(d)-\omega(\gcd(d,m))}\,G(a,a'),
\]
the product over the primes dividing $d$. Then
\[
  \EichB_{\mathrm{gen}}(D,k,n;c,F)=-\frac12\sum_{d\mid D}s_d(m)\,\LamES_{\mathrm{gen}}\bigl(k,n;\dedpsi(d)\,c,F_d,G_d\bigr),
\]
the sum over the positive divisors $d$ of $D$, with $\EichB_{\mathrm{gen}}$, $\LamES_{\mathrm{gen}}$, $\dedpsi$, $\esym_p$ and $\mone_p$ as in
\cref{III.ef.def:eichler-b-expression-general,III.ef.def:lefschetz-es-expression-general,III.ef.def:dedekind-psi,III.ef.def:eichler-symbol,III.ef.def:local-factor-eichler-prime}.
