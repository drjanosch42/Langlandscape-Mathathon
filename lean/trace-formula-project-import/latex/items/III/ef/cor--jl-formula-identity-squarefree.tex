Let $D\in\N$ be squarefree with $\omega(D)$ odd, let $M\in\N$ be squarefree with $\gcd(D,M)=1$, and let $k,n\ge1$. For $L\in\N$ define
$F_L\colon\Z\to\Q$ by $F_L(\Delta)=\prod_{p\mid L}\mone_p(\Delta)$, the product over the primes dividing $L$. Then
\[
  \EichB(D,M,k,n;F_M)=-\frac12\sum_{d\mid D}(-2)^{\omega(D/d)}\,\LamES(dM,k,n;F_{dM}),
\]
the sum over the positive divisors $d$ of $D$, with $\EichB$, $\LamES$ and $\mone_p$ as in
\cref{III.ef.def:eichler-b-expression,III.ef.def:lefschetz-es-expression,III.ef.def:local-factor-eichler-prime}.
