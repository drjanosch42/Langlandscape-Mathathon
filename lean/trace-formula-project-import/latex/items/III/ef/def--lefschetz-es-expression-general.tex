For $k,n\in\N$, $\kappa\in\Q$ and functions $F\colon\Z\times\N\times\Z\to\Q$ and $\beta\colon\N\times\N\to\Q$ the \emph{general Lefschetz
expression} is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \LamES_{\mathrm{gen}}(k,n;\kappa,F,\beta)={}&\delta_{k,2}\,\sigma_1(n)-2\bigl(C(k,n)\,\kappa+A_{2,\mathrm{gen}}^{\mathrm E}(k,n;F)+A_{3,\mathrm{gen}}(k,n;\beta)+A_4(k,n)\bigr)\\
  &-\bigl(\Eist_{\mathrm{gen}}(k,n;\beta)-\delta_{k,2}\,\sigma_1(n)\bigr)\in\Q,
\end{aligned}
\]
where $C(k,n)=\frac{k-1}{12}r^{k-2}$ if $n=r^2$ with $r\in\N$ and $C(k,n)=0$ if $n$ is not a square ($k-1$ computed in $\Q$, the exponent $k-2$
read as $\max(k-2,0)$), $A_{2,\mathrm{gen}}^{\mathrm E}$, $A_{3,\mathrm{gen}}$, $A_4$ and $\Eist_{\mathrm{gen}}$ are as in
\cref{III.ef.def:eichler-elliptic-expression-general,III.ef.def:cohen-a3-general,III.ef.def:cohen-a4,III.ef.def:eisenstein-expression-corrected-general},
$\delta_{k,2}=1$ if $k=2$ and $0$ otherwise, and $\sigma_1(n)$ is the sum of the positive divisors of $n$.
