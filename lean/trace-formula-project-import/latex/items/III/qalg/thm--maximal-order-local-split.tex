Let $a,b\in\Z$ and $D\in\N$ with $\operatorname{RamEx}(a,b,D)$ (\cref{III.qalg.def:ramified-exactly}), let $\Oord$ be a maximal order of
$\Bq$ (\cref{III.qord.def:maximal-order}) and let $p$ be a prime with $p\nmid D$. Then there is an isomorphism of $\Qp$-algebras
$\iota_p\colon\Bq_{\Qp}\to\Mat_2(\Qp)$ with $\iota_p(\Oord_p)=\Mat_2(\Zp)$.
