Let $a,b\in\Z$, let $\Oord$ be an order of $\Bq$, $M\ge1$ and $\phi$ a level structure of level $M$ on $\Oord$
(\cref{III.qord.def:level-structure}). The \emph{Eichler order} of level $M$ attached to $\phi$ is
\[
  \Oord(M;\phi)=\{x\in\Oord:\phi(x)_{21}=0\},
\]
an order of $\Bq$ ($\phi(x)_{21}$ is the lower left entry).
