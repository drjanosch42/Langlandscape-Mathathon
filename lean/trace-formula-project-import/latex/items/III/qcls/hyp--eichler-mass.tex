Let $a,b\in\Z$, $D\in\N$, and let $\Oord$ be a maximal order of $\Bq=\Bq(a,b)$ (\texttt{III.qord.def:maximal-order}). The hypothesis
\emph{H-EM}$(a,b,D,\Oord)$ (Eichler's mass formula) is the statement: if $\operatorname{RamEx}(a,b,D)$ (\texttt{III.qalg.def:ramified-exactly}), then
\[
  \Mass(\Oord)=\frac1{24}\prod_{p\mid D}(p-1),
\]
the product over the primes dividing $D$, with $\Mass$ as in \texttt{III.qcls.def:mass} (full unit groups, conventions row N4).
