Let $(a,b)=(-1,-1)$ and let $\Oord_H=\Z\,\tfrac{1+i+j+k}2+\Z i+\Z j+\Z k\subseteq\Bq$ (the \emph{Hurwitz order}). Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item $\Oord_H$ is a maximal order of $\Bq$, and $d(\Oord_H)=4$;
\item $\abs{X(\Oord_H)}=1$ and $\abs{\Oord_H^\times}=24$;
\item $\Mass(\Oord_H)=\frac1{24}$;
\item the hypothesis H-EM$(-1,-1,2,\Oord_H)$ of \cref{III.qcls.hyp:eichler-mass} holds.
\end{enumerate}
