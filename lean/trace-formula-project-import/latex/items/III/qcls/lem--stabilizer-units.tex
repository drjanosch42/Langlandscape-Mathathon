Let $a,b\in\Z$, let $\Oord$ be an order of $\Bq$ and $x\in\Bhat^\times$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item $\Oord_x=\iota^{-1}(x\Ohat x^{-1})$ is an order of $\Bq$, and $\Gamma_x=\Oord_x^\times$ as subgroups of $\Bq^\times$;
\item $\Gamma_{\iota(\gamma)xu}=\gamma\,\Gamma_x\,\gamma^{-1}$ for $\gamma\in\Bq^\times$ and $u\in\Ohat^\times$;
\item if $a<0$ and $b<0$, then $\Gamma_x$ is finite and $\abs{\Gamma_x}\in\{2,4,6,8,12,24\}$.
\end{enumerate}
