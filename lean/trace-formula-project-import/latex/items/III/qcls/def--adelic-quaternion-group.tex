Let $a,b\in\Z$. The \emph{adelic quaternion algebra} is $\BA=\Bq_{\A}$ (\cref{III.qalg.def:quaternion-algebra} with $R=\A=\R\times\Af$,
\cref{I.adele.def:adele-ring}), with the topology for which the coordinate map $\BA\to\A^4$ is a homeomorphism; its unit group $\BA^\times$
carries the units topology. The reduced norm restricts to a group homomorphism $\nrd\colon\BA^\times\to\A^\times$ into the ideles
(\cref{I.adele.def:ideles}). Put
\[
  \BA^1=\{x\in\BA^\times:\abs{\nrd(x)}_\A=1\},
\]
a subgroup of $\BA^\times$ with the subspace topology, where $\abs\cdot_\A$ is the idele norm (\cref{I.adele.def:idele-norm}). Let
$\iota_\A\colon\Bq\to\BA$ be the base change along $\Q\to\A$. Then $\Gamma_{\Bq}=\iota_\A(\Bq^\times)$ is a subgroup of $\BA^1$ (by the
product formula, \cref{I.adele.thm:product-formula}).
