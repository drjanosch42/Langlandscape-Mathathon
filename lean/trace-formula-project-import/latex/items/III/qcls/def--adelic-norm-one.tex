Let $a,b\in\Z$. The group $\BA^1=\{x\in\BA^\times:\abs{\nrd(x)}_\A=1\}$ of \cref{III.qcls.def:adelic-quaternion-group} is the kernel of the composite
$\BA^\times\xrightarrow{\ \nrd\ }\A^\times\xrightarrow{\ \abs\cdot_\A\ }\R$ (idele norm, \cref{I.adele.def:idele-norm}; $\R$ as a multiplicative monoid, the kernel being the elements of norm $1$), a subgroup of $\BA^\times$.
