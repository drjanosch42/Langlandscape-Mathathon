Let $a,b\in\Z$. The group $\Gamma_{\Bq}=\iota_\A(\Bq^\times)$ of \cref{III.qcls.def:adelic-quaternion-group} is regarded as a subgroup of $\BA^1$ by intersecting
the image of $\Bq^\times$ in $\BA^\times$ with $\BA^1$; by the product formula this intersection is all of $\iota_\A(\Bq^\times)$
(first clause of \cref{III.qcls.thm:diagonal-discrete}).
