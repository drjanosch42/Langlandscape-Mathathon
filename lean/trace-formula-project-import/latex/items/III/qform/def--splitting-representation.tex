Let $a,b\in\Z$, let $K$ be a field of characteristic $0$, $s$ a splitting of $\Bq$ over $K$ and $k\in\N$. For $\gamma\in\Bq$ put
$\rho_k^s(\gamma)=\rho_{k-2}(s(\gamma))\in\End_K(V_{k-2}(K))$ (\cref{III.qform.def:sym-power-action}); here $k-2$ means $\max(k-2,0)$. We write
$\Vk=V_{k-2}(K)$ when $K$ is fixed.
