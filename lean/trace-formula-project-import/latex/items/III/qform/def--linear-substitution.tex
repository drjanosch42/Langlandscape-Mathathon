Let $K$ be a field and $A=\begin{psmallmatrix}A_{11}&A_{12}\\A_{21}&A_{22}\end{psmallmatrix}\in\Mat_2(K)$. The \emph{linear substitution} of $A$ is the pair of linear forms
$(A_{11}x+A_{21}y,\;A_{12}x+A_{22}y)\in K[x,y]^2$, homogeneous of degree $1$. The substitution homomorphism $K[x,y]\to K[x,y]$ determined by $x\mapsto A_{11}x+A_{21}y$, $y\mapsto A_{12}x+A_{22}y$
preserves homogeneous degree and restricts to $\rho_m(A)$ of \cref{III.qform.def:sym-power-action}.
