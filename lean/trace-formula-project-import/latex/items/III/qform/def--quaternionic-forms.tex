Let $a,b\in\Z$, $\Oord$ an order of $\Bq$, $K$ a field of characteristic $0$, $s$ a splitting of $\Bq$ over $K$ and $k\in\N$. The space of
\emph{quaternionic forms} of weight $k$ is the $K$-subspace
\[
  M_k(\Oord;s)=\{f\colon\Bhat^\times\to V_{k-2}(K)\;:\;f(\iota(\gamma)xu)=\rho_k^s(\gamma)f(x)\text{ for all }\gamma\in\Bq^\times,\ x\in\Bhat^\times,\
  u\in\Ohat^\times\}.
\]
