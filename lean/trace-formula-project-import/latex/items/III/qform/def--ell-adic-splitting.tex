Let $a,b\in\Z$ and $D\in\N$ with $\operatorname{RamEx}(a,b,D)$, let $\Oord$ be a maximal order of $\Bq$ and $\ell$ a prime with
$\ell\nmid D$. Choose $\iota_\ell\colon\Bq_{\Q_\ell}\to\Mat_2(\Q_\ell)$ as in \cref{III.qalg.thm:maximal-order-local-split}. The
\emph{$\ell$-adic splitting} is the splitting $s_\ell=\iota_\ell\circ(\Bq\to\Bq_{\Q_\ell})$ of $\Bq$ over $\Q_\ell$.
