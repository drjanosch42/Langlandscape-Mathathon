Let $K$ be a field of characteristic $0$, $a,b\in\Z$ and $s_0\in K$ with $s_0^2=a$. The matrices
$I_0=\begin{psmallmatrix}s_0&0\\0&-s_0\end{psmallmatrix}$, $J_0=\begin{psmallmatrix}0&b\\1&0\end{psmallmatrix}$, $K_0=\begin{psmallmatrix}0&bs_0\\-s_0&0\end{psmallmatrix}$ satisfy
$I_0^2=a$, $J_0^2=b$, $I_0J_0=K_0=-J_0I_0$; they form a quaternion basis of $\Mat_2(K)$ for the algebra $\Bq(a,b)$, i.e.\ the images of $i,j,k$ under the standard splitting of \cref{III.qform.def:standard-splitting}.
