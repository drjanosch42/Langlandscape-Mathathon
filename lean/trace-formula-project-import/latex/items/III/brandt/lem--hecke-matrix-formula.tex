Let $a,b\in\Z$ with $a<0$ and $b<0$, $\Oord$ an order of $\Bq$, $K$ a field of characteristic $0$, $s$ a splitting of $\Bq$ over $K$, $k\in\N$,
$n\ge1$, and let $x_c\in\Bhat^\times$ with $[x_c]=c$ for $c\in X(\Oord)$. For $c,c'\in X(\Oord)$ put
$S_{cc'}(n)=\{\gamma\in\Bq^\times:x_c^{-1}\iota(\gamma)x_{c'}\in\Ohat_n\}$. Then $S_{cc'}(n)$ is finite, $S_{cc'}(n)\,\Gamma_{x_{c'}}=S_{cc'}(n)$,
and for every $f\in M_k(\Oord;s)$ and $c\in X(\Oord)$
\[
  (T_nf)(x_c)=\sum_{c'\in X(\Oord)}\frac1{\abs{\Gamma_{x_{c'}}}}\sum_{\gamma\in S_{cc'}(n)}\rho_k^s(\gamma)f(x_{c'}).
\]
