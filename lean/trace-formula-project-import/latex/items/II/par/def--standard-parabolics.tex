The \emph{standard parabolic subgroups} of $G=\GL_2$ are $B$ and $G$; we write $\mathcal P=\{B,G\}$. The parabolic subgroups
with Levi component $T$ are $B$ and the opposite Borel subgroup $\Bbar$; we write $\mathcal P(T)=\{B,\Bbar\}$. Both are regarded
as two-element index sets, and $P\in\mathcal P\cup\mathcal P(T)$ stands for the subgroup $P(R)\subseteq\GL_2(R)$ for every commutative
ring $R$: $B(R)$ (\texttt{I.conv.def:borel}), $\Bbar(R)$ (\texttt{I.conv.def:opposite-borel}), $G(R)=\GL_2(R)$.
