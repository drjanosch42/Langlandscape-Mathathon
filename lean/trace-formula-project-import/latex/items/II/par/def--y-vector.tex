For $T\in\aB$ and $P\in\mathcal P(T)$ (\cref{II.par.def:standard-parabolics}) put $T_B=T$ and $T_{\Bbar}=wT$, with $w(h_1,h_2)=(h_2,h_1)$.
For $x\in G(\A)$ let
\[
  Y_P(x,T)=\bigl(T_P-H_P(x)\bigr)_B^G\in\aBG,
\]
where $H_P$ is the height map of \cref{II.par.def:parabolic-height} and $h\mapsto h_B^G$ the projection
$\aB\to\aBG$ (\texttt{I.conv.def:space-a-b-g}).
