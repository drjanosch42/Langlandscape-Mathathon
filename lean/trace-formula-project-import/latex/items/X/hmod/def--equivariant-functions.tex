Let $G$ and $\Gamma$ be groups, $\iota\colon\Gamma\to G$ a homomorphism, $U\le G$ a subgroup, $R$ a commutative ring, $V$ an $R$-module and
$\rho\colon\Gamma\to\End_R(V)$ a map. The $R$-submodule of \emph{equivariant functions} is
\[
  \mathcal F(\iota,U,\rho)=\{f\colon G\to V\;:\;f(\iota(\gamma)xu)=\rho(\gamma)f(x)\text{ for all }\gamma\in\Gamma,\ x\in G,\ u\in U\}.
\]
