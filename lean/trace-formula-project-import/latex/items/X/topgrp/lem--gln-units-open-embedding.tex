Let $n\ge0$ and let $F$ be a field with a topology in which addition and multiplication are continuous, inversion is continuous on $F^\times$, and points are closed. Give $\Mat_n(F)$ the product topology and $\GL_n(F)$ the units topology. Then the inclusion $\GL_n(F)\to\Mat_n(F)$ is an open embedding, and its image is $\{X\in\Mat_n(F):\det X\neq0\}$.
