Let $F$ be as in \cref{X.topgrp.thm:gl2-units-open-embedding}, assume that $F$ is second countable, and equip $F$
with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra. Give $\Mat_2(F)=F^{2\times2}$ the product $\sigma$-algebra and $\GL_2(F)$ the
$\sigma$-algebra induced by the inclusion $\GL_2(F)\to\Mat_2(F)$. Then this $\sigma$-algebra on $\GL_2(F)$ is the Borel
$\sigma$-algebra of the units topology.
