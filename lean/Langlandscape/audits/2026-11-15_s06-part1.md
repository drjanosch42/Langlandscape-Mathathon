## 2. Checklist

### (1) `NewOldData.split` + `tf_*` ⇔ splitting — OK
- `NewOldData` (Gorenstein.lean:65–81) has exactly the fields of the §0 block, in order; with `[IsDomain A]
  [IsNoetherianRing A] [IsLocalRing A]` (NewOld.lean:129) these are the hypotheses of `lem:s06-D` (table in s06-m1.tex
  l. 100–118).
- `NewOldData.split_iff` (Gorenstein.lean:126) is literally the §0 statement: `(∃ c ≠ 0, algebraMap A S c ∈ 𝔞 ⊔ 𝔟) ↔
  (𝔞 ⊔ 𝔟).map includeRight = ⊤` in `FractionRing A ⊗[A] S`, for any domain `A`, no further hypothesis. This is the second
  equivalence of `lem:s06-split`. The first one (CRT: `S_K → (S/𝔞)_K × (S/𝔟)_K` iso ⇔ `(𝔞+𝔟)S_K = S_K`, using `𝔞 ∩ 𝔟 = 0`
  and flatness) is not formalized; the docstring should say so (R7).
- Muse F26 (`A = ℤ_p`, `S = ℤ_p × ℤ/p`): excluded by the field `free` (free over a domain is torsion-free); `tf_a`, `tf_b`
  are what `ann_eq_of_inf_eq_bot` (NewOld.lean:43) uses. Without `tf_b` the conclusion `ann 𝔞 = 𝔟` fails
  (`S = A²`, `𝔞 = 0 × A`, `𝔟 = ϖA × 0`), so the fields are necessary and sufficient for (a). No negative Lean test; argued.
- Non-vacuity: `Toy.data` (Toy.lean:242), every `ϖ ≠ 0`, and `Toy.Zp.data p t` for every `t` (incl. `t = 0`).
