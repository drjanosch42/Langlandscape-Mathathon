## 6. Required fixes (prioritized, for S01-L16)

**R1 (L8a/L11a/L17/L13) — `hfin` has no discharge lemma for `T_ℓ`/`U_ℓ` at any standard level.**
`Brandt/Hecke.lean:303, 308, 314` (`Tl`, `Sl`, `Ul`), and every consumer: `Brandt/Corollaries.lean:355` (`Tl_adjoint`,
`hfin`, `hfin'`), `:606` (`Tl_selfAdjoint_U0`), `:789` (`Up_comm_Tl`), `Brandt/ImportDictionary.lean:278, 459`
(section variables), `Hyp/JLClassical.lean:59`.
Problem: the only proofs of `(U ⊓ conjSub U g).relIndex U ≠ 0` in the library are `Up_relIndex_ne_zero` (for
`ϖ_p` at `Utower`), `relIndex_ne_zero_of_normalizing`, `relIndex_ne_zero_of_commute` and
`relIndex_rightConj_ne_zero`. For `elemT` and `(elemT)⁻¹` there is none, not even at `Ô^×`. All tests of `Tl` take
`hfin` as a hypothesis, and the adjoint tests use `g = 1`. So `Tl`, `Ul`, `Tl_adjoint`, `Tl_selfAdjoint_U0`,
`Tl_selfAdjoint_units`, `Up_comm_Tl` and the content of `Hyp.JLClassical` cannot currently be instantiated.
Fix: add `relIndex_elemT_ne_zero` and `relIndex_elemT_inv_ne_zero` for `levelAt d N hN K` at a split prime
`ℓ ∤ N` (hence for `U0`, `U1`, `Utower`, `Ô^×`), and for `Ul` at `ℓ ∣ N` if feasible. Two routes: (i) local, as in
`Up_relIndex_ne_zero`: `GL2Zl ℓ ∩ ϖ GL2Zl ℓ ϖ⁻¹` contains an explicit congruence subgroup of finite index (note it
is the *upper*-triangular-mod-`ℓ` one for `ϖ = diag(ℓ,1)`, so `relIndex_U0` does not apply directly; for `ϖ⁻¹` it is
`K0 ℓ 1`); (ii) at `Ô^×`, from `dcSet_elemT_eq_normNSet` and the import's finiteness for `normNSet`. Then restate
`Tl_selfAdjoint_U0`, `Up_comm_Tl` and the tests without `hfin`, and either drop `∀ hfin` from `Hyp.JLClassical` or
keep it and cite the lemma in the docstring. Also discharge `hfin` in `Up_comm_Sl` (`:798`) with
`relIndex_ne_zero_of_commute` + `elemS_commute`, and `hfinV` in `Up_comm_rightTranslate` (`:829`) with
`relIndex_rightConj_ne_zero` + `Up_relIndex_ne_zero` (the test `RightTranslate.lean:63` already does this).
If route (i)/(ii) does not fit in L16's 45 minutes, record it in the coverage table as an open item with an owner;

**R2 (L4c/L10) — no public finiteness of `X_B` at the standard levels.**
`Quat/MassTower.lean:57–66` proves `Finite (XB (Utower …))` inline; nothing exports it. `Up_comm_push`
(`Brandt/Up.lean:291`), `UpHom`, `Tl_selfAdjoint_U0`, `normMap_heckeT` at these levels need it, and the test

**R3 (L11a) — `diamond` is not the tex's `⟨d⟩`, and its side condition is discharged only at `ℓ = p` on `Utower`.**
`Brandt/Hecke.lean:320`. The tex (and `mathematics/chapters/s01/4-hecke.tex:20`) defines `⟨d⟩` for
`d ∈ (ℤ/Mp^s)^×` with `δ_d` having lower-right entry `≡ d` at *all* primes of the level. Lean has a one-prime
operator for `t ∈ ℤ_ℓ^×`. The normalisation hypothesis `hn` is proved only for `ℓ = p` on `Utower`
(`deltaElt_normalizes`, `Quat/Stab.lean:351`, stated for the duplicate definition `deltaElt`), not for `ℓ ∣ M` nor for
`U0 d N`, `U1 d N`.
Fix (minimum): (a) say in the docstring and in the coverage table that `diamond` is the local factor at one prime and
that `⟨d⟩ = ∏_{ℓ ∣ Mp} diamond ℓ t_ℓ` is not assembled; (b) prove `diamondElt_normalizes` for `levelAt` with
`K = K0` and `K = K1` at any `ℓ ∣ N` (the local lemma `diagOne_conj_mem_K1` exists; a `K0` twin is needed), and
derive `deltaElt_normalizes` from it; (c) remove the duplication `deltaElt`/`deltaLoc` vs `diamondElt`
(`Quat/Stab.lean:301, 304` vs `Brandt/Hecke.lean:297`; they are definitionally equal) or add the `rfl` bridge lemma,
and relate `diamondAct` to `diamond` (`diamond_apply` and `diamondAct_mk` give the same formula).

**R4 (L17) — `Up_comm_diamond` covers only the case where the diamond is the identity.**
`Brandt/Corollaries.lean:807`. It assumes `ℓ ∤ M`, `ℓ ≠ p`, `ℓ ∤ D`. There `δ_t ∈ GL₂(ℤ_ℓ)` at `ℓ` lies in `U_s`, so
`⟨t⟩ = id` (my analysis, not a Lean statement) and the theorem says nothing. The tex clause of `lem:s01-Up`(e) is that
`U_p` commutes with `⟨d⟩`, `d ∈ (ℤ/Mp^s)^×`, i.e. with the diamonds at `ℓ ∣ M` and at `p`.
Fix: (a) `ℓ ∣ M`: drop `hℓM` from `Up_comm_heckeT` (`:779`) by using `splitAt_U1 hpN hp` when
`ℓ ∈ (M * p ^ s).primeFactors` and `splitAt_Utower_of_ne` otherwise; this also gives `U_p U_ℓ = U_ℓ U_p` for
`ℓ ∣ M`. (b) `ℓ = p`: a same-prime argument is needed (`δ_t ϖ_j δ_t⁻¹ = ϖ_{j'}` up to `K1 p s`); either prove it or
mark "diamond at `p`: not planned" in the coverage table. Until then the table's "`lem:s01-Up`(e): core" overstates
the Lean content for the diamond clause.
