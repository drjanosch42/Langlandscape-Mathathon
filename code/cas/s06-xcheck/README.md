## 1. Definitions implemented

Throughout, Hecke operators are the **classical** ones on q-expansions: for a prime ℓ ∤ N,
`T_ℓ(Σ a_n qⁿ) = Σ (a_{ℓn} + ℓ^{k-1} a_{n/ℓ}) qⁿ`, and for ℓ | N, `U_ℓ(Σ a_n qⁿ) = Σ a_{ℓn} qⁿ` (PARI `mfheckemat`). So a normalised
eigenform g has T_ℓ-eigenvalue a_ℓ(g), with |a_ℓ| ≤ 2ℓ^{(k-1)/2}. For a newform f of level N = qM with q ‖ N and trivial character,
a_q(f) = ε q^{(k-2)/2}, ε = ±1.

Integral Hecke algebras: `T_k(N)` is the ℤ-subalgebra of End(S_k(Γ₀(N))) generated by **all** T_n, n ≥ 1 (so it contains T_p for
p ∤ N, U_q and U_ℓ for ℓ | N). It is computed as the ℤ-span of T_1, …, T_B with B ≥ the Sturm bound; we check that the lattice
for n ≤ 2B is the same (flag `*_lattice_stable` in the JSONs). Realisation: t ↦ (a_1(t b_i))_i for the PARI basis b_i (route
"coef"; injective because (t, g) ↦ a_1(tg) is perfect, so it carries the ℤ-span isomorphically), or the flattened matrices
(route "mat", `mfheckemat`); both give identical results where both were run (H3 triples, T4 level 3 for k ≤ 44). In "coef",
t kills S^old iff a_1(t g) = 0 for g in a basis of S^old (S^old is T-stable), and λ_f(t) = x_f · v(t) with f = Σ x_i b_i.
For large spaces the integral HNF overflows, and we use the **ℤ_p route** (`xc_level_qp`): the same lattices ⊗ ℤ_p, as
L + p^e ℤ^d (anemic: after an injective coordinate projection), exact as long as v_p(index) < e (checked; e = 40 or 60). It
agrees with the integral route on (q,p,k,M) = (13,7,4,9), (13,7,4,1), (3,17,12,1), (3,17,28,1). Since S_k(Γ₀(N), ℂ) has a basis with integral q-expansion
and T_k(N) is determined as an abstract ring by the T_n, the basis used by PARI does not matter. T_k(N) ⊗ ℤ_p = ∏_𝔪 T_k(N)_𝔪.

For a newform orbit with coefficient field K = ℚ(f), the maximal ideals of T_k(N) ⊗ ℤ_p met by its eigensystems are given by the
primes 𝔓 | p of K; the pair (f, 𝔓) lies in 𝔪 iff a_ℓ(f) ≡ (the 𝔪-eigenvalue of T_ℓ) mod 𝔓 for all ℓ up to the Sturm bound. ord_𝔓 is the
𝔓-adic valuation, normalised to take value 1 on a uniformiser ϖ of K_𝔓; we record e(𝔓/p) and f(𝔓/p).

### T4 (row S06-C3, `prop:s06-betti`, `cor:s06-allweights`, stage tex H1)
- **𝔪 at p = 17.** k ∈ {12, 28, 44, 60, 124} (all ≡ 12 mod 16, the weight component of Δ). `T(1) := T_k(1)` on S_k(SL₂(ℤ)) and
  `𝔪 := (17, T_ℓ − τ(ℓ) for all primes ℓ, including ℓ = 17)`, the maximal ideal of the branch through Δ, residue field 𝔽₁₇.
  Since τ(17) ≡ 10 mod 17 is a unit, T_17 ∉ 𝔪, so every eigenform in 𝔪 is 17-ordinary; on T(1)_𝔪 the ordinary projector e is the
  identity ("localise at 𝔪, including T_17 ∉ 𝔪, and apply e", stage tex H1). We also count the eigenforms that satisfy the congruence only
  for ℓ ≠ 17 (ρ̄ ≅ ρ̄_Δ but possibly non-ordinary), to see whether the choice matters.
- **δ** `:= (q+1)² − q^{2−k} T_q²` at q = 3, with the classical T_3 above (so δ(g) = 16 − 3^{2−k} a_3(g)²). Because 3 ∈ ℤ₁₇^×,
  δ ∈ T(1) ⊗ ℤ₁₇ and (δ) = (16·3^{k−2} − T_3²) as ideals of T(1)_𝔪; we compute with the integral element `x := 16·3^{k−2} − T_3²`.
- **length T(1)_𝔪/(δ)** `:= length_{ℤ₁₇}`. T(1)_𝔪 is ℤ₁₇-free and δ is a non-zero-divisor (Ramanujan: δ(g) ≠ 0), so
  length = v₁₇(det(x | T(1)_𝔪)) = Σ_{(g,𝔓) ∈ 𝔪} f(𝔓/17)·ord_𝔓(δ(g)) (route A, eigenforms). Route B (independent): the Smith normal
  form (`matsnf`) of multiplication by x on the ℤ-lattice T(1); its 17-part is ⊕_{𝔪' ∋ 17} T(1)_{𝔪'}/(x), and the elementary divisors
  give the module structure. Route B must equal route A summed over all 𝔪' at 17; we report both and which 𝔪' contribute. The reported
  "ord₁₇" is this length (= ord_ϖ when T(1)_𝔪 = ℤ₁₇). By `prop:s06-betti`(a),(c) (rank-one case), the predicted
  ord_ϖ 𝔠₃(f) = ½ length coker(Ψ*Ψ)_𝔪 = length T(1)_𝔪/(δ); prediction 1, 1, 1, 1, 2 (`\stexp`, not gating).
- **t_{G_k}(3)** (stage tex H1, "Galois side, via ord_ϖ(a₃(G_k)² − 3^{k−2}·16) on the old branch"): G_k is the eigenform of
  S_k(SL₂(ℤ)) in 𝔪 (unique iff rk T(1)_𝔪 = 1), and `t_{G_k}(3) := ord_𝔓(a₃(G_k)² − 16·3^{k−2})`, computed from the q-expansion
  coefficient a₃ of the eigenform in its coefficient field (`mfeigenbasis`/`mfcoefs`), not from the Hecke matrix.
- **Extra, genuinely level-3 check.** For each 3-new newform f' of S_k(Γ₀(3)) in 𝔪_N (the maximal ideal generated by 𝔪 and
  U₃ − ε·3^{(k−2)/2}, `prop:s06-betti`(b)), we compute ord 𝔠₃(f') and t^{an}_{f'}(3) exactly as in H3 below (k where feasible).

### H3 (row S06-C7, `prop:s06-levM`, `rem:s06-minus1`)
M = 1, N = q, (q, p, k) ∈ {(13,7,4), (13,7,6), (19,5,4), (29,5,4)}; all have p | q+1, p ∤ φ(q), q ≢ 1 mod p.
- **𝔠_q(f)** (`def:s06-ctam`, fixed weight): `𝔟_k := ker(T_k(N) → End(S^old))`, S^old := span{g(z), g(qz) : g ∈ S_k(Γ₀(M))}
  (cusp forms only), with T_k(N) containing **U_q** (and T_p). Then `𝔠_q(f) := λ_f(𝔟_k)·O_𝔓`, λ_f(T_n) = a_n(f), for every q-new
  newform f and every 𝔓 | p of ℚ(f). Localising first changes nothing: 𝔟_k ⊗ ℤ_p = ∏ 𝔟_{k,𝔪} and λ_f kills the other factors.
  Computed as: ℤ-basis of T_k(N) (as above), 𝔟_k = integral kernel (`matkerint`) of the restriction map to S^old, then
  min ord_𝔓 λ_f over a ℤ-basis of 𝔟_k.
- **t_f(q)** (`def:s06-tam`, `lem:s06-tam`(b), = [KO, Def. 2.12]): the largest t with ρ_f mod ϖ^t unramified at q. This Galois
  quantity is not directly computable from q-expansions. We compute, **without U_q**, the trace-congruence (anemic level-lowering)
  exponent `t^{an}_f(q) := ord_𝔓 λ_f(𝔟^{an})`, `𝔟^{an} := ker(T^{an}_k(N) → End(S^old))`, where T^{an}_k(N) is the ℤ-algebra generated
  by the T_ℓ, ℓ ∤ Np (= ℤ-span of the T_n with (n, Np) = 1). So t^{an} is the largest t such that the Frobenius traces a_ℓ(f) mod ϖ^t,
  ℓ ∤ Np, are those of a level-M eigensystem. Relations: (i) ord 𝔠_q(f) ≤ t^{an} (as 𝔟^{an} ⊆ 𝔟_k); (ii) t^{an} ≤ t_f(q) (Carayol's
  lemma: matching traces mod ϖ^t with the level-M Galois representation, unramified at q, gives ρ_f mod ϖ^t unramified at q; needs ρ̄
  absolutely irreducible); (iii) t_f(q) ≤ t^{an} is an R = T statement at level M, minimal at q ([KO, Thm 3.14], steps (3)–(4) of
  `prop:s06-levM`). So `ord_p 𝔠_q(f) − t^{an}_f(q)` (always ≤ 0) tests step (5) (the U_q-matching) of `prop:s06-levM`; the prediction
  is 0.

## 2. Doubts and ambiguities (stated, not smoothed over)
1. **H3 as specified is vacuous at M = 1.** S_k(SL₂(ℤ)) = 0 for k ∈ {4, 6}. Hence S^old = 0, T''_k = 0, 𝔟_k = T_k(q), 𝔠_q(f) = (1)
   and 𝔟^{an} = T^{an}, t^{an} = 0, for every f and 𝔪. The Galois t_f(q) is also 0 for every non-Eisenstein 𝔪: ρ̄_f unramified at q
   would be an irreducible mod-p representation of level 1, which does not exist for p ∈ {5, 7} (Serre's conjecture
   [Khare–Wintenberger] and S_{k'}(SL₂(ℤ)) = 0 for k' ≤ p+1 ≤ 8; or Ribet's level lowering, k ≤ p+1). So the difference is 0 for a
   trivial reason and **does not test `prop:s06-levM`**. Moreover, for q ≡ −1 mod p and even k, E_k satisfies the level-raising
   condition a_q(E_k)² = (1+q^{k−1})² ≡ 0 ≡ (q+1)² q^{k−2} mod p, so q-new forms congruent to E_k mod p are expected; those 𝔪 are
   Eisenstein and outside the hypotheses ((H2) fails, t_f(q) is lattice-dependent). We list them separately. A non-vacuous M = 1 test
   needs S_k(1) ≠ 0 and k ≤ p−1, i.e. k ≥ 12 and p ≥ 13, p | q+1 and a_q(g) ≡ 0 mod 𝔓 for a level-1 g; for g = Δ, p = 13 the
   smallest such q is 1741 (next 2963), and for p = 17 (k=12 ≤ 16) q = 2311 — far beyond a quick PARI run at weight 12.
   **Supplement (added on request):** non-vacuous q ≡ −1 cases at M > 1 (`H3M_CASES`, `supplement_M_gt_1` in H3.json); §3.
2. **t_{G_k}(3) is identical to δ(G_k) up to a unit.** ord_𝔓(a₃² − 16·3^{k−2}) = ord_𝔓(δ(G_k)) since 3^{k−2} is a unit. So when
   rk T(1)_𝔪 = 1 the T4 "independent" t_{G_k}(3) agrees with length T(1)_𝔪/(δ) by algebra; the independence is in the route
   (eigenform coefficient vs. Smith form on the Hecke lattice). It is also a level-1 (old-branch) quantity, not t_{f'}(3) of
   `def:s06-tam` for the 3-new form f'. The level-3 extra check (ord 𝔠₃(f') and t^{an}_{f'}(3) at level 3) is the genuinely different
   route.
3. **t_f(q) is replaced by t^{an}_f(q)** (Section 1); equality with the Galois t_f(q) is (Lev_M)/R = T, i.e. part of what is being
   tested. A purely Galois computation (e.g. of the Tate-type parameter at q) was not attempted.
4. **Which T_ℓ define 𝔪 at 17.** We include T_17 (ordinary 𝔪); the count of forms congruent to Δ only away from 17 is reported.
5. **Normalisation of T_q.** Classical (above). If the Python toolkit uses the unitary normalisation q^{(2−k)/2}T_q, then
   δ = (q+1)² − (that)², the same element; if it uses q^{2−k}T_q² with a different T_q (e.g. the "T'_q/T''_q" of stage 03 D4) the
   product T'_qT''_q = q^{2−k}T_q² is the same, so δ is normalisation-independent as long as T'_qT''_q = q^{2−k}T_q².
6. **At q ≡ −1 a result t = 1 is automatic.** Level raising forces a_q(g) ≡ 0 mod 𝔓, so the two old U_q-roots are ≡ +q^{(k−2)/2}
   and −q^{(k−2)/2} (distinct mod p). Whatever the sign of a_q(f) = ±q^{(k−2)/2}, one root matches it, so ord 𝔠 ≥ 1 whenever
   t^{an} ≥ 1, and then ord 𝔠 ≤ t^{an} = 1 forces difference 0. Only rows with t^{an} ≥ 2 test step (5) of `prop:s06-levM` (the
   U_q-matching) beyond the residual level.
7. **The Betti cokernel is not computed.** Stage tex H1 asks for e·coker(Ψ*Ψ)_𝔪 ≅ (T(1)_𝔪/δ)²; this cross-check computes only
   the Hecke side, length T(1)_𝔪/(δ) (and the level-3 ord 𝔠₃, t^{an}), not the cokernel itself (that is S06-H1, HACS). The relation
   ord 𝔠₃ = length T(1)_𝔪/(δ) is tested independently only by the level-3 column (one 3-new form per k).
