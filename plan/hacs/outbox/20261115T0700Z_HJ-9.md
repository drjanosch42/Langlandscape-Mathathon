## What

- **`quat/Ordinary.{h,cpp}`** is new. `Level.*` is untouched; only its public API is used: `LevelModel::move`, `LevelSet`, `u0q_correspondence` and `project`.
  - **U_p** at the level prime (O1). It is homological, and sums over the p lines λ ≠ ⟨v mod p⟩ of eI_c/p.
    - J_λ = Ow + pI_c, written as J_λ = I_{c′}μ/N_{c′}.
    - The datum moves by v ↦ v·conj(μ)/N_c (Level (L4)), on the U₁ vector and on the U₀(2) line alike.
    - It works at any class number (H7: B₁₁, h = 2; B₂₃, h = 3).
  - **U′_p** (tower.py's [[1,0],[p^s j, p]]), in lattice form:
    - J = Ov + pI_c, with datum (v − p^s j r₁)/p at p, moved by conj(μ)/(pN_c);
    - at the primes of M the datum moves as for U_p.
    - **<p>_M** (v ↦ p⁻¹v mod M) and **Δ_s** = ⟨1+p⟩ (v ↦ (1+p)⁻¹v mod p^s).
  - **e** by image stabilisation of U_p^k mod 5³ (proj.py), with U^k built by sparse×dense products.
  - **Ranks over ℤ/p^m[Δ]:**
    - directly: g = r − rk_p((D−1)A_J);
    - by the **coinvariant route** (O4), exact: Δ_s acts freely, so M is free over the local ring ℤ/p^m[Δ_s], eM is a direct summand and hence free, and g is the ordinary rank of U_p on F_p[S_s/Δ_s].
- **`tools/jl_hj9.cpp`** has the suites T3, H6 and H7.
- **`hacs_bridge/laneC_hj9_compare.py`** writes `code/results/laneC/hj9_lambda.json`. It compares only and recomputes nothing.

## Deviations

1. At s = 4, 5 the full e on ℤ/5³[S_s] is not formed, which would be dense and 31 250²; the rank uses the exact coinvariant route. The H₁ Δ-rank at s ≥ 4 comes from the Ribet identity, not from a direct restriction.
2. `jl_hj9` builds its own `LevelModel` mod 2·5^s for E_s, instead of `Tower`, because the edges need the U₀(2) line. Its diamonds are `Ordinary::diamond_perm`, which is the same convention as `Tower::diamond`.
