## What

- **`quat/TreeK.{h,cpp}`: the weight-k two-term complexes on BG(U, q) over Z/p^m.**
  - **Local model at p.**
    - ι_p is right multiplication on eO/p^m, in the row convention.
    - The class frames are a_c ∈ I_c with p ∤ nrd(a_c)/N_c, so that I_c ⊗ Z_p = O_p a_c.
    - The transports are u₀ = φ_c(γ₀) and u₁ = ι_p(a_{c′}γ₁g a_c⁻¹), with g = μ/(qN_{c′}). The data come from HJ-7a's `EdgeMove`.
    - H_x = φ_c(Γ_x).
    - Checked: u_j H_e u_j⁻¹ ⊂ H_{s_j e} in every cell (`conj_ok`).
  - **Coefficients** sit behind `CoeffModel`:
    - `SymCoeff`: Sym^{k−2} with (g.P)(X,Y) = P((X,Y)g), as twoterm.sym = [C] def:rhon;
    - `ShapiroSymCoeff`: ihara.py's Sym^n ⊗ Z[(Z/p)²−0].
  - **Maps:**
    - d = (−s_{0\*}, s_{1\*}) with e_H u_j (homological);
    - α with the traces [H_x:H_e] (cohomological);
    - δ = s₁^\*f₁ − s₀^\*f₀;
    - the Ihara map ι = −δ = (s₀^\*, −s₁^\*);
    - Smith form mod p^m.
  - The interim ρ_n was kept. It is cross-checked against BrandtSym (below) instead of being replaced, because BrandtSym lives on the maximal order only, while the tree needs Eichler-level edges and level-U vertices.

**3. NEW, the quaternionic Ihara cokernel (v6 Task 2(b)).**
- **Cells:** coker(ι : M(O;V_k)² → M(O₀(q);V_k)) for D ∈ {2,3,5,7,11,13}, q ∈ {2,3,5,7,11,13}∖{D}, even k = 2..14. That is 210 cells.
- **Free rank:** equal to dim S_k(Γ₀(Dq))^new (PARI `mfdim`, independent) in **210/210** cells.
- **ker ι:** rank 1 at k = 2 (the diagonal constants) and 0 for k > 2.
- **Task 2's range l > k:** there is **no torsion at any prime k < l ≤ 2000 with l ∤ 6Dq, in all 210 cells.** This took 62930 prime scans in total, at the scan primes 5 ≤ l ≤ 2000.
- **Outside that range, l ≤ k:** there is torsion **Z/l, exponent 1**, in 68 cells, only at (k, l) ∈ {(8,5), (10,7), (14,5), (14,11)}.
  - Every one of these has (l+1) | (k−2). This is an observation, not a proof.
  - It is checked independently at one overlap: H_Vk (D=2, q=3, p=5) has Hco1 = [4, 1] at k = 8.
- **Method:**
  - The rank of ι mod l is compared with the rank over Q (mod 1000003).
  - Where it drops, the elementary divisors are taken mod l^m with l^m < 10¹². No divisor reached l^m.
  - The integrality at l is exact for l ∤ 6Dq, because the frames lie in GL₂(Z_l).

**4. NEW, W_D as an integral matrix (v6 Task 2(c)).**
- **Definition:** W_D = Π_D / D^{(k−2)/2} on HJ-4a's intrinsic lattice M_Z (BrandtSym (C4)/(C5), column convention).
- **Cells:** D ∈ {2,3,5,7,11,13}, even k ≤ 14. There are 42 cells, 38 of them of positive rank.
- **Checks:** in all 38 cells it is integral, an involution (W² = 1), commutes with T_ℓ, and is self-adjoint for ⟨,⟩_w.
- **Output:** the matrices, traces and ±1 multiplicities are in `W_D_NEW`.
- **Examples:**
  - D = 11: W = +I at k = 2 and −I at k = 4.
  - At higher k it is a non-diagonal integral involution in the HNF basis, for example at D = 13, k = 4.

## Conventions (flagged; amalgam.py's where it has one)

- **Ihara sign:** α(x, y) = dg₁x − dg_q y (amalgam.py), here ι = (s₀^\*, −s₁^\*). The transport κ is realised by u₁ (moving back along the edge).
- **Weights:** e_H W models both W_H and W^H, since |H| is a unit for l ≥ 5. Cohomological traces use [H_x:H_e], with full |Γ_x| (including −1).
- **No ambiguity remained:** every compared cell matches. The only free choice is the frame of ι_p, which is unique up to GL₂(Z_p)-conjugation by Skolem–Noether; that choice cannot change module structures.

## Addendum (06:40Z): exact integral Ihara cokernel over Z

- **What.** Suite `ihara_exact` in `tools/jl_tree_k.cpp` (code fde0c29d). It does an exact BigInt Smith form of the integral two-term map
  ι = (s₀^\*, −s₁^\*) : ⊕_x Λ_x^{Γ_x} ⊕ ⊕_x Λ_x^{Γ_x} → ⊕_e Λ_{c(e)}^{Γ_e}, replacing the prime scan.
  - The cells are the same 210: D ∈ {2,3,5,7,11,13}, q ∈ {2,…,13}∖{D}, even k = 2..14.
  - **All 210 cells fit.** The slowest takes 0.25 s; the whole suite takes 5 s.
  - Results are `ihara_exact_NEW` in `code/results/laneC/hj7_ihara_k.json`. Each cell gives "coker ι ≅ ℤ^r ⊕ ⊕ ℤ/d_i" (computed), the invariant factors, their primary parts, and the parts at 2, 3, D and q.
- **Lattice convention.**
  - The coefficient lattice is HJ-4a's intrinsic Λ_c (BrandtSym (C5)), on vertices and on edges. It is Sym^n(ℤ_ℓ²) at every ℓ ∤ D (ℓ = 2, 3 included), and the (O⁰)^# choice at ℓ | D.
  - The edge module is Λ_c^{Γ_e}, where c is the class of the edge representative and Γ_e its stabiliser. It is computed as a saturated integer kernel.
  - **Gross form:** (s₀^\*f)(e) = ρ(γ₀⁻¹)f(x) and (s₁^\*f)(e) = ρ(β⁻¹)f(x′), with β⁻¹ = conj(μ)x₁/(N_c N_{c′}).
  - The s₁^\* block was integral without a q-scaling: the exponent a is 0 in all 210 cells. The q-part is still reported apart, because V is not q-integral for the S-arithmetic group.
- **Checks (210/210 each):**
  - The ℚ-rank equals the scan's rank.
  - The free rank equals dim S_k(Γ₀(Dq))^new (PARI `mfdim`).
  - On the scan range (5 ≤ ℓ ≤ 2000, ℓ ∤ Dq), the ℓ-primary parts equal the prime scan's. This includes the absence of torsion for every ℓ > k.
  - The coordinates are exact, and the only denominators are powers of q.
- **Numbers.**
  - 52 cells are torsion-free.
  - Torsion prime to 6Dq occurs in 68 cells, only at (k, ℓ) ∈ {(8,5), (10,7), (14,5), (14,11)}, as in the scan.
  - The remaining torsion is at 2, 3 and D. For example, B₁₁ with q = 2:
    - k = 4: ℤ³ ⊕ ℤ/11;
    - k = 8: ℤ⁵ ⊕ (ℤ/2)³ ⊕ ℤ/660;
    - k = 14: ℤ⁹ ⊕ (ℤ/2)² ⊕ (ℤ/4)³ ⊕ (ℤ/8)² ⊕ ℤ/48 ⊕ ℤ/720.
  - The 2- and 3-parts are canonical for Sym^n(ℤ_ℓ²). The D-part depends on the lattice choice at D.
- **Not fitting:** none.
