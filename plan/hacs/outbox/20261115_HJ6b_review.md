# HJ-6b review: definite congruence modules (Lane B)

Target: merge `216f8064` on `impl/hacs-laneB` (`git diff 216f8064^1 216f8064`). This was a short, targeted review and not a
HACS certification. The reviewer read the code and recomputed the results independently in sympy and cypari2, using
throwaway scripts that are not committed. Every number below is *computed*.

## Verdict

**Accept.** No critical or major findings. The exact linear algebra is correct. The independent recomputation agrees with
`congruence_k.json` on every cell checked. The 37 at D = 13, k = 8 is a genuine congruence, confirmed on the GL_2 side.
One stated claim is incomplete (m1). The remaining findings are minor hygiene.

## 1. Mathematical correctness (JlCongruence.cpp)

- **Echelon** (`JlCongruence.cpp:44-77`). The row step is (x, y) -> (s x + t y, (a/g) y - (b/g) x), which has determinant
  (sa + tb)/g = 1, so it is unimodular. Pivots are made positive, and the reduction above each pivot uses floor division into
  [0, p). The result is the canonical HNF.
- **KernelZ** (`:267-278`). It echelons [A^T | I] by unimodular row operations. The rows that vanish on the A^T part are a
  basis of ker_Z A, and that basis is **saturated** because it is part of a unimodular matrix. So L_i = L ∩ ker f_i(θ) is
  saturated, as required. The column convention (v -> F v) matches the records' `T_p b_j = Σ_i F_ij b_i`.
- **C_i** (`:511-525`). This is Smith(rows of Ki and Kc) on Z^h, with full rank checked (`s.size() == h`). So
  C_i = Z^h/(L_i ⊕ L_i^c) exactly. L_i^c = ker (χ/f_i)(θ) is the right complement, because χ_θ is squarefree and the f_i
  are pairwise coprime.
- **Smith** (`:280-324`). This is the standard minimal-pivot algorithm, with the divisibility fix (adding a row to row t)
  and abs on output. It terminates because |pivot| strictly decreases. Truncated division is harmless there.
- **CharpolyZ.** Faddeev–LeVerrier with M_0 = 0 and the exactness of division by k ENGINE_CHECKed. Correct.
- **Overflow and sign handling.** All matrix arithmetic uses `nf::BigInt`. The uint32 narrowings in the reader are now
  ENGINE_CHECKed (m4 fix). The FactorTrial bound is sound: p_max = 1048573, and a cofactor below 2^40 left after trial
  division is prime because no primes lie in (p_max, 2^20]. No issue found.
- **θ.** If χ_θ is squarefree, then the commutant of θ is Q[θ] ⊇ T ⊗ Q ∋ θ, so the orbits are exactly the Galois orbits
  of eigensystems. Sound.
- **Ring module** (`:537-567`). The Z-algebra T = Z[T_p : p ∈ ops] is the closure of Z·1 under left multiplication by the
  generators (`:451-462`). This is the generated algebra because T is commutative, and the loop terminates because Z^{h²}
  is Noetherian. T_i is the Z-span of the restrictions to L_i, which is stable because T commutes with θ. The Smith form of
  the coordinates of (t|L_i, t|L_i^c) in Bi ⊕ Bc gives [T_i × T_i^c : T]. The definition is sensible: it is an invariant of
  the abstract ring T ⊂ End_Q(V), and so independent of the lattice, as the code claims. It **does depend on the ops
  list**, though. At k > 2 the ops exclude p = D, and T is far from saturated (hecke index 2^3·13^6 at D = 13, k = 8), so
  the large D-powers in `ring_cong` (for example 13^15 at D = 13, k = 12) mostly measure that non-saturation. They should
  not be read as congruences (m2).
- **Gram.** The self-adjointness test F_ij w_j = F_ji w_i is exactly the condition F^T W^{-1} = W^{-1} F for
  ⟨x, y⟩ = Σ x_c y_c / w_c. Correct.

## 3. Plausibility of 37 at D = 13, k = 8

- **Resultants.** The GL_2 T_2 charpoly on S_8(Γ0(13))^new, from `symn.json`, equals (x−10)(x²+19x+6)(quartic) exactly.
  The pairwise resultants over Z are:
  - Res(x−10, x²+19x+6) = 296 = **2³·37**;
  - Res(x−10, quartic) = 13520 = 2⁴·5·13²;
  - Res(x²+19x+6, quartic) = 911210144 = 2⁵·13⁴·997.

  So 37 divides the resultant of exactly the two orbits that C links, and no other pair. The collector's gcd over
  p ∈ {2, 3} agrees: 2⁴·5·13²·37 for orbit deg 1 and 2⁸·13⁴·37 for orbit deg 2.
- **Atkin–Lehner signs.** Method: PARI through cypari2, using `mfinit([13,8],0)`, `mfsplit` and
  `mfatkineigenvalues(mf,13)`, cross-checked by a_13 = −w_13·13^{k/2−1}. The PARI orbits have a_2 = 10 for deg 1 and
  a_2 = y−10 with y² − y − 84 for deg 2; the latter has charpoly x²+19x+6. The signs are:
  - deg 1 (x−10): w_13 = −1 (a_13 = +2197);
  - deg 2 (x²+19x+6): w_13 = −1 (a_13 = +2197);
  - deg 4 (quartic): w_13 = +1 (a_13 = −2197).

  So the 37-congruence is between **equal** signs (−1, −1). This is forced: a congruence mod ℓ between opposite signs
  would need a_13 ≡ −a_13, so ℓ | 2·13³.
- **Record side.** The record lattice itself splits as a T-stable coordinate block sum {2,3,6} ⊕ {0,1,4,5}. T_2 on the
  first block has charpoly (x−10)(x²+19x+6), and on the second the quartic. These blocks are exactly the w_13 = −1 and
  w_13 = +1 parts, and the 37 lives inside the −1 block. This record-side identification uses the block charpolys plus the
  PARI signs; w_D was not computed directly on the class set.
- Conclusion: 37 is a plausible and GL_2-confirmed congruence prime between these newform orbits. Since C ≠ 0 forces an
  eigen-congruence on any T-stable lattice, the prime 37 does not depend on the lattice choice. Its exponent does.

## Findings

No crit or major findings.

- **m1 (claim wording).** The claim "ring-module primes ⊂ {2, 3, D}, plus 7 at D = 13, k = 12" is incomplete.
  `congruence_k.json` also has **37** in `ring_cong` at D = 13, k = 8 (2·13²·37 and 2²·13⁴·37). At k = 2 the ring primes are
  the Mazur primes (5, 7, 11, …), as expected, so the claim must be restricted to k > 2. Corrected statement: at k > 2,
  the ring-module primes lie in {2, 3, D} ∪ {37 at (13, 8)} ∪ {7 at (13, 12)}.
- **m2 (interpretation, `JlCongruence.h:15-16`, the CHANGES.md HJ-6b row).** `ring_cong` depends on the ops list (no T_D
  at k > 2), and the large D- and 2-powers reflect the non-saturation of Z[ops]. Say so next to "lattice-independent".
  Separately, the CHANGES row says "at k = 2 the ring congruence module", but the code computes it at every k
  (`JlCongruence.cpp:537`). Fix the wording.
- **m3 (`JlCongruenceValidation.cpp:155`).** The Mazur gate compares only the prime-to-6 parts. The exact equality,
  including the 2- and 3-parts, holds in the data but is not enforced. Consider adding an exact-equality counter so the
  stronger claim is gated.
- **m4 (`JlCongruence.cpp:500, 527, 536`).** `bad` is cell-cumulative. After one bad orbit, later orbits silently skip
  `tp_on_Li` and `ring_cong`. This is harmless now (bad_decomposition = 0), but it should be per orbit.
- **m5 (`JlCongruence.cpp:604`).** `f` and `f_text` are null for deg > 6, leaving only an FNV key. The k = 12 orbits of
  deg 5 and 6 are fine, but higher-degree orbits lose a human-readable key.
- **m6 (`laneB_collect.py:153-169`).** The provenance has the binary sha but no hash or commit of the input records.
  Lane A's `f54b109e` appears only inside the path. Add the sha256 of `index.json`, or a records digest.
  `json.load(open(...))` at `:140` leaves the file unclosed (cosmetic).
