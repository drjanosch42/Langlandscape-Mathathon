## How the GL₂ side is obtained (all *computed*)
1. `descent(N, kk−2, P).module_Z()` for N = q and N = 1 (Q4: allowed): exact integer T_ℓ on L_N = H¹(Γ₀(N); Symⁿℤ²)/tors in HACS's basis (record words: rule `iso_dixon/v3`, saturation `det_pm1`). Primes ℓ ≤ Sturm+1 (and p), split into batches (≤ 6 primes ≤ 97, sum ≤ 100), one HACS process per batch; `basis_fnv` asserted equal across batches.
2. `level_n(q, kk−2, ℓ, [q])` (ℓ ∉ torsion, two ℓ, must agree): U_q charpoly mod ℓ → number of new forms with a_q = ±q^{(kk−2)/2}; signs distributed over the Galois orbits by subset sum. If several assignments fit, every one is computed; the cell is `computed` only when all agree (`sign_robust`).
3. `hecke_structure(q, kk−2, "full")`: its words (C1, components, G2/G4 at ℓ = p, G3.C_T*, S_cand) are copied into each cell as `record_words`. At the T5 levels the record does not certify the maximal ideals at p (C1 `open(cap)` at q = 13; p ∉ S_cand elsewhere), and the console exports no integral U_q matrix (the U_p matrix files are an owner-gated dump), so the defect numbers come from step 4.
4. The full algebra S = ℤ[T_ℓ, U_q] is rebuilt from eigenvalue data: S ⊂ ∏_s K_s over the systems s. New orbit: T_ℓ = p_{ℓ,s}(x) from a Krylov basis of ker φ_s(A) (A generic, checked on the whole kernel), U_q = ε_s q^{(kk−2)/2}. Old orbit g: T_ℓ, a_q(g) from L_1 (checked equal at level q), U_q = y, y² − a_q(g)y + q^{kk−1} = 0. **Local route** (default): the places v of degree 1 in m, λ_v(gens) mod p^E (E = 40, PARI p-adics), R = ℤ[λ(gens)] + p^E ℤ^r; R ⊗ ℤ_p = S_m exactly because p^{E−1}ℤ^r ⊂ R is checked (Nakayama). Then the S06-C4 code (`defect.congruence.defect`, Character, Gorenstein test) runs unchanged on R. Two other routes are kept: the S06-C4 matrix closure in End(⊕Λ_s), and the global order ℤ[gens]·1 ⊂ ∏ K_s. Tests: local = matrix closure and global order = matrix closure on a toy old/new congruence (`test_local_route_matches_matrix_algebra_toy`, `test_abstract_hecke_matches_matrix_algebra_toy`); on real cells the local route reproduces S06-C4 at weight k (7/7) and at (5,18,23)@40, (5,16,31)@46. **The two new cells (13,18,19)@36 and (13,12,31)@42 rest on the local route alone** (the other two routes do not finish there).

## Deviations
- D1. U_q is not read from HACS as an operator (no console export); it enters through the U_q charpolys of `level_n` (signs) and the Eichler–Shimura relation on old forms (a_q(g) from HACS). The HeckeZ record is quoted, not used for the numbers.
- D2. T_p is dropped from the generators when p > 97 (HACS cap), i.e. T_101 at (13,16,101). Generation by Sturm is unaffected (101 > Sturm bound 18).
- D3. Weights k + p − 1 with kk − 2 > 60 ((3,18,53)@70, (3,18,61)@78, (13,16,101)@116) are outside descent's bound: `open` with the reason. Python S06-C4 has 70, 78.
- D4. The local route needs every place of m to have e = f = 1; otherwise the cell is refused (`open`), never approximated.
- D5. η^B at weight k: Python S06-C5 (gating) and native BrandtSym (cross-check, equal). At k + p − 1 the Gram is not η^B (p ≤ n); the ring-theoretic η^B of worker C's native file is used instead (rank-1 block, so the congruence ideal is (1) without any pairing).
- D8. The result file is a splice: six triples from the run at 13bd624b, the (5,18,23) row from the rerun at 28961d73 (the first run refused (5,18,23)@40 because the local route rejected a ramified place without checking whether it lies in m; the fix refuses only when its residues match m).
