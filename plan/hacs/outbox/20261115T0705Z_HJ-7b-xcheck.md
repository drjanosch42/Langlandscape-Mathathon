# HJ-7b cross-check (Lane C, worker A): exact Ihara cokernel by a second route

- **What.** `code/hacs/src/hacs/Registry/JL/quat/tools/jl_ihara_xcheck.cpp`. It computes
  coker(s0^*, −s1^*) : M_Z(O^×)² → M_Z(O₀(q)) through the HJ-4b `BrandtLevel` Eichler API:
  - s0^* = `pullback` and s1^* = `degeneracy_s1` = ρ_n(g)Φ(I_c′, d·g), with g = conj(μ)/N_c;
  - the cokernel by an exact ℤ Smith form (`zlin::smith`).

  It shares no code with TreeK's local ι_p frames. Common ground is only the class set, the splitting s and the intrinsic
  lattice (BrandtSym (C5)).
- **Result** (*computed*): `code/results/laneC/hj7_ihara_xcheck.json`. On all **210/210** cells of `hj7_ihara_k.json`
  (D ∈ {2,3,5,7,11,13}, q ≤ 13, q ≠ D, even k ≤ 14):
  - the ranks of M(O) and M(O₀(q)) agree;
  - the free rank agrees;
  - the full list of torsion invariant factors agrees **exactly**. That includes the parts at D (the (O⁰)^# choice) and
    at q, so there was nothing to explain at D or q.

  Runtime 6.3 s single-threaded.
- **Normalisation note.** TreeK writes s1^* = ρ(β⁻¹), with β⁻¹ = conj(μ)x₁/(N_c N_c′). This is the same element as our
  g·γ⁻¹. The agreement at q confirms that no q-power separates the two conventions.
