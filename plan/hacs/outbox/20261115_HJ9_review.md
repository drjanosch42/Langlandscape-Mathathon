## 2. The coinvariant route (O4) is sound

Argument, with R = ℤ/p^m[Δ_s], which is local because Δ_s is a p-group:
- If Δ acts freely on S, then M = ℤ/p^m[S] ≅ R^{|S/Δ|} is free.
- If U_p commutes with Δ, then e (a limit of powers of U_p) is an R-linear idempotent. So eM is a direct summand of M. It is therefore finitely generated projective over a local ring, hence free, say eM ≅ R^g.
- Tensor with R/(p, D−1) = 𝔽_p. Direct summands commute with ⊗, so g = dim e(M/(p, D−1)M) = dim ē·𝔽_p[S/Δ].
- ē is the reduction of e, which is the ordinary projector of the induced Ū_p. So g is the ordinary rank of Ū_p on the coinvariants. **The route is exact, not a bound.**

The two hypotheses are exactly the ones named in the task: **free action** and **[U_p, Δ] = 0**. Freeness of eM then follows; it is not an extra hypothesis.

- **Minor m2 (hypothesis [U_p, Δ] = 0 not checked in code).** `coinvariant_rank` (Ordinary.cpp:484) builds Ū from one representative column per orbit. This is well defined only if U_p commutes with Δ. jl_hj9.cpp:237-238 checks α∘U_E = U_S∘α and α∘D_E = D_S∘α, but **not** U_S D_S = D_S U_S or U_E D_E = D_E U_E.
  - I checked it independently with a separate build, `/home/janosch/data/hacs-build/laneC/review9/commute_check`: commute_S = commute_E = true for s = 1..4.
  - It is also a theorem (diamonds commute with U_p). Fix: add the cheap sparse check to the T3 output.
- **Minor m3 (freeness test is weaker than stated).** Ordinary.cpp:476 sets `delta_free` from "all orbits have equal length". It does not test "length = |Δ|", so a uniform nontrivial stabiliser would pass.
  - The numbers are fine: orbits_S · |Δ| = 2·1, 10·5, 50·25 and (my rerun) 250·125 = |S_s|, and likewise for E. The minimum orbit length equals |Δ| in commute_check.
  - Fix: test `first == order` or `orbits·order == n`.
- **Minor m4 (H₁ at s ≥ 4 rests on more than the identity).** jl_hj9.cpp:252 takes rk_Δ eH₁ = rk eℤ[E] − 2 rk eℤ[S]. This needs 0 → eH₁ → eℤ[E] → eℤ[S]² → 0 to be exact over R:

  - The free terms make the sequence split, so eH₁ is free of rank 4 − 2 = 2.

## 3. The U_p convention agrees with HJ-4b

- BrandtLevel.h (B3) on `impl/hacs-laneC` is the classical U_p: the sum over the ℓ lines ≠ the datum line, giving a_n ↦ a_{np}, in functional form.
- Ordinary.h (O1) uses the same lines, in homological form. Its matrix is the transpose of the functional one, so the Hecke operator and its eigenvalues are the same.
- Diamonds: (O2) v ↦ d⁻¹v on points, and (B4) Φ(L, d⁻¹d_L) on functions. These are dual, and both are "classical <d>⁻¹ = adelic <d>".
- Transposing the homological identity U_pᵀ = <p>_M U′_p gives the functional identity U_p^* = <p>_M U′_p, with the same sign. hj4b_level.json records exactly `U_ell_adjoint: "<p>_M U'_p"`, and H6 confirms the other sign is false. **Consistent.**
