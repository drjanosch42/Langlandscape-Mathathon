## Method (extension of HJ-8a)
For a definite block m^B with residues a_ℓ mod p (its `m_id`), the GL₂ side keeps every HACS system that can meet those residues, adds the T_ℓ of m_id to the generators, and computes, by the HJ-8a local route, every GL₂ maximal ideal m with those residues (a definite block may split on the GL₂ side by U_q or T_ℓ, ℓ > 11). Per block it checks:
1. rank m^B = Σ rank S'_m over those m (JL);
2. multiset {v_p η^B over the orbits} = multiset {ord η_new over the new places} (JL + η^B = η(S'_m), `if:s04-etaB`);
3. Thm E per form (ord η = ord 𝔠 + ord η_new);
4. ord η − ord 𝔠 multiset = {v_p η^B} (the identity η = 𝔠·η^B), and per form when the block has one orbit;
5. where a level-one form g of weight k exists (dim S_k(1) = 1): ord 𝔠 vs t_f(q) = v_p(a_q(g)² − q^{k−2}(q+1)²), a_q(g) from HACS.
Blocks whose residue field is F_{p^f}, f > 1 (H3): S_m = O_v when exactly one unramified new place of degree f = rank carries the block's residual polynomials and no old system does (Nakayama), so η = 𝔠 = η_new = (1); otherwise open.
