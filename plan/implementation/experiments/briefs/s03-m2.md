## 2. What "full proof" means here

- `prop:s03-phieis`: state it as in the stage tex (any prime `q`, `U = U_0(M)`, `ell ∤ qM`, homological Brandt convention
  `[x] ↦ Σ_i [x g_i]`), then prove steps (1)–(4) so that a referee accepts every step. In particular step (1) (the
  stabiliser of a neighbour `J` of `I`; finite order of `gamma` in a definite algebra; the ring `Z[gamma]`; the local
  structure `R_ell ≅ M_2(Z_ell)`; the Morita / lattice argument that a `gamma`-stable index-`ell` sublattice is `pi L`; the
  passage from `J_ell = pi I_ell` and `J_v = I_v` (`v ≠ ell`) to `J = pi I`) must be written out, including why the class of
  `pi I` is the class of `I` in the convention of `def:s03-DR` / stage 01 (left or right ideals, which side `Gamma_e` acts on).
  Check the stage tex's claims as you go (e.g. whether `Z_ell[gamma]` is the maximal order of `Q_ell(gamma)` for every
  `ell ∤ qM`, the case `ell` split / inert / ramified in `Z[gamma]`, `ell = 2, 3`), and fix the text where it is sketchy or
  wrong; keep the statement. Steps (2)–(4): derive `w_{e'} B(e,e') = w_e B(e',e)` from `prop:s01-adjoint` (cite the exact
  part), the action on the dual and the identity for `(T_ell − (ell+1)) phi`, and the conclusion in terms of the lattice
  `X(G)` and `Phi^comb` exactly as defined in `1-graphs.tex`.
- `prop:s03-LRdef`: trivial coefficients `V = Z`, `U = U_0(M)`. Define `s_j^dagger` as adjoints for the weighted pairings,
  prove the explicit formula and integrality, that `alpha^dagger = (−s_0^dagger, s_1^dagger)` maps `(Z[X_B(U)]^0)^2` into
  `Z[X_B(U')]^0`, and compute `alpha alpha^dagger` entry by entry with the sign convention (C1) of `3-ribet.tex`
  (`alpha = (−s_{0*}, s_{1*})`), identifying the off-diagonal entries with the Brandt operator `T_q = s_{1*} s_0^dagger`
  (and its transpose) in the homological convention; then `det = (q+1)^2 − T_q^2`. State which identification (vertex
  types, `U' = U ∩ ...`, the two maps `s_0, s_1`) you use and where it is fixed in the chapter. The `V_k`-normalisation is out of
