2. **F2 — location: stage.tex:44-55 def:s04-e — severity: minor — category: correctness**

   Fix: add hypothesis " $A$ acts and $e\in A$ central" to exactness statement; keep finite-monoid $e=T^N$ remark for the finite case only (currently correct).
