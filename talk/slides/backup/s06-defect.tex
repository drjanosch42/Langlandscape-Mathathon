\begin{frame}[shrink=6]{K2: the defect equals a Tamagawa ideal}
  \scriptsize
  \begin{block}{Defect formula (thm.\ s06-defect)}
    $\eta_F=\mathfrak c_q(F)\cdot\eta^B_F$ and $\mathfrak c_q=\mathfrak t_q$ (Tamagawa ideal), i.e.\ $(h_F)=\mathfrak c_q(F)=\mathfrak t_q(F)$:
    the squeeze $\mathfrak t_q\subseteq\mathfrak c_q\subseteq(h_F)$ closes via the local specialisation $P:I_F\to\widetilde{\mathcal O}_P$ (normalisation, a DVR) of (P), with Thm~E for $\eta=\mathfrak c_q\eta^B$.
  \end{block}
  \emph{Status:} proved modulo cited results; formalized modulo registered hypotheses, including the Galois half (\texttt{Hyp.FreeRankTwo}).\\
  \emph{Scope:} under (H0)--(H2), (P) and (H0$^-$) or (Lev); for $q\equiv-1 \bmod p$ only $M=1$ and (F1), (F1$'$) ($N(\bar\rho)=1$) are covered; $N(\bar\rho)>1$ is \emph{open} (see K3).\\[2pt]
  \textbf{Numbers} (all \emph{computed}; PARI/Python; \texttt{code/results/s06/}):

At weight $k+(p-1)$: $\mathrm{ord}\,\eta=\mathrm{ord}\,\mathfrak c=1$ on the $\mathrm{GL}_2$ side for 4 of 7 (the 3 of level 13 not computed, runtime); $\eta^B$ not computed there.
