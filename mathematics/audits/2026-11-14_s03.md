## 5. Required fixes (for S03-M9), by priority

**M8.1 — major — `1-graphs.tex:42–46` (statement of `prop:s03-phi`), `:52–54` (proof).** *Known item, still open, plus a second
hidden assumption.*
(i) The statement has no hypothesis `E ≠ ∅`. For `E = ∅`, `X = 0`, `Φ^comb = 0`, so `|Φ^comb| = 1`, while the right-hand side is the
empty sum `0`. The parenthesis in the proof ("all groups are 0 and the empty sum is 0") reads as if this were consistent; it is not.
(ii) The statement says only "every edge joining them", but `X(𝔾) = Z[E]⁰` and `Φ^comb = Z^E/(W·Z[E]⁰ + Z𝟏)` are equalities only if all
edges are oriented `v₀ → v₁`. For a mixed orientation `X(𝔾) = {x : Σ±x_e = 0}`, and the proof gets the result only up to the isometry
of fact (3). This is the tex side of the Lean item (`Graph.card_phiComb_twoVertex` assumes all edges `v₀ → v₁`).
*Fix:* state "Let `𝔾` have two vertices `v₀ ≠ v₁`, `E ≠ ∅`, every edge oriented from `v₀` to `v₁`". Add one sentence: for an arbitrary
orientation the order formula and the isomorphism class of `Φ^comb` are unchanged (fact (3)), but `X(𝔾)` is `Z[E]⁰` only after the
sign change. Replace the parenthesis by "for `E = ∅`, `Φ^comb = 0` has order 1 and the sum is 0, so the formula fails; this case is
excluded". `lem:s03-cyclic` inherits the hypotheses through "in the situation of". The Lean statements (S03-L3) then match the tex.

**M8.2 — major — `5-amalgam.tex:258–273` (`lem:s03-H1G`), with `:74–75`, `:279–281`, `:306`, `:323`, `:326`.**
The proof needs the eigenvalues of `T_q = T_{α'}` on all of `H¹(Γ, V⊗C)`, including the non-parabolic part. Two inputs are cited for
this, and neither covers it. Hida §6.3 Thm 1 (p. 177) is Hecke equivariance for the *parabolic* cohomology only. Stein (A.2.4), p. 205,
states `H¹(Y₀(N); M̃_{k−2}) ≅ S_k ⊕ S̄_k ⊕ E_k` as an isomorphism, without Hecke equivariance (the sentence "one of Hecke modules"
there refers to (A.2.1), weight 2, compact curve). So the claim "the Eisenstein eigenvalues of `T_{α'}` are `χ(q) + ψ(q)q^{k−1}`" is
supported only by the level-1 numerics of `lem:s03-Tclassical`. The statement is true, but the status `\stpmc` is not earned as cited.
`H¹(G,V) = 0` is exported in `if:s03-amalgam` and used by the short form of `prop:s03-MV`, by `def:s03-LR` ("always holds") and by the
"equivalently" of the conjecture.
*Fix (either):* (a) add the missing step: Hida's map `f ↦ (γ ↦ ∫_{z₀}^{γz₀} ω(f))` is defined for every `f ∈ M_k(Γ)`, and the
computation of §6.3, pp. 176–177, that it intertwines `f|[Γα'Γ]` with the cocycle operator of `lem:s03-Tclassical`(c) does not use
cuspidality; with the dimension count of (A.2.4) this gives `H¹(Γ, V⊗C) = im(M_k) ⊕ im(S̄_k)` as `T_{α'}`-modules. Write this as
part (e) of `lem:s03-Tclassical`, status `\stpmc`, and cite it in `lem:s03-H1G`. Or (b) mark the Eisenstein step of `lem:s03-H1G` as
`\stsketch` (cusp part `\stpmc`), and say so in the status lines of `prop:s03-MV`, `def:s03-LR` and `if:s03-amalgam`.
