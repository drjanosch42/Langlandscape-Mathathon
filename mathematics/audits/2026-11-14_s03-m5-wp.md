## Defects

### M5.1 — major — `5-amalgam.tex:164–170`, with `:125`, `:139–140`, `:209–211`
**Problem.** The binding item §0.2.8 and the exported interface are stated in terms of the *classical* `T_q`. The section proves
the table only for `T_q := T_{α'}` and leaves "`T_{α'}` = classical `T_q`" as `\stsketch` ("to be confirmed numerically by
S03-C5/H1"). Yet `prop:s03-PsiPsi` (l. 125) and `if:s03-amalgam` (l. 209–211) carry `\stproved` with no caveat, and C5 feeds
classical PARI eigenvalues into exactly this table. The supporting argument given is not a proof: "does not preserve the lattice
for `k>2`" is asserted, the relation `T_q² = T_{q²} + q·q^{k−2}` does not single out the operator, Stein §8.3 (pp. 128–130) concerns
the action on modular symbols (homology, action through `g̃`), and Stein Ex. A.17 (p. 226) is stated in a framework with
`g ∈ SL_n(Q)` and without specifying the action of `g` on the coefficient module, so it cannot decide a factor `q^{2−k}`.
The claim itself is true (evidence 2 and 3 above).
**Required fix.** Replace l. 164–170 by a lemma with proof, status *proved modulo cited* (Hida Thm 1):
(i) cocycle formula `(T_g z)(γ) = Σ_i β_i z(β_i^{-1}γβ_{j(i)})` from `cor`'s cocycle formula with `β_i = r_i g`,
`r_i ∈ Γ/(Γ∩gΓg^{-1})`; (ii) `ι` turns `ΓαΓ = ⊔Γα_i`, `α_iγ = γ_iα_j` into `ΓαΓ = ⊔β_iΓ`, `γ_i = β_i^{-1}γβ_j` (uses
Lemma groups (c)), so Hida's `[Γα'Γ]` is `T_{α'}`; (iii) `L(n;A) ≅ Sym^n(std)` through `w`, cite Hida §6.1 p. 165 for the action
and §6.3 p. 175–177, Thm 1 for equivariance. Then drop "sketched" and the Stein A.17 citation (or keep Stein only as "compare").
Add to the interface block the sentence "`T_q` = classical operator: proved modulo [Hi93, §6.3 Thm 1]; computed at level 1 for
`(k,q) = (12,2),(12,3),(16,2),(4,2)`".
