## B. Fix list

Items M9.1, M9.2 and M9.10-M9.16 are the M8 items, confirmed here (M8 number in brackets); the others are new.

**M9.1 — maj — hsieh.tex:115-127, also :11 [= M8.1, confirmed].**
Problem: the Translation never says which projector Hsieh's forms carry. His forms carry the cohomological U_p (p. 30,
p. 31 "tf(x) = f(tx)"); under ν this is e* on Z_p[X_α], not e (`lem:s04-ekills`(d)). The twist U_p^{-α}[τ^D_{Np^α}] in
B_{N,α} is what turns the (e, e*) duality into a pairing on eS^D × eS^D.
Required fix: add clause (v) to the Translation with this statement and a reference to `lem:s04-ekills`(d) and
`lem:s01-Up`(d); qualify "agree on U_p" in :11. Fix M9.4 in the same edit.

**M9.2 — maj — hsieh.tex:143-148 and :194-195 [= M8.2, confirmed on Hsieh p. 34].**
Problem: `thm:s04-Gor` is stated for every maximal ideal m satisfying (CR_D). Hsieh's proof covers only the m
containing ker λ^D_f for a primitive family f of tame conductor N^+N^-.
Required fix: add the hypothesis "m = m_F for a primitive I-adic family F of tame conductor MD", or mark the general
case `\stexp`. Same change in `if:s04-Mord`.

**M9.3 — maj — control.tex:139-142 and :152-159; hsieh.tex:51-53 and :76-78; hsieh.tex:190-193.**
Problem: `thm:s04-control`(b) is stated for every U of level prime to p, integrally over O, with status `\stcited`.
The chapter's own dictionary supports less: `rem:s04-controlb` covers only U = U_1(M) ("for other U the source is Hida
1988, Thm. 9.4, whose hypotheses are only partly legible"), and step (iv) says the first sentence of (b) is integral
only if p ∤ φ(M), otherwise `\stsketch`. Neither restriction appears in the theorem, its status line or `if:s04-Mord`.
The theorem is the source of `Hyp.HidaControl` (plan §0.4).
Required fix: state (b) for U = U_1(M); tag the general U `\stexp`; add "if p ∤ φ(M)" to the first sentence, or tag the
case p | φ(M) `\stsketch` in the theorem's status line; repeat both in `if:s04-Mord`. Check that the docstring of
`Hyp.HidaControl` carries the same scope.

**M9.4 — min — ordinary.tex:176-179 ("Why (d) matters").**
Problem: "the family (e*Z_p[X_s])_s is compatible with neither π_* nor π^*" is false as written. By (d), ν identifies
e*Z_p[X_s] with eZ_p^{X_s}, which is a direct system under π^*; on Z_p[X_s] this is the transfer π^! (`prop:s01-adjoint`(c):
ν'∘π^! = π^*∘ν, valid inside the tower). What is true: e*Z_p[X_s] is not stable under π_* (U'_p does not commute with
π_*), so (d) pairs an inverse system (e, π_*) with a direct system (e*, π^!).
Required fix: replace the sentence by this statement; the conclusion (a twist is needed, `def:s04-BN`) stays.

**M9.5 — min — ordinary.tex:114-117 and :122-123 (`lem:s04-ekills`(d), last sentence).**
Problem: "e and e* are different idempotents in general" is asserted inside the lemma and tagged `\stopen`.
Data point from this audit: with the 48×48 matrix `U_p` stored in `H6_sign.json` (D = 2, M = 7, p = 5, s = 1) the
idempotent e = U_p^N mod 5^4 has rank 27 and is not symmetric mod 5, so e ≠ e* there. This was an ad hoc computation,
not a committed script.
Required fix: move the sentence out of the lemma into a remark; have the C lane record the check in
`code/results/s04/` and then tag it `\stcomp` with that example.

**M9.6 — min — control.tex:13-14 (`def:s04-Mord`).**
