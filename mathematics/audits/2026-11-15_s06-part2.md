### Verdicts (i)–(iv)

**(i) M = 1: CORRECT as claimed (`\stpmc`, proved modulo cited).** Steps (0)–(5), `lem:s06-levM-hecke` specialised to M = 1 and
`rem:s06-levM-M1` (i)–(iv) were re-derived. The projector identity `tr(ρ(Φ)e) = δ(Φ)` holds whether δ is the sub or the quotient
(product of upper triangular matrices), the roots in `𝒪/ϖ^t` are unique because their difference is a unit, and the matching on
`T_ℓ`, `T_q`, `T_r`, `T_p`, `U_q` is complete. Cited inputs actually used: KO Thm 2.9 + Lemma 2.4 at `N(ρ̄) = 1` (no twist problem
there), Fontaine–Laffaille at p, (I2), Fontaine/Edixhoven, the ordinary shape, `lem:s06-decomp`(b). Remarks P2-R5, R6, R7, R8

**(ii) M > 1 under (Lev′_M): NOT correct as claimed.**

  for ℓ | M with ℓ ≡ −1 mod p it holds in the sign-fixed reading R4, which I checked point by point and find sound, but it is our
  reading and not KO's text.
- For `N(ρ̄) > 1` step (4) is wrong whenever `ρ̄|I_ℓ` is a non-trivial scalar for some ℓ | N(ρ̄), ℓ ∉ P_ρ̄ (then necessarily a
  quadratic character, since det is unramified at ℓ). In that case KO Lemma 2.4 / Thm 2.9 / Thm 3.14 are not available (KO Rem. 2.2,
  p. 3; DFG §3.1 lists "ρ₀ has minimal conductor among its twists" as a standing condition), and the asserted isomorphism
  `π: R ≅ A` is false. (L1) + (L2) do not exclude this. P2-R1.
- With an added twist-minimality condition (L3) I find no error in the argument for odd ℓ (§3), but the cited theorem then rests
  on Dim09 Lemma 6.4(i) through KO Lemma 2.4, which nobody has read. Stating that case as open, or as `\provisional`, is right.

**(iii) "Equal conductor does not imply KO-minimality": the writer is RIGHT, with one wrong side remark and one consequence not
drawn.** The example is valid: `ρ_f|G_ℓ ≅ μ ⊗ sp`, μ ramified and quadratic on `I_ℓ`, monodromy ≡ 0 mod ϖ on the lattice. Then
`ρ̄|G_ℓ ≅ μ̄χ̄ ⊕ μ̄` (split because ℓ ≢ 1), both conductor exponents are 2, `d(ℓ) = 0`, `ρ_f(I_ℓ)` is infinite and `#ρ̄(I_ℓ) = 2`,
so KO Def. 3.5(1) fails. Such f exist (f = h ⊗ χ_ℓ with h an ℓ-new form whose ρ̄_h is unramified at ℓ). S06-M6's unconditional
"equal conductor gives minimal ramification" was therefore wrong as written. But:
- the sentence "[KO, Rem. 2.2] assumes minimal conductor among twists, which does not exclude this" (l. 56–57) is **wrong**:
  `f ⊗ χ_ℓ` has trivial character and exponent 1 at ℓ, so f is not twist-minimal, and Rem. 2.2 excludes exactly this example;
- there is a second counterexample that survives twist-minimality of f but not of ρ̄: ℓ ≡ −1 mod p,
  `ρ_f|G_ℓ ≅ μ ⊗ Ind(θ)` from the unramified quadratic extension with `θ|I` of p-power order, μ ramified quadratic. Exponent 2 on
  both sides, `ρ̄|G_ℓ` reducible (so ℓ ∉ P_ρ̄), `#ρ_f(I_ℓ) = 2p^a`;
- in every counterexample (given (H0) at ℓ, ℓ ∉ P_ρ̄, ℓ odd) `ρ̄|I_ℓ` is a non-trivial scalar. This is the regime in which the
  cited `R = T` theorem is not available at all. So the right repair was not (L2) alone but (L2) plus twist-minimality of ρ̄, and
  under twist-minimality S06-M6's formulation "`ord_ℓ M = c(ℓ)`" is equivalent to (L2) (§3). The correction is right about the
  statement and incomplete about what it implies for step (4).

## 3. Under (L3): what is true (own derivation, ℓ odd; `\stproved` by me, not checked against Diamond's types)

Let ℓ | N(ρ̄), ℓ ∉ P_ρ̄, ℓ ≢ 1 mod p, det unramified at ℓ, and `ρ̄|I_ℓ` not a non-trivial scalar.
- `d(ℓ) = 0`: every lift is KO-minimal. Infinite inertia image forces `μ ⊗ sp` with `ρ̄|I_ℓ = μ̄ ⊗ (unipotent)`, non-scalar means
  the unipotent part is non-trivial, which is KO Def. 3.5(2). Finite image with a non-trivial pro-p kernel K: if `ρ|I_ℓ` is a sum
  of two characters, Frobenius fixes them (`p | ℓ−1`, excluded) or exchanges them (then `ρ̄|I_ℓ` is scalar, or ℓ ∈ P_ρ̄); if
  `ρ|I_ℓ` is irreducible, K commutes with wild inertia, is scalar or diagonal of determinant one, and is trivial (p odd; for the
  diagonal case the index-two subgroup is tame for ℓ odd).
- `d(ℓ) = 1`: (L2) ⟺ `ord_ℓ M = c(ℓ)` (lemma (c)(iii)(β), (γ)).
So under (L3) the condition (L2) can be replaced by `M = N(ρ̄)·M₀`, which is S06-M6's formulation. ℓ = 2 was not checked.
