## Findings
### F1 (major, APPLIED) talk frame 6 contradicted N1 in the two route bullets
`talk/slides/f05b-jlproof.tex` still said that route 1 removes "the JL input to the current proof of Hyp.X" and that route 2
is "resolve Hyp.X without JL: new mathematics, unclear (open)". Item 2 of the same frame says Hyp.X for D = q is expected
JL-free by the same geometry. Replaced by:
> *Route 1, trace formula:* completing our GL(2) trace-formula formalization discharges `Hyp.JLClassical` and the JL input
> beyond D = q (Shimura-curve version of Hyp.X), provided the trace-formula comparison is carried out for the quaternion
> algebra occurring there. Planned; timing uncertain.
> *Route 2, geometric:* the comparison without JL: for D = q this is Hyp.X (*expected*); beyond D = q new mathematics,
> unclear (open).

Frame 6 still fits (one line more, no overfull box; page image checked).

### F2 (major, APPLIED) "That hope was not met" misstates the seed and contradicts the end of the paragraph
`whycited.tex`: "... and expected only a comparison of Jacobians to remain at the end. That hope was not met, for a
structural reason." The seed says the opposite of a failed hope: its abstract and §1.2(b) state that the geometry
(Deligne–Rapoport, Néron models, monodromy) is *concentrated in* Hyp.X, the comparison with the character group of the modular
Jacobian, and that this is the only geometric input. That is what the paragraph's last sentence reports as achieved.
Replaced the second sentence by:
> That comparison still needs Deligne--Rapoport, for a structural reason.

### F3 (minor, NOT applied) "beyond D = q it invokes it" can be read as "for D = q nothing JL-like is cited"
Abstract, `motivation`, `achieved`, `mainresults`, talk f05b. The main theorem for D = q (Thm. 21.5) cites the Λ-adic JL
(Hsieh, Longo–Vigni) as an input, which rests on classical JL. `whycited` and `summary.tex` say this correctly ("beyond D = q,
and for the p-adic and Λ-adic statements, JL is an input"). The short forms speak about the route to classical JL (item (a)),
so they are not false, but a reader of the abstract alone may conclude that the D = q results are JL-free. Word-neutral
replacement for the abstract, if the team wants it (150 words kept): "... modulo cited geometry; elsewhere Jacquet--Langlands
is cited." (replaces "beyond $D=q$ it invokes it", 5 words by 4). Team decision; N1 fixed the present wording.

### F4 (minor, NOT applied) "an explicit form of route 2 below is essentially O2"
Route 2 for D = q is Hyp.X at Γ₁(Mp^s)-level, "expected by the same geometry"; the sentence before calls O2 "new
mathematics". Read together, route 2 at D = q looks both expected and new. Suggested: "a simplicial form of route~2 would
be O2" (O2 asks for more than route 2: a map of simplicial objects, not only the identification of character groups).

### F5 (minor, NOT applied) subsection title "…; cited in general"
`whycited.tex` title "Classical JL: first case modulo cited geometry; cited in general". Not wrong (Thm. 6.1 is cited
throughout), but "in general" is the wording N1 replaced. Option: "...; cited beyond $D=q$". Changing it also changes the
table of contents and the report heading.

### F6 (note) "a ``Jacobian comparison at the end'' presupposes Deligne–Rapoport"
The quotation marks suggest a seed quotation; the seed's words are "comparison hypothesis at the GL₂ end". Harmless.
