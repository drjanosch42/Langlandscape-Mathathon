## Defects

**M3.1 — major — `3-ribet.tex:138–139` (Remark `rem:s03-torsion`) versus `:200` ((R4) table) and `:97–98` (status of Thm (b)).**
Problem: the remark says "Part (b) and the identification of $H_0$ need full strong approximation (see (R4) below)". The (R4) table
(line 200) and the theorem's own status line say the opposite: Thm (b) needs only (iii) plus Prop. s02-coh(b) (the tree
property and Shapiro), with no density. The table is right. Prop. s02-coh(b) assumes only (iii), and density enters only in
s02-coh(c),(d). So the deliverable "(R4) SA dependence explicit" contradicts itself. The sentence is copied from the stage tex,
but it is false as a dependence claim.
Fix: rewrite the sentence as "Part (b) needs (iii) together with the cited tree property and Shapiro's lemma, but no density. The
identification $H_0(\GU,V)=V_{U^1_p}$ of its last term, and Cor. (ii′), need full strong approximation (see (R4))." Record this in the
WP report as a correction of the stage-tex wording, not a silent change.

**M3.2 — major (sign convention) — `3-ribet.tex:29–30`.**
Problem: the text says that $\alpha$ and $\delta_{\rm Ri}$ differ by $\sigma(x,y)=(-x,y)$, and "This changes no kernel or image, so every
exactness statement below holds verbatim for Ribet's sign". This is false as stated in two ways.
- $\mathrm{im}\,\delta_{\rm Ri}=\sigma(\mathrm{im}\,\alpha)$. This equals $\mathrm{im}\,\alpha$ only when the image is $\sigma$-stable. That is true in (a), where $\alpha$ is onto, but not in (b). There $\mathrm{im}\,\alpha=\ker\varepsilon$, and $\varepsilon(-f_0,f_1)\ne\varepsilon(f_0,f_1)$ in general.
- The kernels of the transposes change. For Ribet's sign, $\ker\delta_{\rm Ri}^\vee$ is $\{(c\mathbf1,-c\mathbf1)\}$, not the line of constants in Cor. (ii). In (ii′) it is $\{(f_w,-f_{[q]^{-1}w})\}$.

A reader working in Ribet's convention would carry Cor. (ii)/(ii′) over with the wrong sign.
Fix: say that $\ker\delta_{\rm Ri}=\ker\alpha$, that $\mathrm{im}\,\delta_{\rm Ri}=\sigma(\mathrm{im}\,\alpha)$ and $\coker\delta_{\rm Ri}\cong\coker\alpha$ via $\sigma$, so that
exactness (Thm (a), (b)) carries over. Add that in Ribet's sign the kernels in Cor. (ii), (ii′) become $\{(c,-c)\}$ and
$\{(f_w,-f_{[q]^{-1}w})\}$ (that is, $\sigma^\vee$ applied to them), alongside the existing remark on the off-diagonal entries of $\alpha\alpha^\dagger$.

**M3.3 — minor — `3-ribet.tex:74–83` (proof of Lemma bip (c), degenerate case).**
Problem: the short exact sequence $0\to K_\bullet\to C_\bullet(\G)\to C_\bullet(\G^{(2)})\to0$ needs $\pi_*$ to be onto in degree 0. That
fails when $E=\emptyset$: $\G$ is then a single vertex, and $R\to R^2$ is not onto. The parenthetical "all terms vanish" covers the
sequence of (a) but not the claim that it *is* the long exact sequence of the collapse.
Fix: state (c) for $E\ne\emptyset$ (equivalently $V_0,V_1\ne\emptyset$), and note that for $E=\emptyset$ the sequence of (a) is $0$; or
define $\G^{(2)}$ with vertex set $\{j:V_j\ne\emptyset\}$.

**M3.4 — minor — `3-ribet.tex:89` (Thm (a), equivariance clause) versus `:113–114`.**
Problem: the statement claims equivariance for "all Hecke operators supported away from $q$". The proof needs $U$ to be factorisable
at the support $\Sigma$ of $g$, which is the "acting compatibly" hypothesis of Prop. s02-quot(c). This hypothesis appears only in a parenthesis in the
proof.
Fix: put it into the statement, e.g. "for all $T=[UgU]$ with $g$ supported at a finite set $\Sigma\not\ni q$ at which $U$ is
factorisable (all standard levels)". The interface `if:s03-ribet` inherits the clause.

**M3.5 — minor — `bib/s03-m3.bib:2–3`.**
Problem: the comment says the WP cites "Ri83, Ri84, Ri90, Vo, Mz, DR, Se, Br, DT, MS19, SGA7". The section cites only Ri90 (two `\lit`
calls). The list looks copied from another WP and misdescribes this one.
Fix: list only the sources this WP actually cites (Ri90), or make the comment WP-neutral.
