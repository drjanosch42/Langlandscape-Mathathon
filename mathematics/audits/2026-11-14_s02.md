### 1.1 `prop:s02-BG` (focus item)

- Step 0 (`[γ_q h] = [h]`), Step 1 (well defined), Step 2 (injectivity by the norm argument), Step 3 (morphism),
  Step 4 (local surjectivity, both types, with `u_q = k d`, `d = diag(det u_q, 1) ∈ Iw_q`), Step 5 (graph lemma): all correct.
  They use only `def:s02-tree` and `lem:s02-tree`(b)–(d), `Nrd(B^×) ⊆ Q_{>0}`, `Nrd U ⊆ Ẑ^×`.
- (iii)⇒(i), (i)⇒(a), (i)⇔(ii) are tree-free and SA-free, as claimed. (i)⇒(ii) correctly yields both (SA_U) and the
  surjectivity of `X^1_B(U) → X_B(U)` at this level.
- Tree connectivity enters exactly at the two places marked **[tree]**: the last clause of (b) and (i)⇒(iii). Correct.
- "SA alone does not give (ii)" is correct (the surjectivity of `X^1_B → X_B` is the Hasse–Schilling part).
- The split of the tex's "Moreover" clause into statement / (a) / (b) is a correct refinement and is declared.
- One caveat on the tree-free claim: M6.4.

### 1.2 Sign and index table (focus item)

- `∂ = (−s_{0*}, s_{1*})` and `δ(f_0,f_1) = s_1^* f_1 − s_0^* f_0` agree verbatim with `if:s02-coh`, with Serre II.2.8
  (`∂y = t(y) − o(y)`, minus sign at `o` = type 0), with `lem:s02-cellmod`(b), and with the L8/L9 rows
  (`Graph.boundary`, `partialHom`, `deltaCoh`).
- `ν ∘ s_{j*} = tr_j ∘ ν'` with no index factor and no factor 2: correct. The pairs `(uU', γ)` biject with
  `γ ∈ P_U(y,x)`, and `{±1} ∩ U = {±1} ∩ U' = {±1} ∩ U'_α` because `−1 ∈ Iw_q`.
- `ν' ∘ s_j^! = s_j^* ∘ ν` with `s_j^![x] = Σ [Γ_x:Γ_e][e]`, `[Γ_x:Γ_e] = w_x/w_e`: correct (orbit–stabiliser on `U/U'`).
- `tr_j 1_e = [Γ_{s_j e}:Γ_e] 1_{s_j e}`, `∂^T = δ` exactly, Brandt adjoint `s_1^! b_1 − s_0^! b_0`: correct.
