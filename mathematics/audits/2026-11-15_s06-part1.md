## 1. Required fixes

### Major

**R1 (maj) — `𝒪_P = I_F/P` is not a DVR in general.** `s06-m3.tex:127` ("a DVR: `I_F` is normal and `P` has height one");
used in `s06-m2.tex:209, 229–231` (`lem:s06-tam`(b)), `s06-m3.tex:32–34` (`cor:s06-fixed`, application), `s06-m3.tex:145–157`
(steps (4), (5)), `s06-m3.tex:165–176` (`cor:s06-allweights`).
Normality of `I_F` and height one give that the *localisation* `(I_F)_P` is a DVR, not the *quotient* `I_F/P`. The quotient is
a one-dimensional local domain, finite over `𝒪`, i.e. an order in `κ(P)`; it need not be integrally closed. Abstract example
consistent with all standing hypotheses on `I_F`: `I = 𝒪[[Y]]`, `Λ = 𝒪[[T]] → I`, `T ↦ Y² − p²u` with `u` a non-square unit
(regular, hence normal, étale over `Λ` at `T = 0`), and `I/(T) = 𝒪[Y]/(Y² − p²u)` is not normal. The plan tex does not make
this claim; it was introduced in the chapter. The Lean plan treats it as an explicit assumption (`Setting`: `𝒪` a local domain
with `P_surj`; `Defect.cor_fixed` has `[IsDiscreteValuationRing 𝒪]`; `squeeze` needs only `[IsLocalHom P]`).
*Fix (preferred, no new hypothesis):* let `𝒪̃_P` be the normalisation of `I_F/P` (a DVR, finite over `𝒪`, `I_F → 𝒪̃_P` a local
homomorphism, not surjective) and replace `𝒪_P` by `𝒪̃_P` in `lem:s06-tam`(b), `lem:s06-control`(b), `cor:s06-fixed`
(application), step (5) and `cor:s06-allweights`. Everything goes through: Lemma S base-changes (Gor_R) along any ring map;
`𝒪 → 𝒪̃_P` is flat; in step (5), from `P(h)P(𝔡)𝒪̃_P = P(h)𝒪̃_P` one gets `P(𝔡)𝒪̃_P = 𝒪̃_P`, so some `d ∈ 𝔡` has `P(d) ∈ 𝒪̃_P^×`
(`𝒪̃_P` local), hence `d ∉ 𝔪_{I_F}` because `I_F → 𝒪̃_P` is local (integral extension of the local ring `I_F/P`). This is exactly
the hypothesis shape of the Lean `squeeze` (S06-L7). *Alternative (matches the current Lean `Setting` literally):* add
"`I_F/P` is a DVR" to (P) for the point `P`, and to the hypotheses of `lem:s06-tam`(b) and `cor:s06-allweights` for `P'`, and
delete the parenthesis at `s06-m3.tex:127`. In both cases tell the Lean track (L8a/L9b/L12) which variant the chapter uses.

**R2 (maj) — `cor:s06-fixed` is stated with too few hypotheses.** `s06-m3.tex:24–25`.
(i) (H0) (`q ≢ 1 mod p`) is not assumed, but the proof uses it twice: `lem:s06-decomp`(b) at weight `k` (distinct roots) and
condition (6) of `thm:s06-KO` (`s06-m3.tex:45`). (H0⁻) alone allows `q ≡ 1` with `ρ̄` unramified at `q`, where [KO, Thm 1.1]
does not apply. (ii) "`f` `q`-new of level `N = qM`" must be "`f` a newform of level `N = qM`": [KO, Thm 1.1] is for a newform
of level `N⁺N⁻` (verified, KO p. 1), and the proof itself uses "`f` is a newform" (`s06-m3.tex:46`). A form that is `q`-new but
old at a prime of `M` is not covered. In `thm:s06-defect` both hold ((H0); `F` primitive of tame level `N`).
*Fix:* "Let `f` be a newform of level `N = qM` and weight `2 ≤ k ≤ p−1`, with `p ∤ N`, (H0), (H0⁻), (H2) for `ρ̄_f`, `𝔪_f`
non-Eisenstein." In `lem:s06-control` and `cor:s06-allweights` add "(`F_P` is the `p`-stabilisation of a newform of level `N`,
`F` being primitive of tame level `N`)".
