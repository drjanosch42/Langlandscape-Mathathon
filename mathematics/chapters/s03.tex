\section*{Stage 03: weighted graphs, the definite Ribet sequence and the Ihara lemma}\label{sec:stage-03}

Stage 03 attaches to the Brandt graph of stage 02 its weighted-graph invariants (the lattice $X(\G)$ and the group
$\Phicomb(\G)$), checks them against Mazur at level $1$, proves the definite Ribet sequence as a vertex collapse, proves the
definite Ihara lemma including the Hecke action on $H_0$, and records the Betti side for $\GL_2$ (amalgam,
Mayer--Vietoris, $\Psi^*\Psi$). The conventions (C1)--(C6) are fixed in \S\ref{ss:s03-conv}.
