\section*{Stage 00: introduction}\label{sec:stage-00}

This chapter states the problem and the question that guided the project (an integral Hecke-equivariant map), presents
Ribet's exact sequence as its solution in the first case and its form on the Bruhat--Tits tree, gives the scope and the precise
setup, what was achieved on the question, the choice of route, an outline of stages 01--06 with the status of their main results, and what is new
and what is known, and closes with the plan for stage 2 of the Mathathon. It introduces no new
results: every statement carries the status of the chapter where it is established.
