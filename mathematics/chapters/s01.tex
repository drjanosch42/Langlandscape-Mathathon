\section*{Stage 01: quaternionic foundations}\label{sec:stage-01}

its levels and class sets, the Eichler mass formula, Brandt modules and the Brandt pairing, the Hecke operators with
the fixed normalisation $N_{U_p}$, the conventions dictionary, and the cited targets.
