\section*{Stage 06: the integral Jacquet--Langlands defect}\label{sec:stage-06}

Stage 06 makes seed \S9 precise. On the $\GL_2$ side, with the definite algebra $B=B_q$ entering only through the $\Lam$-adic
Hecke Jacquet--Langlands isomorphism $\T'\cong\T^{\ord}_B$ (\texttt{if:s01-targets}, \texttt{if:s04-Mord}), it proves that the
defect $(h_F)=\eta_F(\eta^B_F)^{-1}$ of a $q$-new Hida branch is the old/new congruence ideal $\cc_q(F)$ (commutative algebra) and
that $\cc_q(F)$ is the Tamagawa ideal $\tq_q(F)$ (Galois theory plus one fixed-weight input).
