\section*{Stage 05: the comparison hypothesis and the simplicial shape}\label{sec:stage-05}

Stage 05 owns the single geometric input of the route: the comparison $\HypX$ between the character group at $q$ of the
Jacobian of the $\Gamma_1(Mp^s)\cap\Gamma_0(q)$ modular curve and the degree-zero part of the definite Brandt module, with
every normalisation fixed (the sign $\varepsilon$ and the exponent of $\Pi_q$, the diamonds, $U_p$). It proves the formal
consequences of $\HypX$ (the sub- versus quotient-lattice comparison, the $\Lam$-adic version), records what is known in
the literature and what is not, and describes the simplicial shape and the zig-zag for several ramified primes.
