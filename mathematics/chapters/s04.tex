\section*{Stage 04: the ordinary and \texorpdfstring{$\Lambda$}{Lambda}-adic passage}\label{sec:stage-04}

Stage 04 passes from the finite levels of stage 01 to Hida families on the definite side. It constructs the ordinary
projector, shows that it kills every Eisenstein-type term met so far (degree, vertex and component terms), proves
freeness and control of the $\Lam$-adic Brandt module by an elementary argument, proves the $\Lam$-adic definite Ribet
sequence, and translates Hsieh's Gorenstein and self-pairing results for $\eta^B_F$ into the conventions of stage 01.
