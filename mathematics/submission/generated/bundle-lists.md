# Lean submission bundle: work lists

Generated by `mathematics/submission/tools/bundle_lists.py` from `generated/lean-table.json` (gated tree 2323a252).

- `bundle-uncited-104.csv`: the 104 chapter-cited declarations that are not among the 840 `#print axioms` rows (name, file:line, kind, chapter labels, Hyp arguments, `only_discharged_hyps`, reason). 102 are library declarations not listed in `Audit/PrintAxioms.lean` (covered only by the sorry count and the forbidden-token grep); the test-library ones are marked. 3 of the 104 take ONLY discharged hypotheses (column `only_discharged_hyps = yes`), e.g. `Quat.nrdSurj_Utower`.
- `bundle-discharged-18.csv`: the 18 audited theorems whose only registered-hypothesis arguments are the discharged `Hyp.PadicOrdDecomp` / `Hyp.RamNrdSurj` (name, file:line, which Hyp, binder position among the declaration's bracket groups and the binder text, discharge lemma, other explicit binders).
- `bundle-lists.json`: both lists.

**Caveat for the `_uncond` corollaries.** Discharging the registered hypothesis removes only that argument. Several of the 18 keep other explicit, non-registered hypotheses that are part of the statement. Examples:
- `lim_exact_ribet`: `hconn` (connectivity of the Brandt graphs) and `hA` (surjectivity of the transition maps, the content of the open `Hyp.InvLimExact` discussion);
- `ordRibet_exact_Utower`, `rank_ordRibet`, `rank_ordRibet_full`: `hconn` / `hc`;
- `Mord.proj_surjective`, `TowerHyp.mordTrans_surjective`: a `TowerHyp` structure.

Such corollaries are "Lean-verified, free of registered hypotheses, under the stated hypotheses (connectivity, …)", NOT unconditional versions of the chapter theorems. The `other_explicit_binders` column is a heuristic parse of the bracket groups: it may include parenthesised terms from the conclusion. Check each signature in the source.

Binder position: the 1-based index of the hypothesis binder among all bracket groups `( ) [ ] { } ⦃ ⦄` of the signature, as written in the source.
