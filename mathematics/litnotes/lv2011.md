## Items
1. **Sect. 3 (definite case) starts on p. 22 (Sect. 3.1 "Modular forms on quaternion algebras"), p. 23 (3.2 Hecke algebras), p. 24 (3.3 Hida families, 3.4 Hecke action), p. 25 (Prop. 3.3, 3.5 Measure-valued forms), p. 26 (3.6 specialisation maps, 3.7 The Control Theorem), p. 27 (Prop. 3.5), p. 28 (Prop. 3.6, Thm 3.7): MATCH (tex: "Sect. 3 (definite case) p. 22ff").**
2. **Prop. 3.6 and its proof, p. 28 (tex: "Prop. 3.6 and its proof, p. 28: lim_r S_2(U_r,O_f)^ord is Lambda-free by [Hid88b, Cor. 10.4]"): MATCH.** Statement: the module W^ord_K is a 1-dimensional K-vector space (K = fraction field of the Hida-family coefficient ring R), and the action of the universal definite Hecke algebra h^D_univ on W^ord_K factors through h_R. In the proof: V := lim over r of S_2(U_r, F_f/O_f)^ord (limit induced by U_r contained in U_{r-1}), V^ := its Pontryagin dual = lim S_2(U_r, O_f)^ord = W^ord; "thanks to [14, Cor. 10.4], V^ is free of finite rank over Lambda" (ref. [14] in the bibliography on p. 29 is Hida, Ann. of Math. 128 (1988), 295-384, so it is the 1988 paper, checked in `hida1988.md`); then, using Prop. 3.3 in place of Prop. 2.2 and the argument of Prop. 2.17, W^ord_L is free of rank 1 over h^{D,ord}_infty tensor_Lambda L, and the decomposition (17) h^{D,ord}_infty tensor L = K + N finishes. So the Lambda-freeness is cited from Hida, in the proof, and the tex says exactly this.
3. **Thm 3.7, p. 28 (tex: "Thm 3.7, p. 28 (eigen-components, over a field)"): MATCH.** For every kappa in A(R) the map rho_kappa of Prop. 3.5 induces an isomorphism of 1-dimensional F_kappa-vector spaces W^ord_{h_kappa} / P_kappa W^ord_{h_kappa} -> W^ord_kappa, where W^ord_kappa = S_k(U_r, F_kappa)^{ord, f_kappa} (the f_kappa-eigenspace, one-dimensional since f_kappa is a newform or a p-stabilised newform). Proof: Prop. 3.5 (p. 27, injectivity rho_kappa) gives one inequality and Prop. 3.6 (dim 1) the other. This is a statement over a field (the tex's phrase "over a field" is accurate): it is a control theorem for the eigenspace, not for the whole ordinary module over Lambda.
4. **Surrounding facts the tex does not cite but which matter for the dictionary:** the indefinite analogue is Thm 2.18 (p. 22) with Prop. 2.16, 2.17; the introduction says (p. 4) "the above result is a combination of Thm 2.18 (indefinite case) and Thm 3.7 (definite)". Prop. 3.5 (p. 27): rho_kappa is injective on F_kappa-spaces of weight k = k_kappa, character eps = eps_kappa, n := k - 2.
