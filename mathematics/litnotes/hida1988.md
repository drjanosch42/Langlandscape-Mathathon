## A. [Hid88b] = tex "hida_1988_p-adic-hecke-algebras-for-gl2-over-totally-real-fields" (`thm:s04-control`, tex lines near "The original source is ...")
Setting of the paper: B a quaternion algebra over a totally real field (Sect. 8-10 treat the cohomology of Shimura-type quotients X(U) of D^x for B definite (r = 0) or indefinite), V^ord(n,v;U) = lim_alpha e H^q(X(U_alpha*), L(n,v;K/O)) the ordinary part of the limit of cohomology with divisible coefficients.
1. **Thm 8.6, p. 362 (PDF 69) (tex: "weight independence of V^ord"): MATCH.** For an open subgroup U of the hat-group of the shape (a product with a p-component; symbols lost in OCR), and integers n >= n' >= 0 satisfying a congruence condition (garbled), there is a canonical isomorphism V^ord(n,v;U) = V^ord(0,0,chi_n;U) = V^ord(n',v;U). For U = U_1(N) with N prime to p it is Hecke-equivariant for T_0(p^2)-type and T_0(n,n) operators. Hence V^ord(n,v;U) depends only on v (modulo a subgroup), and is written V^ord(v;U) (or V^ord(v;N)). Proof: reduction to weight 0 via Cor. 8.2.
2. **Thm 9.4, p. 369 (PDF 76) (tex: "control"): MATCH.** U as above and 0 <= q <= 1 (cohomological degree): for each beta > 0 the restriction map induces an isomorphism between the lambda-eigenpiece of V^ord(v;U) and the corresponding ordinary cohomology of the finite-level group, for finite-order characters lambda satisfying a compatibility condition (garbled in OCR). The proof (p. 369) uses Lemma 9.2, Cor. 9.3, and Prop. 8.3 with (9.1) for q = 0. This is the control used by Hsieh for Thm 4.2(2) (p. 32). Remark 9.5 (p. 370) extends it.
3. **Thm 10.1, p. 370 (PDF 77) (tex: "freeness over O[[Z_a]] under p-divisibility"): MATCH.** Sect. 10 is "Co-freeness of V^ord(v;N) over Lambda"; B is a division algebra. Statement: let a be a positive integer, suppose a torsion-freeness hypothesis (on groups printed "T^i(U*)" in the OCR) for all i, and q = 0 or 1. If e H^q(X(U_beta*), L(n,v,lambda; K(lambda)/O(lambda))) is **p-divisible** for all finite order characters lambda (of the type described, with a compatibility condition) and all integer pairs a <= gamma <= beta with beta >= beta(lambda), then the Pontryagin dual of V^ord(v;U) is **free of finite rank over the continuous group algebra O[[Z_a]]** of the group Z_a (the OCR prints "O[[Z,]]"). There is **no "Corollary 10.1"**: Sect. 10 contains Thm 10.1, Lemma 10.2 (finite-order points are Zariski dense in Spec O[[G]], p. 370), Cor. 10.3 (p. 372) and Cor. 10.4 (p. 373). So Hsieh's "[Hid88b, Cor. 10.1]" (Thm 4.2 proof, p. 32) is Thm 10.1: **tex point (10) of `missing/stage-04.md` confirmed.** The p-divisibility hypothesis is exactly what Hsieh checks (p > 3, trivial stabilisers).
4. **Cor. 10.4, p. 373 (PDF 80) (tex: "Lambda-freeness, p > 2"): MATCH.** Suppose r = |I_infty| = 0 or 1 (OCR), P the product of the distinct prime factors of p in the base field, p > 2 and either (10.2a) or (10.2c) "T^i(NP) is torsion free for all i". Then V^ord(v;N) is Lambda-free of finite rank, Lambda = O[[W]] the continuous group algebra of the torsion-free part W of the relevant congruence group Z(N). The proof (pp. 373-374) compares two cofinal filtrations Z_alpha and Z^alpha of Z(N) and uses Thm 9.4 for the second filtration. Cor. 10.3 (p. 372): for r = |I| = 0 or 1, V^ord(v;N) is a free module over a continuous group algebra of a smaller group (a precursor; statement partly garbled).
5. **The pages in the tex are right: p. 362, p. 369, p. 370, p. 373.** Contrary to the note in the tex ("statements only partly legible"), in this file the statements of Thm 8.6, Thm 9.4, Thm 10.1 and Cor. 10.4 are readable enough to identify their shape and hypotheses (above); the exact displayed formulae (indices, congruence conditions on n) are garbled and should be re-read on the page image before being quoted. **No text-layer replacement is needed for the page/theorem checks; one is still desirable for the formulae.**

## B. [Hid88a] (tex does not give a `\lit`; Hsieh cites it for the congruence ideal principle)
6. **Thm 0.1, p. 329 (PDF 8):** h: R^o(N;O) tensor_Lambda I -> I primitive, R the local ring through which h factors. Assume (0.7a) Hom_Lambda(R,Lambda) = R as R-modules (Gorenstein-type), or (0.7b) I a UFD and R(X) free. Then there is a nonzero H in I with C(lambda) = I/(H) (formula lost in OCR), and under (0.8a) k(P) not in {1,2} / (0.8b) a = 2 and N = 1 / (0.8c) a = 1 and Hom_Lambda(R,Lambda) = R, it has the expected specialisation: for each arithmetic P of weight >= 2 a p-adic unit u_p(f_P) with H(P) = (transcendental period ratio) u_p(f_P). (0.7a) proven when N = 1, a = 2 (Mazur) or a not in {1,2} and the residual representation is irreducible (Mazur-Wiles, Tilouine), p. 330. This is the "[Hid88a, Thm 0.1]" quoted by Hsieh p. 4.
7. **pp. 363-366 (Sect. 5, proof of Thm 0.1), p. 364-365 (Hsieh Rem. 7.3 "following [Hid88a, pp. 363-366]"): MATCH.** Under (0.7a), R tensor_Lambda I is isomorphic to Hom(R tensor I, I) as a module, so R(B) (the image in the "other" factor) is dual to the intersection a = R(X) cap R(B)-module and R(X) + R(B) is I-free; choose bases x of R(X) + R(B) and y of R tensor_Lambda I with x.a = y, and take H = det(a); then H generates the congruence module (|C(X_P)| formula via [11, Cor. 10.6]). This is the determinant argument of Hsieh Rem. 7.3.

## C. [Hid93] §7.2 (tex: "Sect. 7.2, Lemma 1, p. 201: lim x^{n!} exists and is idempotent")
8. **Sect. 7.2 ("The projection to the ordinary part"), Lemma 1, p. 201 (PDF 213): MATCH.** K a finite extension of Q_p, O its integer ring: for any commutative O-algebra A of finite rank over O and any x in A, lim_{n} x^{n!} exists in A and is an idempotent (proof: first A the p-adic integer ring of a finite extension, using |(A/p)^x| = p^{f-1}...; then the semisimple case; then Wedderburn decomposition x = s + n with n nilpotent). The tex's "apply to A = O[T] contained in End M" is a correct application. (Proposition 1 on the same page is about eigenforms being complex eigenforms; Lemma 1 follows it.)

## D. [Hid06] Lemma 3.5 (quoted by Hsieh p. 30; "low" item of `missing/stage-04.md`)
9. **Sect. 3.1, p. 169 (PDF 183), pairing and Lemma 3.5:** for a totally real F and level N with condition (sm1) ([F(mu_p):F] > 2, or an inert prime of F(mu_p)/F divides N), a perfect pairing (.,.)_N exists (by Lemma 3.3), and <f,g>_N := (f, g|[tau(N)])_N is perfect on M(N,eps;A) (sentence just before the lemma); **Lemma 3.5:** T(l) is self-adjoint for this pairing (proof pp. 169-170). So Hsieh's "Hecke equivariant and perfect (cf. [Hid06, Lemma 3.5])" is supported: equivariance (self-adjointness) is Lemma 3.5, perfectness is the preceding sentence of p. 169. This was the open "low priority" item in `plan/staged/missing/stage-04.md`: it is now verified for F = Q (Hsieh's case, where (sm1) is p > 3).
