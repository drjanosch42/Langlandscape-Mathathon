## Items (all on p. 250 = PDF 21)
1. **Thm 3.1, p. 250 (tex: "Lambda-freeness of T(M)_m is [Hi86b, Thm 3.1] (p >= 5)"): MATCH.** Assume p >= 5 and p divides N. Then the ordinary parts of the universal Hecke algebras (the full one and the cuspidal one) are free of finite rank over the Iwasawa algebra Lambda_K; their ranks equal the rank of the weight-one-type algebra at level N_0 (statement "rank over Lambda of the Hecke algebra at level (N, a) = the rank in weight k >= 3 at level N_0 p"; garbled in the OCR). So the hypothesis is "N divisible by p" (tame level times p); the tex uses it for tame level M times p.
2. **Cor. 3.2, p. 250: MATCH.** Control: the natural map gives isomorphisms (3.3a) T^o(N,a)/P_k T^o(N,a) = T^o_k(Gamma_1(N p^r), a) for k >= 3 with k = a mod p-1, and (3.3b) the analogue for k = 2 (cusp forms), where P_k is the polynomial (1+X) - (1+p)^k in Lambda (no shift; read on the page image by the S06 audit part 2, §6). Comparison with the chapter (S06-M10b, from audit part 2 §6): by (3.1), p. 248, and p. 249 (l acts by l^2 T(l,l)), Hida's Lambda-structure lets z act on weight k by z^k<z>, i.e. it is our GL_2-side structure twisted by [u] -> u^2[u] (no inversion). Hence Hida's P_k and our P_k = ker([u] -> u^{k-2}) are the same weight-k point of the Hecke algebra; as an ideal of the abstract Lambda, Hida's P_k is our P_{k+2}. This is the control used in tex `thm:s06-defect` ("Cor. 3.2 (3.3b)", p. 250). The OCR of the conditions after "if" is partly illegible.
3. **Cor. 3.3, p. 250: MATCH with the tex's reading.** The intersection of the "old" ideal (defined in (3.4a-d) as the annihilator of the old forms, sums over 1 < t | N_0) with the nilradical of the Hecke algebra is zero, and the ideal is free of finite rank over Lambda_K. So Cor. 3.3 is about the old *ideal* and its Lambda-freeness, not about the Hecke algebra, as the tex says ("[Hi86b, Cor. 3.3, p. 250] gives Lambda-freeness of the old ideal, not of T''"). The tex also says that Hida's old ideal is the annihilator of the forms old at any divisor t > 1 of the tame level (3.4a-d): MATCH (the sums run over 1 < t | N_0).
4. Hida says (end of p. 250) that Thm 3.1 and Cors 3.2, 3.3 are proved in Sect. 5. Thm 3.5 (p. 251) and Thm 3.6 (p. 252) concern the structure over the quotient field and the pseudo-null/torsion module; not used.
