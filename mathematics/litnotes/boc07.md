## Items
1. **Cor. 4.3, p. 39 (PDF 16) (cited by [KO, Thm 3.12]: MATCH).** For the presentation 0 -> J^(eta) -> O[[T_1..T_h]] -> R^(eta)_{S,O} -> 0 of the universal deformation ring, the number of variables minus the number of relations is bounded below: h^(eta) - gen(J^(eta)) >= h^0(G_{F,S}, ad rho-bar) - h^0(G_{F,S}, (ad rho-bar)^dual) - delta(G_{F,S}, ad rho-bar)^(eta) + sum over the local places nu in S of (h_nu^(eta) + delta(G_nu, ad rho-bar)^(eta) - h^0(G_nu, ad rho-bar) - gen(J_nu^(eta))). This is formula (4.5), a global-to-local Euler-characteristic estimate; Thm 4.2 (p. 37-38, PDF 14-15) is the presentation theorem behind it.
2. **Thm 5.8, pp. 49-50 (PDF 26-27) (cited by [KO, Thm 3.12]: MATCH).** F totally real, rho-bar odd, and: (i) at places nu above the residue characteristic and infinity, the local deformation problem satisfies h^eta_nu - h^0(G_nu, ad^0) - gen(J_nu^eta) >= 0 (resp. condition at the places above the residue characteristic); (ii) at the infinite places one of the versal hulls of Example 5.4 is used according to rho-bar(c_nu); (iii) at places above the residue characteristic either F_nu = Q_p, rho-bar satisfies the requirements of 5.5 case I and Def_nu is the functor of flat deformations, or rho-bar is ordinary with delta_{nu,unr} = 0 and Def_nu is ordinary deformations with fixed determinant; (iv) h^0(G_{F,S}, (ad^0 rho-bar)^dual) = 0. Then R^eta_{S,O} has a presentation O[[T_1..T_n]]/(f_1..f_n) (same number of variables and relations), i.e. is a complete intersection (after the flatness part of [KO, Lemma 3.15]). Proof uses Props 5.6 and Example 5.4, 5.7 (p. 48-49).
3. **KO's use (compare):** [KO, Thm 3.12, p. 12] states conditions (1) l not p: dim L_{l,1} - dim H^0(Q_l, ad^0) - gen(a_l) >= 0; (2) p: rho low weight crystalline and the same inequality at p; (3) (TW); conclusion: R is a complete intersection. Thus KO's (1)-(2) are Bockle's condition (i)-(iii), and (3) gives (iv) because H^0(ad^0 rho-bar dual) = 0 under (TW). Consistent.
