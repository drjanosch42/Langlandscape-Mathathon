## Details per source

### Voight, Quaternion Algebras (GTM 288)
- **Thm 25.1.1, p.415 [PDF 422]**: for a maximal order in a definite quaternion algebra over Q of discriminant D, the sum of 1/w_J over the
  class set equals phi(D)/12, with w_J the half-order of the unit group of the left order. Next page (p.416) refers to Thm 11.5.14 for w_J <= 24.
  Also Thm 25.3.18 (p.423, Eichler orders: phi(D)psi(M)/12). OK.
- **Thm 30.1.5, p.543 [PDF 548]**: Eichler class number formula over Q for an Eichler order of level M in disc. D: mass term phi(D)psi(M)/12
  plus 1/4 and 1/3 times correction products e_2, e_3 that depend on (-4/p), (-3/p) over p | D and p | M, vanishing if 4 | N (resp. 9 | N). OK.
- **Exercise 30.6, p.566 [PDF 571]**: specialise 30.1.5 to D = p, M = 1 and obtain the classical class numbers by residue of p mod 12. OK.
- **Thm 11.5.14, p.176 [PDF 191]**: unit group of any order in a definite quaternion algebra over Q (a,b<0): cyclic of order 2, 4, 6, or
  quaternion of order 8, binary dihedral of order 12, binary tetrahedral of order 24; the last three occur exactly for Lipschitz, the order (11.5.12), Hurwitz. OK.
- **Thm 28.2.3**: SL_2(Q) is dense in SL_2 of the finite adeles. The *statement* is on p.480 [PDF 486]; the proof (via SL_2(Z) -> SL_2(Z/m) onto,
  Thm 28.2.6, and Lemma 28.2.4) is on p.481 [PDF 487]. ST 346 says p.479: WRONG (p.479 precedes the statement). CH 5a 39/46 says p.481 (the proof page); acceptable, sharper "p.480 (proof p.481)".

### Serre, Trees
- **II.1.4 Thm 2, p.78 [PDF 87]**: if the closure of G in GL(V) contains SL(V), the segment A--A' (lattice L subset L' of index 1 over the local field) is a fundamental domain
  for G on the tree (transitivity on vertices of each parity via invariant factors and openness of GL_2(O); on edges via congruence subgroup).
  Proof continues p.79. OK.
- **Thm 3, p.79 [PDF 88]**: under (D) G is the amalgam of G_A and G_A' over G_AA'; follows from Thm 2 and I.4.1 Thm 6. OK.
- **Cor 1 (Ihara) p.79**: SL_2(K) = SL_2(O) *_Gamma SL_2(O). **Cor 2, p.80 [PDF 89]**: SL_2(Z[1/p]) = SL_2(Z) *_{Gamma_0(p)} SL_2(Z) (taking A = Z[1/p]). 
  **p.81 [PDF 90]**: the second factor is the group of matrices (a, p^{-1}b; pc, d) (conjugate copy of SL_2(Z)); presentations for p = 2, 3. OK.
- **I.4.2(c), p.35 [PDF 44]**: SL_2(Z) acts on a tree with fundamental segment PQ, stabilisers Z/6, Z/4 (vertices), Z/2 (edge), recovering Z/4 *_{Z/2} Z/6. OK.

### Brown, Cohomology of Groups (GTM 87)
- **II.7 Cor 7.7, p.51 [PDF 62]**: Mayer-Vietoris sequence for H_n of an amalgam G = G1 *_A G2 with trivial coefficients. OK.
- **III.6 Exercise, p.74 [PDF 85]** (end of III.6): (a) obtain MV from the permutation-module sequence 0 -> Z[G/A] -> Z[G/G1] + Z[G/G2] -> Z -> 0 (II.7.8);
  (b) apply -(x)M and Hom(-,M) to get MV with coefficients in homology and cohomology. This is the MV-with-coefficients source. OK.
- **III.8, p.80 [PDF 91]**: conjugation c(g): H^*(gHg^-1, M) -> H^*(H, M) via the category of pairs; **(8.3)** says h in H acts trivially on H^*(H,M). OK.
- **III.9, p.81-83**: transfer; **(9.5)(ii) p.82 [PDF 93]**: cor o res = multiplication by the index (iii) double coset formula (p.82-83, [PDF 93-94]); **Exercise 1, p.83**: res, cor commute with conjugation. OK.

### Ribet 1990 (Invent. Math. 100; scan, OCR)
- **Prop 2.2, Thm 2.3, p.436 [PDF 7]**: character group X of the torus = kernel of Z^F -> D (degree-0 chains), monodromy pairing is the restriction of the
  Euclidean pairing, Phi = coker(X -> Hom(X,Z)). Pages 437-438 [PDF 8-9]: admissible curves (chains of P^1 with thickness e(i)), the pairing is
  weighted by e(i), Thm 2.4. OK.
- **Prop 3.1, p.439 [PDF 10]**: for X_0(Mq) mod q, X = degree-0 divisors on the supersingular set Sigma(M) (graph has two vertices, edges = supersingular points); e(i) = #Aut/2 via DR VI 6.9 (p.439).
  **Prop 3.3, p.440 [PDF 11]**: Sigma(M) is in bijection with H^x\ H_f^x / R_f^x (ideal classes of the Eichler order). OK.
- **Thm 3.12, p.449 [PDF 20]**: n_r = T_r - (1+r) kills Phi for every prime r prime to qM (proof pp.449-450 divisibility of off-diagonal Brandt entries by e(i), Rem 3.13 p.450). OK.
- **Thm 3.15, p.451 [PDF 22]**: the degeneracy map L -> X (+) X of the X_0(pqM) character group is surjective, proved with Eichler's results (Lemmas 3.16-3.17, p.451-452). OK.
- **Rem 3.18, p.452 [PDF 23]**: another proof of 3.15 via graph connectivity, suggested independently by Livne and Edixhoven. OK.

### Mazur (Publ. IHES 47), Appendix by Mazur-Rapoport
- **Thm A.1(b), p.173 [PDF 142]**: for N = p prime the fibre of the Neron model of J_0(p) at p is J^0 x C, C cyclic of order num((p-1)/12) generated by (0)-(inf). OK.
- **Thm 1.1 of the appendix, pp.175-176 [PDF 144-145]**: structure of X_0(N) mod p: two copies of M_0(N') meeting at supersingular points, local type A_{k-1} with k = |Aut(E,H)|/2 (smooth outside the supersingular points); (d) on p.176: the minimal model replaces a crossing point by a chain of k-1 projective lines (self-intersection -2); for p >= 5, k > 1 only for j = 0 (k = 3), j = 1728 (k = 2). OK.

### Diamond-Taylor, Non-optimal levels (Invent. Math. 115)
- **pp.439-440 [PDF 5-6]**: definition of an Eisenstein maximal ideal (reducibility of rho_m, with T_p, S_p congruent to p^a(1+p), p^{2a}). OK.
- **Lemma 2, p.445-446 [PDF 11-12]** (Ihara-analogue for definite quaternion algebras): kernel A of the map (f1,f2) -> f1 + eta_p f2 into forms on U_0(p) mod l; T_q - 2 and S_q - 1 kill A[l] for almost all q = 1 mod some d; proof by strong approximation (p.446). OK. 
- **Cor 1, p.446 [PDF 12]**: maximal ideals in the support of the cokernel/kernel modules are Eisenstein; proved by duality. *Suggestion*: 4i 285 cites Lemma 2 for "Eisenstein support"; Cor 1 (p.446) is the statement actually matching the Eisenstein conclusion; cite "Lemma 2, Cor 1, pp.445-446".
- **Adjunction formula, p.446 [PDF 12]**: perfect pairing on weight-k quaternionic forms with the factor (det(g_l) ||det g||)^{k-2}; ([UgV]f1,f2)_V = ||det g||^{2-k} (f1,[V g^-1 U] f2)_U. OK.

### Deligne-Rapoport, VI 6.9
- **Th. 6.9(iii), pp.286-287 [PDF 144-145]**: at a supersingular point x of the special fibre given by (E, alpha), put k = #Aut(E,alpha)/2 if -1 is in Aut, else #Aut(E,alpha); the complete local ring of the Gamma_0(p)-model is W(F_p-bar)[[x,y]]/(xy - p^k) (singularity A_{k-1}, formula (6.9.1)). Page 286 ends with the statement of (iii), page 287 gives its conclusion. ST 159 has no page. OK.

### Ribet 1983, Invent. Math. 71 (scan)
- **Thm (1.4), p.195 [PDF 4]**: for a rational decomposition S = X (+) Y of weight-k cusp forms on Gamma(N), a "large" prime (prime to N, at least k) is a congruence prime iff it divides the index (L~ : L) of the integral parabolic-cohomology lattice L in the direct sum of its projections. Not about level raising. OK.

### Ribet 1984 (ICM Warsaw; scan, image-only)
- Readable (OCR + visual check of p.509). **p.506 [PDF 4], formula (2.2)**: the necessary condition for level raising from level N to NM (M prime): a_M^2 = (1+M)^2 mod lambda;
  the paper then asks for the converse. **p.509, Prop 3.3 [PDF 7]**: the kernel of eta = T_M^2 - 1 on J_0(N)^2 equals Delta (the pairing subgroup);
  the matrix a'∘a = [[M+1, tau],[tau, M+1]] with tau = T_M. OK as cited in 5a 177; ST 382/573 ("missing") and `MISSING_LITERATURE.md` item 4 are outdated: the file
  `ribet_1984_congruence-relations-between-modular-forms_scan.pdf` is present (12-page scan, printed pp.503-514). Only OCR-verifiable on pp.506, 509. (Note that scan PDF page = printed page - 502.)

### Manning-Shotton 2019 (arXiv 1907.06043)
- **Thm 1.1, p.2 [PDF 2]**: for l > 2 and image of rho_m containing a conjugate of SL_2(F_l) (plus a Taylor-Wiles condition if l = 5), Ihara's lemma at p for the Shimura curve X_K localized at m holds. Strategy: patching, bypassing the congruence subgroup property. OK.

### Hida 1993 (Elementary theory of L-functions and Eisenstein series)
- **6.3, pp.175-177 [PDF 187-189]**: Hecke operators [Gamma alpha Gamma'] on group cohomology via coset decompositions with alpha^iota = det(alpha) alpha^{-1}; Eichler-Shimura map is a Hecke-module map (p.177); **Thm 1, p.177**: the Eichler-Shimura isomorphism S_k(Gamma_0(N),chi) (+) conj = H^1_par(Gamma_0(N), L(n,chi;C)) is Hecke equivariant. OK.
