1. **Thm 6.6, p. 23 (tex: "Thm 6.6, p. 23"): MATCH.** For rho: G_F -> GL_2(F_p) continuous satisfying (LI_Ind rho) and (Mod_rho), and Sigma a finite set of primes containing P_rho: pi_Sigma: R_Sigma -> T_Sigma is an isomorphism of complete intersections over O, M_Sigma is free of finite rank over T_Sigma (so all Sigma-ramified deformations are modular), and for Hilbert newforms f with rho_{f,p} a Sigma-ramified deformation, #H^1_Sigma(F, Ad^0 rho_{f,p} tensor Q_p/Z_p) = #(O/eta_f^Sigma) < infinity (the statement follows the proof by induction on #Sigma, starting from Sigma = P_rho using Thm 5.1 / Prop. 5.5, p. 23). Thm 6.6 is used in [KO, Thm 2.9].
