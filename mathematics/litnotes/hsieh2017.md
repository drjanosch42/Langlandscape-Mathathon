## Items (tex place -> result)
1. **Sect. 4.4, Def. 4.1, p. 31 (Pk/diamond comparison, `def:s04-Pk` justification): MATCH.** X_alpha = D^x \ D^_x / U_1(N p^alpha); Lambda acts on O[X_alpha] by <z>_Lambda x := x.diag(z,z), z in 1+pZ_p (same page, a few lines before Def. 4.1); Delta = (Z/pN^+Z)^x acts by diamonds sigma_d. Def. 4.1: S^D(N,Lambda) = functions f on X_infty = D^x \ D^_x / U_1(N p^infty) to Lambda with f(xz) = f(x) <z>^2 <z>_Lambda^{-1} for z in 1+pZ_p, factoring through X_alpha modulo P_alpha = ((1+T)^{p^alpha} - 1) for alpha large. The tex's "f(xz)=f(x)<z>^2<z>_Lambda^{-1}" is exactly this.
2. **(4.6), p. 31 (tex: "his forms are Lambda-duals twisted by iota_2 ((4.6), p. 31)"): MATCH.** S^D(N,Lambda) = lim_alpha Hom_Lambda(O[X_alpha], Lambda/P_alpha) tensor_{Lambda, iota_2} Lambda, with iota_2(T) = (1+T)^{-2}(1+p)^2 - 1. Hecke action tf(x) = f(tx).
3. **Homological U_p, p. 31 (tex: "x -> sum_b x.(varpi_p b; 0 1), p. 31, i.e. N Up"): MATCH.** U_q x := sum over b in Z_q/qZ_q of x.(varpi_q b; 0 1) for q | N^+ p (and U_q x = x varpi^D_q for q | N^-), on O[X_alpha] (p. 31). e = lim U_p^{n!}. Note: on forms (p. 30) U_p f(x) = sum_b rho_{kappa,p}(...) f(x.(varpi_p b;0 1)), the cohomological version.
4. **Thm 4.2 (Control theorem) and proof, p. 32 (tex: "Thm 4.2 and its proof, p. 32: lim_alpha e O[X_alpha] is Lambda[Delta_p]-free, via [Hid88b, Cor. 10.1]"; "Thm 4.2(2), p. 32 via [Hid88b, Thm 9.4]"): MATCH (with the known renaming).** Statement (p. 32): for p > 3, N_chi = sum chi(d) sigma_d, P_chi the ideal of Lambda[Delta] generated by chi(d)sigma_d - 1: (1) S^D(N,chi,I) is a free I-module and N_chi: eS^D(N,I)/P_chi -> eS^D(N,chi,I) is an isomorphism; (2) for each arithmetic point Q of weight k_Q there is a Hecke-equivariant isomorphism eS^D(N,chi,I) tensor I/p_Q = eS^D_{k_Q}(N p^alpha, chi omega^{2-k_Q} eps_Q; O(Q)), alpha = max(1, c_p(eps_Q)), f mod p_Q -> f_Q, Q(f(x)) = <f_Q(x), X^{k_Q-2}>. Proof (sketch): reduce to I = Lambda; V^ord(N) := lim_alpha lim_beta e H^0(X_alpha, p^{-beta}O/O) is Hida's V_0^ord(0;U_1(N^+)) of [Hid88b, Thm 8.6]; its Pontryagin dual is lim e O[X_alpha]; since p > 3 the stabilisers D^x cap xU_1(Np^alpha)x^{-1} are trivial, so H^0(X_alpha, xi, K/O_K) is p-divisible, and "the Lambda[Delta_p]-freeness follows from [Hid88b, Corollary 10.1] (and the proof therein)"; (2) uses [Hid88b, Thm 9.4] and the map iota of [Hid88b, (8.10)] with explicit description at "[Hid88b, lines 9-11, p. 375]". Hida's paper has no Corollary 10.1: the statement used is Thm 10.1 (p. 370) (checked in `hida1988.md`), as `missing/stage-04.md` point 10 says. Also: the freeness assertion in (1) is for S^D(N,chi,I), as the tex claims.
5. **Sect. 4.2 "Inner products", p. 30 (tex: "finite-level version <f_1,f_2>_N = (f_1,[tau^D_N] f_2)_N, Sect. 4.3, p. 30"): PAGE MATCH, SECTION MISMATCH.** The pairing (f_1,f_2)_N (sum over [x] of <f_1(x),f_2(x)>_kappa eps_cyc(nu(x))^kappa (#Gamma_{N,x})^{-1}), the Atkin-Lehner involution [tau^D_N] and the new pairing <f_1,f_2>_N := (f_1,[tau^D_N] f_2)_N are in the paragraph "Inner products" of **Sect. 4.2** (p. 29-30; needs 6 kappa! invertible in A). Sect. 4.3 ("Automorphic forms on definite quaternion algebras") begins lower on the same page 30. Hsieh says it is "well known that this new pairing is Hecke equivariant and perfect (cf. [Hid06, Lemma 3.5])". Correct the section number in the tex to 4.2.
6. **(4.1), p. 29 (tex: "#Gamma_{N,x} := #((B^x cap x R^x x^{-1}) Q^x / Q^x), (4.1), p. 29"): MATCH.** The integration formula: integral over A^x D^x \ D^x(A) of f dtau x = vol(R_N^x) sum over [x] in D^x \ D^_x / R_N^x of f(x) (#Gamma_{N,x})^{-1}, with Gamma_{N,x} := (D^x cap x R_N^x x^{-1}) Q^x/Q^x. (Tamagawa measure with total volume 2; vol(R_N^x) from Eichler's mass formula (4.2).) The identification "Gamma_{N,x} = our w_x" is the tex's own, not Hsieh's.
7. **Sect. 4.4 "A perfect pairing" (p. 32) and Def. 4.3, pp. 32-33 (tex: "Sect. 4.4, 'A perfect pairing', and Def. 4.3, pp. 32-33"): MATCH.** (Hsieh's own heading is spelt "A perfect paring".) For f, f' in eS^D(N,chi,I): B_{N,alpha}(f,f') := sum over [x] in X_0(N p^alpha) of U_p^{-alpha} f(x tau^D_{Np^alpha}) f'(x) chi_I(nu(x)) (#Gamma_{Np^alpha,x})^{-1} mod P_alpha (p. 32 bottom), with chi_I(z) = chi(z)<eps_cyc(z)>^{-2}<eps_cyc(z)>_I, compatible in alpha. Def. 4.3 (p. 33): B_N := lim B_{N,alpha}, a Hecke-equivariant I-bilinear pairing eS^D(N,chi,I)^2 -> I; for k_Q = 2, B_N(f,f')(Q) = <U_p^{-alpha} f_Q, f'_Q>_{Np^alpha}, "this in particular implies B_N is perfect". The tex's formula for B_{N,alpha} matches (the tex sums over X_0(N p^alpha) and uses tau^D_{Np^alpha}).
8. **Lemma 4.4, p. 33: MATCH.** For each arithmetic point Q and alpha >= max(1, c_p(eps_Q)): B_N(f,f')(Q) = (-1)^{k_Q} <U_p^{-alpha} f_Q, f'_Q>_{Np^alpha}. So the sign (-1)^k of the tex is right (the weight-2 case of Def. 4.3 has sign +1).
9. **Sect. 1.4, Hypothesis (CR,Sigma), p. 4 (`hyp:s04-CR`): MATCH.** See `hs.md` item 1 of branch `abhijit/s06-m11`; rho-bar_F absolutely irreducible, p-distinguished, ramified at every l in Sigma with l = 1 mod p (not -1), Sigma a subset of the support of N_F. (Tex: Sigma = supp N^- as in Thm 4.5.) The "non-Eisenstein implied by absolute irreducibility" remark is the tex's. The p-distinguished definition is spelled out on the same page (restriction to the decomposition group at p, semisimplified, is chi^+ + chi^- with chi^+ not = chi^- mod m_I).
10. **Thm 4.5 and proof, p. 34 (`thm:s04-Gor`): MATCH.** Statement: under (CR, supp(N^-)), eS^D(N,I)[lambda^D_f] is free of rank one over I; a generator f^D is the primitive JL lift (unique up to I^x). Proof (not statement): m = max ideal of T^D(N,I) over Ker lambda^D_f; under (CR), eS^D(N,I)_m is free of rank one over T^D(N,I)_m "in virtue of [Wil95, Thm 2.1] and [Hel07, Cor. 8.11 and Rem. 8.12] and Hida's control theorem (cf. [PW11, Prop. 6.4, 6.5])"; with Thm 4.2(1), eS^D(N,chi,I)_m is free of rank one over T^D(N,chi,I)_m, hence T^D(N,chi,I)_m is Gorenstein (it carries the perfect equivariant pairing B_N); then the eigenline is free of rank one. Matches the tex ("freeness of Mord_m is in the proof, not the statement"). Note: hypotheses of Hsieh p. 34 do not include "p > 3, p does not divide MD", but Thm 4.2 (used in the proof) needs p > 3; the tex adds p > 3 and p not dividing MD, consistent.
11. **Def. 4.12, p. 43 (`thm:s04-etaB`): MATCH.** F primitive I-adic Hida family satisfying (CR, Sigma^-), F^D a primitive JL lift with tame conductor N_F = N^- N_F^+; eta_{F^D} := B_{N_F}(F^D,F^D) in I (using the pairing of Def. 4.3); then the Gross period Omega_{F^D_Q} is defined from eta_{F^D_Q} (formula with ||F_Q^o||^2, the modified Euler factor E_p(F_Q,Ad), and the prime-to-Sigma^- root number). Hsieh says (p. 43) that by Rem. 7.8 the canonical period is an integral multiple of the Gross period.
12. **Rem. 7.3, p. 67 (`thm:s04-etaB`, principle "congruence ideal = self-pairing"): MATCH.** (Generalities after [Hid88a, pp. 363-366].) R a domain, T a finite reduced R-algebra with lambda: T -> R, C(lambda) := lambda(T[lambda]) = lambda(Ann_T(Ker lambda)); H a free T-module of rank d, T Gorenstein, with a perfect Hecke-equivariant pairing < , >: H x H -> R (<tx,y> = <x,ty>). Then T[lambda] is free of rank one over R, H[lambda] is free of rank d with basis e_1..e_d, and C(lambda)^d = (det <e_i,e_j>). Note: the printed statement is the d-th power: for d = 1 it is the self-pairing equality. The check that the same principle is in Hida's [Hid88a, Sect. 5, pp. 363-366] was done in `hida1988.md`; DDT Lemma 4.17 (preprint p. 126) is the finite-level version.
13. **Rem. 7.8, p. 71 (`thm:s04-etaB`, "(eta^B_F) contains (eta_F)"): MATCH.** If f satisfies (CR,Sigma^-), eta_{f^D} generates the congruence ideal of lambda_f on T^D(N,psi,I), which is the N^--new quotient of T(N,psi,I); consequently (eta_{f^D}) contains (eta_f) and equality holds if rho-bar_f is ramified at all l in Sigma^-. Matches the tex's "with equality if rho-bar_m is ramified at all l | D".
14. **PW11 pieces quoted in Hsieh's proof of Thm 4.5 (tex: Prop. 6.4, p. 19; Prop. 6.5, p. 20):** MATCH, see `pw.md` (branch `abhijit/s06-m11`): Prop. 6.4 (Selmer/size of X_r(J)_f/xi^* X_r(A) and self-pairing = congruence number when X_r(J) is free) and Prop. 6.5 (canonical iso Pic(X_{N^+,N^-}) tensor O with X_r(N^+r,N^-/r)); arXiv numbering of PW (v1).
