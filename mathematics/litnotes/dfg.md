## Items
1. **Thm 0.2 (= Thm 3.7), journal p. 666 (PDF 4) (tex: "Thm 0.2, p. 4"): MATCH on number; PAGE differs in kind.** The tex "p. 4" is the PDF page; the printed page is 666. Statement (on that page): f a newform of weight k >= 2 and level N with coefficients in K; Sigma a finite set of primes containing those dividing N; lambda a prime of K not in S_f and not dividing any prime in Sigma. Then the Sigma-imprimitive Selmer group H^1_Sigma(G_Q, A_f,lambda / ...) has O_{K,lambda}-length v_lambda(eta_f^Sigma), where eta_f^Sigma (Sect. 1.7.3) is the generalised congruence ideal of Hida and Wiles (it measures the failure of the pairing on M_f to be perfect on a sublattice). Thm 3.7 is its Sect. 3 form (the proof ends on PDF p. 48 = journal p. 710). Thm 0.1 (= Thm 2.15) (same page) is the lambda-part of the Bloch-Kato conjecture for A_f and A_f(1) for lambda not in S_f.
2. Sect. 1.7.3 (cohomology congruence ideal) and Prop. 1.4(c) (the adjoint L-factor formula used in [KO, Prop. 5.6]) were not re-read here; they are cited through [KO].
