## 1. Hypothesis (CR, Sigma), Sect. 1.4, p. 4 (tex: `rem:s06-CR`, "Sect. 1.4, p. 4"): MATCH
Hypothesis (CR,Sigma) for a primitive Hida family F: the residual representation is absolutely irreducible and p-distinguished
(restriction to the decomposition group at p is a sum of two characters that differ mod m_I), and it is ramified at every l in Sigma
with l = 1 mod p. The congruence is +1 only (not -1). Directly after the definition (same page) Hsieh says that, under (CR), the local
Hecke algebra is Gorenstein "by [MW86, Prop. 2, Sect. 9] and [Wil95, Cor. 2, p. 482]", and that Hida ([Hid88a, Thm 0.1]) then shows the
congruence module is I/(eta_F). This is the source of the MW86 reference in tex `rem:s06-CR`/(Gor_R). Matches tex.

## 2. Section 3.3, pp. 17-18 (tex: "(Gor_R) is [Hs, Sect. 3.3, p. 18]"): MATCH (section starts on p. 17)
Sect. 3.3 starts at the bottom of p. 17 (Hecke algebras T(N,I)). The Hypothesis (CR) and the sentence "T_{m_f} is Gorenstein by
[Wil95, Cor. 2, p. 482]; with this Hida [Hid88a] proves that C(f) is generated by one element eta_f" are on p. 18. Also on p. 18: the
congruence ideal C(f) is defined by lambda_f(Ann_{T_m}(Ker lambda_f)), and (3.1) T_m tensor Frac I = Frac I x B (cites [Hid88b, Cor. 3.7]).
Also: the control isomorphism T(N,I)/P_{Q,chi} = T_k(...) is quoted from [Hid88b, Thm 3.4]. So the page in the tex (p. 18) is right; for the
section heading use "Sect. 3.3, pp. 17-18".

## 3. Section 7.2, p. 67-68, proof of Lemma 7.4 (tex: "Sect. 7.2, p. 68; proof of Lemma 7.4"): MATCH
Sect. 7.2 (comparison of the canonical periods of a Hida family with its twists) starts on p. 67 together with Remark 7.3 (generalities on
congruence ideals, after [Hid88a, pp. 363-366]: for T Gorenstein, H free of rank d with a perfect Hecke-equivariant pairing,
C(lambda)^d = det of the Gram matrix on H[lambda]) and Lemma 7.4 (C(f#) = C(f) E_q(f), E_q = 1 - q^{-2} if pi_q is unramified special,
(q-1)(a_q^2 - psi(q)(1+q)^2) if q does not divide N, 1 - q^{-1} if ramified principal series, 1 if supercuspidal). The proof continues on
p. 68: with H_p(M) = lim H^1_et(X_1(Mp^n), Z_p) tensor O, "by [Wil95, Cor. 1 and 2, p. 482], H_p(M)_m is free of rank two over T(M,I)_m and
T(M,I)_m is Gorenstein under (CR)". So the freeness input quoted in tex (Prop. galois, cited input (1)) sits on p. 68. Matches.
Side remark: E_q = 1 - q^{-2} for unramified special is the factor used in tex `rem:s06-minus1`(b).

## 4. Theorem 4.5 and proof, p. 34 (tex: "(Gor_{R'}) = if:s04-Mord ([Hs, Thm 4.5 and proof, p. 34])"): MATCH
Statement (p. 34): if f satisfies (CR, supp N^-), then the I-module eS^D(N,I)[lambda_f^D] is free of rank one (the primitive JL lift f^D, unique up
to I^x). The proof (same page, no Thm number for the next lines): put m = max ideal of T^D(N,I) over Ker lambda_f^D; under (CR) the module
eS^D(N,I)_m is free of rank one over T^D(N,I)_m "in virtue of [Wil95, Thm 2.1] and [Hel07, Cor. 8.11, Rem. 8.12] and Hida's control theorem
(cf. [PW11, Prop. 6.4, 6.5])"; with Thm 4.2(1) (control) one gets eS^D(N,chi,I)_m free of rank one over T^D(N,chi,I)_m, and this is Gorenstein
because the module carries the perfect Hecke-equivariant pairing B_N. Hence the eigenline is free of rank one. So the Gorenstein property and freeness
over T_m are in the proof, not in the statement (as stage 04 point (4) says). Matches tex. Note the proof takes (CR) to mean (CR, supp N^-) in Hsieh's
hypothesis of Thm 4.5.

## 5. Remark 7.8, p. 71 (tex: "[Hs, Rem. 7.8, p. 71]: T^D is the N^--new quotient"; tex `if:s04-etaB`): MATCH
If f satisfies (CR, Sigma^-), then eta_{f^D} generates the congruence ideal of lambda_f on T^D(N,psi,I); T^D(N,psi,I) is isomorphic to the N^--new quotient
of T(N,psi,I); hence the congruence ideal (eta_{f^D}) contains (eta_f), with equality if rho_f mod m_I is ramified at all l in Sigma^-. (Improves
[CH18b, Prop. 6.1] by weakening (CR+) to (CR, Sigma^-).) Matches tex: Thm E gives eta_F inside eta^B_F, which is this inclusion.

## 6. Section 4.5, p. 34 (tex S7: "Hsieh's opposite sign, Sect. 4.5, p. 34"): MATCH
The JL map JL: T(N,I) -> T^D(N,I) sends U_q to (-1) U_q for q | N^- (and U_q to U_q for q | N^+ p, T_q to T_q, diamonds to diamonds); lambda_f factors
through JL when supp N^- is inside the set Sigma_0^f. So Hsieh's sign at q | N^- is minus, as the tex claims.
