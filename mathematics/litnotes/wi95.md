## Items
1. **Thm 2.1, journal p. 482 (PDF 40; tex: "Thm 2.1 and Cor. 2, p. 482"): MATCH.** Let T = T_H(N) (resp. T_H(Np)), m a maximal ideal. (i) If p does not divide N and rho_m is irreducible, then J_H(N)(Q)[m] is
   two-dimensional over T/m. (ii) If p does not divide N, rho_m is irreducible and m is D_p-distinguished, then J_H(Np)(Q)[m] is two-dimensional over T/m (m a maximal ideal of T = T_H(Np)). (The symbol between "p" and "N" is lost in the text layer; read as "p does not divide N" in both cases.) The text says it is due to Mazur in prime level and generalised by Ribet, Gross, Edixhoven and others.
2. **Cor. 1, p. 482: MATCH (tex: "[Wi95, Cor. 1, 2, p. 482]" via Hs p. 68).** In case (i) (resp. (ii)): J_H(N)(Q)_m (resp. J_H(Np)(Q)_m) is free of rank 2 over T_m, and so is the m-adic Tate module. This is the freeness of H(M)_m of rank two over T(M)_m used in `prop:s06-galois` input (1) (stage 06, Hsieh p. 68).
3. **Cor. 2, p. 482: MATCH.** In either case (i) or (ii), T_m is a Gorenstein ring (follows from Cor. 1 by duality (2.4)). Used for (Gor_R) (tex "[Wi95, Cor. 2 p. 482]"; Hs p. 4, p. 18). The Gorenstein notion is the Z_p-algebra notion of the appendix, which coincides with the ring notion for finite flat local Z_p-algebras.

## Mismatches / remarks
- No mismatch. The hypotheses carry over to the tex: Wiles needs rho_m irreducible (case (i)), and additionally p-distinguished at p for level Np (case (ii)); tex (CR) gives both.
- The weight is two and the level Gamma_0-type (T_H, H = Hecke algebra of the group Gamma_H); passing to Lambda-adic statements is done by [Hs] with control (stage-06 `lem:s06-G`), as the tex says.
