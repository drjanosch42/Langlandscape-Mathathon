## Items
1. **Sect. 9 ("p-adic Hodge structure", starts p. 251), Prop. 2, p. 254 (PDF 25) (tex "[MW86, Sect. 9 Prop. 2]"; Hs p. 4: "[MW86, Prop. 2, Sect. 9]"): MATCH.** Setting: T the Hecke algebra of Sect. 8, W the module of Sect. 8, P a prime of T, T_P the completion of the localisation, W_P = W tensor T_P. Four conditions are equivalent (stated just before, p. 254): (a) dimension 2 (of the relevant fibre), (b) the semisimplification of W tensor F is a residual representation attached to P, (c) W_P is free of rank 2 over T_P, (d) T_P is Gorenstein ("follows from Prop. 2 of Sect. 8 plus standard commutative algebra"). **Prop. 2:** if the residual representation attached to P is irreducible, then (a)-(d) all hold. **Cor. 1 (p. 255):** for a maximal ideal m with irreducible residual representation, (a)-(d) hold for every prime P contained in m (so also for T_m itself, with (d) = T_m Gorenstein); Cor. 2: twists of W at weight-k points. Sect. 10 (p. 255) then gives the same conclusions when the residual representation is "full" (image contains SL_2), and Sect. 12 (p. 258) concludes "T_m is Gorenstein".
2. **Sect. 8, Prop. 2, p. 247 (PDF 18):** the Hecke algebra T = lim T_n is finite and flat over Lambda of rank r, semi-local, complete, noetherian, Cohen-Macaulay of dimension two; M is free of rank 1 over T; M, W are Lambda-free of ranks r and 2r; exact sequence 0 -> W^F -> W -> W/W^F -> 0 of T[G_{Q_p}]-modules with W^F free of rank 1 over T. (Not cited in stage 06; it is the "Prop. 2 of Sect. 8" used in item 1.)
3. The Mazur-Wiles proof uses Hida's results [Hi1] Cor. 3.3 (semisimplicity of the total quotient ring, p. 253) and [Hi2] Lemma 8.1 (V_K free of rank 2; see `hi86a.md`).
