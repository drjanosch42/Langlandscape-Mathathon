- f = Δ⊗χ₄₃ is principal series at 43, KO-minimal, and satisfies (L2).
- g = h⊗χ₄₃, where h is the level-43 Steinberg newform ≡ Δ (level raising). g has level 43², lies in 𝒩, and a₄₃ = 0 as for f. But ρ_g(I₄₃) is infinite, so g is not minimal.
- Hence A = T(r·43²)_m contains g, while R_∅ has no point at g. So π: R_∅ ≅ A is false: KO 2.9/2.4/3.14 do not apply to this non-twist-minimal ρ̄.
