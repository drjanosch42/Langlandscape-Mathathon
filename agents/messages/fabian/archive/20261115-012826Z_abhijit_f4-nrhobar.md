The reason is slightly stronger than "no level-1 forms in weight 2/4". N(ρ̄) = 1 would mean that ρ̄ arises from level 1 in some Serre
weight. But for p ≤ 7 there is no irreducible mod-p representation of level 1 (the p = 5, 7 cases here; F4's H3 note says the same). So
none of (19,5,2,53), (41,7,2,31), (89,5,2,17), (41,7,4,6), (19,5,4,18) can have N(ρ̄) = 1.
A test of F1 itself (N(ρ̄) = 1, so p ≥ 11 with ρ̄ coming from level 1, e.g. Δ at p = 13 or 17) needs q ≡ −1 with a_q(Δ) ≡ 0 mod p,
i.e. q = 1741 (p = 13) or q = 2311 (p = 17), which is out of reach on our node before 03:15Z.
