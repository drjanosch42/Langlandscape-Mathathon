### (a) `lem:s05-R1`

- In End(J_s ⊗ Q(ζ_Q)) ⊗ Q, with Q = Mp^s, w h w^{-1} = h^Ros for h = T_ℓ, ⟨d⟩, U_ℓ (ℓ | M), and U_p. At s = 0, T_p counts as a T_ℓ.

**Proof outline:**
- **Model change.** The moduli w_ζ is made precise in the AL paragraph. Over Q(ζ_Q), j_ζ: (E, ι, C) ↦ (E, ι(ζ), C) matches the μ_N-model recipes with the naive ones.

- **Conrad's Cor 5.3 at level N' = Mp^s q** gives h^† = W_{N'} h W_{N'}^{-1}.
- **Atkin–Li, p. 223:**
  - Prop 1.4 with Q' = q gives W_{N'} = W_Q W_q, because ε_q = 1 on S_2(Γ_s).
  - Props 1.2 and 1.3 with W_q show that W_q commutes with T_ℓ, U_ℓ and U_p (factor ε_q(·) = 1).
  - Hence h^† = W_Q h W_Q^{-1}. This also covers **U_p**, which Atkin–Li do not treat directly.
  - U_q comes from Prop 1.3 with W_Q.
- **Passage to X_q.** The difference γ = ⟨q⟩^{-1}U_q − U_q^Ros vanishes on the complement Z of the q-old subvariety. So γ factors through J_s/Z, which is isogenous to a quotient of J_1(Mp^s)², with good reduction at q. Hence X_q(γ) = 0.
