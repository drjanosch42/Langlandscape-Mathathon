Langlandscape: the six-stage plan

A human-readable summary of plan/staged/ (final state of 15 Nov 2026: all six stages completed).

0. The big picture

The project rebuilds the definite Jacquet–Langlands correspondence over ℚ as an explicit, integral and simplicial construction. Throughout, B = BD is the definite quaternion algebra over ℚ ramified exactly at ∞ and at the primes dividing a squarefree D (with an odd number of prime factors). The basic idea:

  • On the quaternion side, automorphic forms are just functions on a finite set, the class set XB(U) = B×\B̂×/U.
  • Choose an auxiliary prime q ∤ D. The norm-one group 𝒪U[1/q]1 acts on the Bruhat–Tits tree of SL₂(ℚq), and the quotient is a finite graph built out of class sets.
  • The homology of these graphs plays the role of the character group of a modular or Shimura curve with bad reduction at q. On these graphs the classical arguments of Ribet (level raising) and Ihara (Ihara's lemma) become combinatorics.
  • Everything is then taken up the p-power tower and made Λ-adic (Hida theory).

The plan has three layers:

(a) Combinatorial core. Tree quotients, the definite Ribet sequence, the definite Ihara lemma. This needs only strong approximation and graph theory.
(b) One geometric hypothesis, Hyp.X. It identifies the graph objects with GL₂ objects (character groups of Jacobians). It is used only to interpret and to produce maps.
(c) Arithmetic output, independent of Hyp.X. For D = q, a Λ-adic defect formula
(hF) = 𝔠q(F) = 𝔱q(F),    hF = ηF / ηFB,
comparing the congruence ideals on the GL₂ side and the quaternion side with the old/new congruence ideal 𝔠q and the Tamagawa ideal 𝔱q (generated by ρF(Iq) − 1). This extends the fixed-weight results of Kim–Ota (and Pollack–Weston in weight 2) along a whole Hida family.

Classical JL, Chenevier's p-adic JL and the Λ-adic Hecke-algebra JL (Longo–Vigni, Hsieh) are cited, not reproved. Whether the last arrow of the construction (the Deligne–Rapoport graph of the modular curve at q to its Jacobian) can itself be made simplicial is left as an open question.

Stage 1 — Quaternionic foundations

Role. A conventions-and-bookkeeping layer with no deep new mathematics. It fixes every object, normalisation and sign on the definite side once, so that the later stages cannot silently disagree.

Objects

  • A maximal order 𝒪 ⊂ B with splittings 𝒪ℓ ≅ M₂(ℤℓ) for ℓ ∤ D.
  • Levels U₀(N), U₁(N) (lower row ≡ (0, 1)), the p-tower Us = U₁(Mps) with class sets Ss, and U₀(q)U (Iwahori at q).
  • For x ∈ B̂×: the stabiliser Γx = B× ∩ xUx⁻¹ (finite, of order dividing 24) and the weight wx = |Γx/±1|. In lattice terms, x is a right ideal and Γx is the unit group of its left order.

Results

  • Stabiliser lemma proved. For p ≥ 5: Nrd(Γx) = 1; at level U₁(p) every Γx is trivial and Δs = (1+pℤp)/(1+psℤp) acts freely up the tower; p never divides |Γx|.
  • Mass formula. Eichler's Σ 1/|Γx| = (1/24)·∏ℓ|D(ℓ − 1) cited, and its level change by the index [U : U′]. With weights wx the level change holds iff −1 ∈ U′ or −1 ∉ U; otherwise an extra factor 2 appears (the “factor-2 hypothesis” used throughout). Consequence:
    |Ss| = (φ(D)/24)·ψ(Mps)·φ(Mps)
    (e.g. D = 3, p = 5: 2, 50, 1250).
  • Brandt modules M(U; V) with Vk = Symk−2, the homological dual ℤ[X], the pairing ⟨[x],[y]⟩ = wxδxy and the norm map ν : [x] ↦ wx𝟙x. Subtlety: ν is Hecke-equivariant only up to adjoints (T ↦ T*), and it turns pushforward into trace.
  • Hecke operators Tℓ, Sℓ, Uℓ, ⟨d⟩, Πℓ (ℓ | D), Up. Main decision: Up = Σj (p j; 0 1), the normalisation that commutes with pushforward up the p-tower proved. The seed's literal formula [U·diag(1, p)·U] is the other operator, the one that commutes with trace.

Convention flags passed on

  • The adelic diamond ⟨d⟩ is the classical ⟨d⟩⁻¹.
  • Under JL, Uℓ ↦ +Πℓ for ℓ | D. Checked on D = 11 against the elliptic curve 11a; this contradicts the sign printed in Hsieh.

Cited targets

Classical JL (Brandt form), Chenevier's p-adic JL, and the Λ-adic Hecke JL 𝕋ord, D-new ≅ 𝕋ordB.

Stage 2 — Tree quotients

Role. Turn the finite class sets into a graph. Fix q ∤ Dp. The group ΓU = 𝒪U[1/q]1 (norm-one elements, integral away from q) acts on the Bruhat–Tits tree 𝒯q, type-preserving and without inversions.

Results

  • Quotient graph proved given connectivity. ΓU\𝒯q is the bipartite Brandt graph BG(U, q): vertices are two copies of XB(U), edges are XB(U₀(q)U), endpoint maps s₀[y] = [y] and s₁[y] = [y·diag(1, q)], and stabilisers are the Γx. Hecke operators away from q commute with s₀ and s₁.
  • Deep inputs cited: strong approximation (SA) for B¹ at {∞, q}, and the Hasse–Schilling norm theorem (used silently in the seed).
  • Connectivity criterion proved, new. For one level U, SA at that level is equivalent to BG(U, q) being connected, which is a finite check that HACS or Lean can do directly.
  • Two-term complex proved. Since p ∤ |Γx|, the cohomology H*(ΓU, V) is computed by 0 → M(U; V)² → M(U₀(q)U; V) → 0. Harmonic cochains, the kernel of (s₀*, s₁*), equal H₁ and are taken as the definition of the q-new part.
  • Free case. At level U₁(ps), ΓU is free of rank (q − 1)·|XB(U)| + 1, and everything is plain graph homology.
  • Indefinite stand-in. 𝒳Dq(U) := H₁(BG, ℤ) plays the role of the character group of the Shimura curve of discriminant Dq.

Caveats

  • Over ℤ, H₁(ΓU, ℤ) has 2- and 3-torsion from the vertex groups (e.g. D = 2, q = 3 gives ℤ/3, although the graph has H₁ = 0). Hence the graph's H₁ is used, not group homology.
  • H₀(ΓU, V) needs genuine density, which no single-level check gives.
  • SA is not available in Lean (the seed was wrong about this); it is a registered hypothesis.

Stage 3 — Ribet and Ihara on the definite side

Role. Prove the definite analogues of Ribet's level-raising exact sequence and Ihara's lemma as statements about weighted graphs.

Results

  • Weighted graphs. For a graph with edge weights we: the lattice X(𝒢), a weighted pairing, and the combinatorial component group Φcomb. Two-vertex formula |Φ| = ∏we · Σ 1/we proved.
  • Mazur check proved modulo cited: Φcomb of the Deligne–Rapoport graph at q equals ℤ/num((q − 1)/12), recovering the component group of J₀(q).
  • Φcomb is Eisenstein proved, all q: Tℓ − (1 + ℓ) kills it (an elementary argument found in the audit).
  • Definite Ribet sequence proved modulo cited, obtained by collapsing vertices:
    0 → H₁ → ℤ[XB(U′)]⁰ → (ℤ[XB(U)]⁰)² → 0.
  • Definite Ihara lemma. Parts (i) and (ii) proved. Part (iii), that Tℓ acts on the cokernel as (1 + ℓ), i.e. the cokernel is Eisenstein, to prove.
  • Level-raising operator. det(αα†) = (q + 1)² − Tq² to prove — the familiar level-raising congruence condition.
  • GL₂ side. Amalgam and Mayer–Vietoris, with det Ψ*Ψ = (q + 1)² − q2−kTq². The GL₂ Ihara lemma in Betti form is only expected.

Stage 4 — The Λ-adic passage

Role. Run stages 2 and 3 up the p-tower Ss and apply Hida's ordinary projector e = lim Upn! to get statements over Λ = 𝒪E[[1 + pℤp]].

Results

  • e kills the junk proved: the degree term, the vertex term, Φcomb ⊗ ℤp and H₀ all vanish after e. The ordinary pairing between e and its adjoint e* is perfect (with e ≠ e* in general).
  • Freeness and control proved modulo cited: the ordinary Brandt module 𝓜Bord is Λ-free and specialises correctly at each weight (elementary Hida-type argument; also in Hsieh and Longo–Vigni).
  • Weight points. Because of the diamond inversion from stage 1, the seed's weight points must be inverted: Pkad = ker([u] ↦ u2−k).
  • Λ-adic definite Ribet sequence, exactness proved:
    0 → 𝒳ordDq → 𝓜ordB(U₀(q)U) → 𝓜ordB(U)² → 0.
    It needs connectivity at every level s, which no finite check provides (so SA is genuinely used).
  • From Hsieh cited, under a residual condition: the localised Hecke algebra is Gorenstein, the Brandt module is free of rank one over it, and the quaternionic congruence ideal ηFB is a self-pairing.

Stage 5 — The comparison hypothesis Hyp.X

Role. Isolate, in one precise hypothesis, everything that compares the graph objects with the geometry of modular curves, and derive its consequences.

The hypothesis Hyp.X(M; q)

For every s, an isomorphism between the character group at q of the Jacobian Js and the degree-zero divisors on the class set, Xq(Js) ≅ ℤ[Ss]⁰, such that:

  • (X1) it is Hecke-equivariant, with Uq ↦ +Πq;
  • (X2) the monodromy pairing goes to the weighted pairing weδ;
  • (X3) it is natural in s;
  • (X4) it respects the Galois filtration, with Frobq = q·Uq.

At Γ₀-level the ingredients are cited (Deligne–Rapoport, Ribet 1990, SGA7). At Γ₁(Mps)-level with Up there is essentially no reference: expected. In Lean it is a named Prop, never an axiom.

Results

  • Sign ε = +1 fixed proved modulo cited, against Hsieh's printed −.
  • Λ-adic version of Hyp.X follows formally; the Ohta twist is only sketched.
  • A non-canonical isomorphism version holds at good maximal ideals proved modulo cited.
  • Sub/quotient comparison proved: the q-new sub- and quotient lattices differ by 𝕋/(𝔞 + 𝔟).
  • Frobenius conventions fixed once (Ribet–Stein, Picard functoriality): Frobq = q·Uq.

Stage 6 — The integral defect

Role. Prove the arithmetic output (c): for D = q, the ratio of congruence ideals equals the old/new congruence ideal equals the Tamagawa ideal, along the q-new Hida branch. This does not use Hyp.X.

Status: completed (merged on main at aed93c37; final gate at Lean tree 4405815: PASS, PrintAxioms 836/836 standard axioms, from scratch on TITAN and on CONCEPTD).

Results

  • Commutative algebra proved: Lemmas S, G, D and Theorem E (i)–(iv), ηF = 𝔠q · ηFB under Gorenstein hypotheses. Without the second Gorenstein hypothesis it holds at height-one primes, and 𝔠q is principal iff that ring is Gorenstein. In Lean the layer CommAlg/ is formalized (standard axioms only, 0 sorry), including the DVR half of Theorem E(iii).
  • Galois half: 𝔱q ⊆ 𝔠q, via Chebotarev and Burnside. The output of the Galois steps 1–3 is the registered hypothesis Hyp.FreeRankTwo, which is equivalent to the conclusion 𝔱q ≤ 𝔠q given ResidualBurnside (Defect.freeRankTwo_iff_le). ResidualBurnside is conditionally discharged from absolute irreducibility.
  • Squeeze at a Kim–Ota point via level lowering mod ϖt gives the other inclusion, hence the defect theorem, proved modulo cited (Hida control, Hsieh, Kim–Ota) and registered hypotheses. In Lean, Defect.defect_formula, cor_fixed and cor_allweights are formalized modulo the registered hypotheses, including the Galois half (Hyp.FreeRankTwo); this is not an unconditional theorem. The all-weights corollary needs c ≠ 0 at both points (not assumed).
  • The case q ≡ −1. M = 1: proved modulo cited. M > 1: F1 and F1′ (F1′ under our reading R4) proved modulo cited, i.e. for squarefree M with ρ̄ unramified at all ℓ | M. N(ρ̄) > 1 is open: the counterexample B2 refutes the step-(4) proof (and only that step).
  • Corrections to the seed: the old quotient is 𝕋(M)𝔪 via Uq ↦ αq (the seed had twice the rank); one hypothesis needs “no p-new forms”; 𝒪P is not claimed to be a DVR.
  • Betti incarnation. coker(Ψ*Ψ)𝔪 ≅ (𝕋(M)𝔪/δ)² computes 𝔠q in rank one (HACS computation: see below).
  • Computed checks: T4 Hecke side lengths 1, 1, 1, 1, 2 at k = 12, 28, 44, 60, 124, as predicted; definite Gram side positive control (13, 8, 37) with vp = 1; the C4(i) regression is partial (131/319 cases run).
  • Several primes (D with ≥ 3 prime factors): (hF) = ∏ 𝔱qi conjectural.
